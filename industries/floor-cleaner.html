How it works

Complete Floor Cleaner Process Flow

Raw Material Intake & Storage

Water, surfactants, builders, fragrance and colour concentrates are received and held in dedicated vessels before batching.

Storage TankBag Dump Station

➜ Ensures a controlled and traceable start to every batch.

Weighing, Dosing & Batching

Each ingredient is weighed and dosed into the batching vessel as per the approved formulation.

Weighing & DosingJacketed Tank

➜ Ensures:

  • Repeatable batch composition
  • Controlled addition sequence
  • Reduced manual handling
Mixing & Blending

The batch is agitated until the surfactant, fragrance and colour phases are fully blended into a stable, clear liquid.

AgitatorHigh Shear MixerHomogenizer

➜ Delivers:

  • Uniform, homogeneous solution
  • Consistent appearance and viscosity
  • Stable finished product
Bottle Rinsing & Line Feeding

Empty bottles are rinsed and fed onto the filling line in a controlled sequence.

RinsingConveyor Belt

➜ Prepares clean, correctly oriented containers for filling.

Filling, Capping & Labelling (HIGH-SPEED FMCG)

The blended cleaner is filled into containers, capped and labelled on a continuous line.

Servo FillingCappingLabellingBottle Packing (Spray, Pet, Glass)

➜ Suitable for:

  • Spray bottles
  • PET and glass bottles
  • Jerry cans and bulk packs
Coding, Weighing & Inspection

Filled packs are coded with batch details and checked before secondary packing.

Batch CodingCheckweigher With PrinterVision Inspection

➜ Confirms fill quantity, closure and label placement.

Secondary Packing & Dispatch

Bottles are collated into cartons, wrapped and prepared for warehousing and dispatch.

Master Carton PackingStretch WrappingPalletizer

➜ Ready for retail distribution and export.
