How it works

Working Principle of Material Handling Robot Machine

Product Detection & Input

Products or materials are identified using: ✔ Sensors ✔ Vision systems ✔ Conveyor tracking systems ✔ Barcode scanners

Robotic Automation Activation

PLC or robotic controller sends movement instructions to robotic arm system

➜ Servo motors control precise robotic movement for product handling tasks.

Material Handling Operation

Robot handles: ✔ Cartons ✔ Bags ✔ Pallets ✔ Containers ✔ Bottles ✔ Industrial components ✔ Finished products using: Vacuum grippers Mechanical grippers Clamp systems Magnetic grippers

Automated Product Transfer

Robot automatically performs: Loading Unloading Stacking Sorting Packaging Palletizing with high accuracy and speed.

Conveyor & Automation Integration

Robotic system integrates with: Conveyor systems Packaging machines AGV/AMR systems Warehouse automation systems Production lines

Finished Product Movement

Products automatically transferred to: Storage zones Packaging lines Dispatch sections Warehouse systems
