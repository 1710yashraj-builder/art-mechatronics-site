Condiment Processing Plant & Machinery Solutions

Turnkey plant · Machinery · Automation · India · UAE · Thailand

Condiments cover peanut butter, honey, syrups, ketchup and sauces, spreads, dips and dressings, and pickles and chutneys. Ingredients are cleaned and prepared, cooked or blended into a paste or liquid, milled and homogenised for texture, then filled into jars, bottles or pouches and capped.

How it works

Complete Condiment Process Flow

Raw material cleaning and preparation

Nuts, fruit, vegetables or spices are cleaned of stones and soil, washed, and cut or chopped down to a workable size. Peanut lines de-stone and clean before roasting; pickle lines cut and salt.

Pre-CleanerDe-StonerWasherPeelerChopper
Roasting and cooking

Peanuts are roasted and cooled; sauce, ketchup and chutney bases are cooked in a jacketed kettle with the sugar, salt and acid of the recipe. Honey and syrup lines warm the product rather than cook it.

RoasterCooking Kettle, Boiling TankJacketed TankSteam Generator
Grinding and refining

The cooked or roasted material is reduced to a paste and then refined so the finished texture is smooth or deliberately coarse. Peanut butter, spreads and dips all pass this stage.

Colloid MillWet GrinderGrinderHomogenizer
Blending, dosing and heat treatment

Stabiliser, oil, salt and flavour are dosed in and the batch is blended, then heat treated and held in tank ahead of filling.

Weighing & DosingRotary MixerPasteurizerStorage Tank
Filling and closing

The condiment is filled by volume into jars, bottles or pouches and closed. Thick pastes are piston filled; thin sauces and syrups run on nozzle filling.

Piston FillingServo Nozzle Tracking FillingBottle Packing (Spray, Pet, Glass)Jar PackingCapping
Labelling, coding and case packing

Closed containers are labelled, date coded and packed into cartons or cases for despatch.

Wraparound Labelling for Cans & JarsBatch CodingLoad CartonerMaster Case Packing
