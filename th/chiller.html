Chiller Machine for Thailand

A Chiller cools process water or glycol, which then removes heat from tanks, moulds, kettles and cooling tunnels; ART supplies it with a Cooling Tower. Thai beverage, dairy, seafood, curry paste, chocolate, plastic moulding and rubber lines use chilled water because mains and well water are warm all year. Thai plants are supported by ART Mechatronics from its Pattaya office.

Supplied to plants in Thailand from ART's Thailand Marketing Office in Pattaya City, with a service team in Chonburi; designed and built in Kanpur, India.

For Thai plants

Why Thai processors choose the Chiller Machine

  • Thailand's ambient heat means cooked curry paste, sauces and syrups need chilled water in Jacketed Tank and Cooling Tunnel stages.
  • Seafood, dairy and beverage plants use chilled water for washing, holding and the cooling side of ART's Pasteurizer and Heat Exchanger.
  • Humid air limits how much a Cooling Tower alone can cool water, so the Chiller takes the final step in Thai conditions.
  • Service from Chonburi, within reach of the food, beverage and plastics plants around Bangkok and the Eastern Seaboard.
