Bakery production covers bread and buns, brown and multigrain loaves, rusk and toast, and biscuits and cookies. Flour, water and other ingredients are dosed and mixed into dough, divided and shaped, proved or laminated, baked, cooled and packed.

How it works

Complete Bakery Process Flow

Ingredient handling and dosing

Flour is taken from silo or sack, sifted and weighed, and the minor ingredients are dosed to the recipe. Water, fat, sugar and yeast are metered in on the same batch call.

SiloBag Dump StationSifterWeighing & DosingScrew Conveyor
Dough mixing

The batch is mixed until the dough is developed. Bread doughs are taken to full development; biscuit and cookie doughs are mixed short. Chilled water is used to hold the dough temperature down through the mix.

Spiral MixerPlanetary MixerHigh Shear MixerChiller
Dividing, shaping and proving

Bread dough is divided, rounded, rested, moulded and panned, then proved in humidified air. Biscuit and cookie dough is sheeted, cut or wire-cut into pieces instead. This is where the bread and the biscuit routes separate.

FormingBatch CutterRoller CutterAir Handling Unit
Baking

The pieces are baked in a rack or tunnel oven. Rusk and toast lines return the baked loaf to the oven a second time after slicing.

Rotary Rack OvenTray Dryer, Tray OvenHot Air GeneratorThermic Fluid Heater
Depanning and cooling

Baked product is turned out of the pans and cooled on a spiral or belt cooling conveyor until it is firm enough to slice and wrap.

Cooling ConveyorSpiral ConveyorCooling TunnelBlower Fan
Slicing, stacking and primary packing

Loaves are sliced and bagged; biscuits are stacked into slugs and flow wrapped. Packs pass a metal check before they leave the line.

SlicerCount & StackFlow WrapMetal Detection
Case packing and palletising

Wrapped packs are collated into cartons or trays, cased, stacked and wrapped for despatch.

Collating & CartoningRobotic Case PackerStretch WrappingPalletizer
