How it works

Working Principle of Can Packing Machine

  • 1. Empty Can Feeding Empty cans are automatically fed into packaging line through synchronized conveyor systems.
  • 2. Product Weighing & Filling Process Machine automatically weighs and fills products into cans using precision dosing systems.

➜ Servo synchronization ensures accurate filling and smooth product handling.

  • 3. Can Filling Operation Machine handles: ✔ Food products ✔ Beverage products ✔ Powders ✔ Spices ✔ Tobacco products ✔ Oils ✔ Confectionery products

accurately and continuously.

  • 4. Lid Placement & Can Sealing Process Machine performs: Lid placing Can seaming Airtight sealing Vacuum sealing (optional) Nitrogen flushing (optional)

for hygienic and moisture-protected packaging quality.

  • 5. Coding & Inspection Integration Optional systems include: Batch coding Date printing QR code printing Vision inspection Leak testing systems
  • 6. Finished Can Discharge Finished cans discharged automatically for: Cartoning Case packing Retail distribution Export shipment handling
