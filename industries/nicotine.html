Nicotine Extraction Plant & Machinery Solutions

Turnkey plant · Machinery · Automation · India · UAE · Thailand

Nicotine is an alkaloid recovered from tobacco leaf, stem and dust, which are dried, milled and treated with alkali before the nicotine is taken into a liquid phase. The extract is separated from spent solids, clarified and concentrated, then filled as a liquid or dried to a powder.

How it works

Complete Nicotine Process Flow

Raw Material Preparation & Milling

Tobacco leaf, stem and dust are received, dried where needed and reduced to a coarse powder so more surface is presented for extraction. Milling runs under dust collection, as tobacco dust is fine and readily airborne.

Bag Dump StationHammer Mill MachineScreenerScrew ConveyorDust Collector Machine
Alkali Conditioning

The milled material is wetted and treated with alkali, the usual way of releasing nicotine from its bound salt form. Conditioning is carried out in jacketed vessels with heating and continuous agitation.

Ribbon MixerJacketed TankAgitatorSteam GeneratorWeighing & Dosing
Extraction

The conditioned mass is contacted with the extraction liquid and agitated under heat so the nicotine passes into solution. Heat is drawn from the plant's steam and heat exchange circuit.

Jacketed TankStirrerHeat ExchangerBoiler
Separation & Clarification

Spent tobacco solids are separated from the nicotine-bearing liquor, and the liquor is clarified before it moves on. Clarified liquor is held in tanks ahead of concentration, and the spent solids are conveyed away.

SeparatorCyclone SeparatorStorage TankScrew Conveyor
Concentration & Drying

The clarified liquor is concentrated, and where a solid grade is wanted the concentrate is dried to a powder. Vacuum drying is used where the product should not be exposed to open heat.

Heat ExchangerVacuumRotocone Vacuum Dryer - RCVDSpray Dryer
Filling & Packing

Liquid nicotine is filled into bottles or drums, capped, coded and labelled; powder grades are filled and sealed as dry product. Filled containers are checked for leaks before despatch.

Piston FillingBottle Packing (Spray, Pet, Glass)CappingAuger FillerLeak TesterLabelling
Fume & Effluent Handling

Vapour, fume and process air from the extraction and drying sections are scrubbed before release. Spent solids and process residues are handled as separate controlled waste streams.

Wet ScrubberFume Extraction SystemAir Pollution Control EquipmentIncinerator
