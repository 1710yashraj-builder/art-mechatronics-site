Fish Processing Plant & Machinery Solutions

Turnkey plant · Machinery · Automation · India · UAE · Thailand

Fish processing turns whole landed fish into gutted fish, fillets, steaks, dried fish and frozen packs. Fish are washed and graded, descaled, gutted and headed, filleted and skinned, trimmed to portion size, then chilled, frozen or dried, and packed.

How it works

Complete Fish Process Flow

Washing, Grading & Icing

Landed fish are washed of slime and sand and graded by species and size before the cutting line. Grading at intake decides which fish go to fillet, which to whole frozen and which to drying.

WasherGraderVibratory FeederRoller Conveyor
Descaling, Gutting & Heading

Scales are taken off, the belly is opened, and gut and head are removed and carried away. Offal is conveyed to a separate collection point so the edible line stays clean.

Blade CutterSplitterRinsingScrew Conveyor
Filleting, Skinning & Portioning

Fillets are taken off the frame, skinned, trimmed and cut into steaks, loins or cubes. Portion cutting gives packs of even thickness that cook through at the same rate.

Knife CutterSkin RemovalSlicerDicer
Chilling & Freezing

Fresh fillets are held chilled, while the rest is frozen as separate pieces, blocks or whole fish. Frozen stock is moved into cold storage until it is packed against an order.

ChillerBlast FreezerIQF FreezerCold Storage/ Cold Room
Salting & Drying

Fish for the dried trade is salted and dried in trays or on a tunnel dryer under controlled air. Moisture is checked during drying because it governs how the dried fish keeps in storage.

Jacketed TankTray Dryer, Tray OvenContinuous Conveyor, Tunnel DryerDehumidifierMoisture Analyzer
Packing & Dispatch

Fillets and portions are vacuum or thermoform packed, inspected and cased. Cases are palletised and moved out through chilled or frozen dispatch.

VacuumThermoforming SealingMetal DetectionMaster Carton PackingPalletizer
