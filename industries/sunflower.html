Sunflower Processing Plant & Machinery Solutions

Turnkey plant · Machinery · Automation · India · UAE · Thailand

Sunflower processing separates the seed from its hull and prepares kernels for oil pressing or for confectionery packing. Seed is cleaned, dried, dehulled and the light hulls aspirated away, after which kernels are graded, flaked and pressed.

How it works

Complete Sunflower Process Flow

Cleaning & Pre-Grading

Seed arriving from the field is screened free of stalk, husk fragments, dust and field soil, and separated by size before dehulling. Even sizing at this point makes the dehuller easier to set.

Pre-CleanerAir Screen CleanerDe-StonerMagnetic SeparatorGrader
Drying & Conditioning

Seed is dried down and held until the moisture is even through the lot, which is what makes the hull brittle enough to crack cleanly. Moisture is checked on samples before the seed is released to the dehuller.

Rotary Dryer, Drum DryerHot Air GeneratorMoisture AnalyzerZ Type Elevator
Dehulling & Hull Separation

Seed is impacted to split the hull from the kernel, and the two are then parted by air because the hull is much lighter. Unhulled seed returned by the separator is recirculated for a second pass.

Shell RemovalAir AspiratorAir ClassifierWinnowerVibratory Conveyor
Kernel Grading & Sorting

Kernels are graded by size and density, and discoloured kernels, remaining hull and foreign matter are picked out optically. This stage decides which kernels go to the food line and which go for crushing.

Gravity SeparatorColor SorterVision InspectionSifter
Flaking & Oil Expelling

Kernels for oil are flaked between rolls and then pressed in a screw expeller to draw off crude sunflower oil. The cake leaving the press is cooled before it goes to storage or milling.

Roller Mill MachineExpeller MachineCooling ConveyorScrew Conveyor
Kernel Weighing & Packing

Food-grade kernels are weighed and filled into pouches, with gas flushing used where a longer shelf life is wanted. Filled packs pass a metal check and a weight check before they are cased.

Multi Head WeigherVertical Form Fill Seal (Vffs)Nitrogen PackingMetal DetectorCheckweigher With PrinterMaster Case Packing
