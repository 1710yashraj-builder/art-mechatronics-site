Pin Mill Machine (Industrial Fine Grinding & Ultra-Fine Pulverizing System)

A Pin Mill Machine, also known as an Industrial Pin Mill or Pin Mill Grinder, is a high-speed grinding system designed for fine and ultra-fine pulverization of materials using intermeshing pins mounted on rotating discs.

Overview

About the Pin Mill Machine

Pin mills are ideal for applications requiring precise particle size, minimal heat generation, and high-quality powder output, making them widely used in food processing, spices, pharmaceuticals, chemicals, sugar, starch, and cosmetic industries.

The unique design allows for efficient size reduction with controlled fineness, especially for heat-sensitive and friable materials.

How it works

Working Principle of Pin Mill

Material Feeding

Material is fed into the grinding chamber via:

HopperControlled feed system
High-Speed Pin Impact

Inside the mill:

Two discs fitted with pins rotate (one or both may rotate)Material passes through intermeshing pinsHigh-speed impact breaks particles
Fine Grinding
Particle size is reduced to fine powderUniform distribution is achieved
Airflow & Cooling
Airflow helps in coolingPrevents overheating of material
Product Discharge

Final product is discharged through:

Outlet systemCyclone / dust collector (optional integration)
