How it works

Working Principle of Load Cartoner Machine

  • 1. Product Feeding Products are fed continuously through synchronized conveyor systems.
  • 2. Product Loading Process Machine automatically arranges, synchronizes, and loads products into cartons.

➜ Servo synchronization ensures accurate product positioning and smooth loading operations.

  • 3. Carton Feeding & Erection Flat cartons are automatically picked, erected, and positioned for filling operations.
  • 4. Product Filling & Carton Loading Operation Machine handles: ✔ Sachets ✔ Pouches ✔ Tobacco packs ✔ Cigarette packs ✔ FMCG products ✔ Pharmaceutical products ✔ Cosmetic products

accurately and continuously.

  • 5. Carton Sealing & Coding Process Machine performs: Flap folding Glue sealing Tuck-in sealing Batch coding Barcode printing

for premium retail packaging quality.

  • 6. Finished Carton Discharge Finished cartons discharged automatically for: Case packing Palletizing Dispatch operations Retail distribution
