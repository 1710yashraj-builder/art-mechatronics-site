Walnut Processing Plant & Machinery Solutions (Raw Walnut to Kernel Processing)

Turnkey plant · Machinery · Automation · India · UAE · Thailand

Walnut processing is a high-value segment in the global dry fruit industry where raw walnuts (in-shell) are processed into clean, ready-to-eat walnut kernels. The process requires controlled cracking, careful handling, and precise grading to ensure maximum whole kernel recovery and premium product quality.

How it works

Complete Walnut Process Flow

Raw Walnut Handling

Raw walnuts (with shell) are received and fed into the processing system.

Material Handling SystemsFeeding Systems

Cleaning & Destoning

Walnuts are cleaned to remove dust, stones, and foreign particles.

Pre CleanerDestonerAir Classifier

➜ Ensures:

  • Product purity
  • Protection of machinery
Conditioning / Moisture Adjustment

Walnuts are conditioned to achieve optimal moisture for cracking.

Conditioning SystemHumidification Setup

➜ Ensures:

  • Reduced kernel breakage
  • Better shell separation
Cracking / Shelling

Walnuts are cracked to separate the shell from the kernel.

Walnut Cracking MachineShelling Machine

➜ Ensures:

  • Controlled cracking force
  • Maximum whole kernel recovery
  • High efficiency
Kernel & Shell Separation

Shell fragments are separated from kernels.

Air SeparatorGravity Separator

➜ Ensures clean kernel output.

Drying (IF REQUIRED)

Kernels may be dried to achieve desired moisture level.

Tray DryerHot Air Dryer

  • Extended shelf life
  • Prevention of spoilage
