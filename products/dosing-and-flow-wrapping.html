How it works

Working Principle of Dosing & Flow Wrapping Machine

Product Feeding & Dosing

Products are fed into dosing system through: ✔ Multihead weighers ✔ Cup fillers ✔ Auger fillers ✔ Volumetric dosing systems

Accurate Product Filling

Precision dosing system dispenses accurate product quantity automatically

➜ Servo-controlled synchronization ensures uniform product filling and wrapping operations.

Horizontal Flow Wrapping Process

Machine handles: ✔ Snacks ✔ Biscuits ✔ Candies ✔ Dry fruits ✔ Bakery products ✔ Frozen food products smoothly and continuously.

Sealing & Pillow Pack Formation

Rotary sealing systems perform: Center sealing End sealing Pillow pack formation Continuous wrapping for hygienic and attractive packaging.

PLC & Servo Automation Control

System controls: Product synchronization Filling accuracy Film tracking Packaging speed Sealing timing

Finished Pack Discharge

Wrapped products discharged automatically for: Collection Cartoning Secondary packaging Dispatch operations
