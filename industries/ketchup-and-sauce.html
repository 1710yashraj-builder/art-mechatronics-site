Raw Material Handling & Washing

Material Handling SystemsWashing System

Sorting & Inspection

Raw materials are sorted to remove defective produce.

Crushing & Pulping

Tomatoes or chillies are crushed and converted into pulp.

CrusherPulping Machine

Cooking & Concentration

Jacketed Cooking KettleSteam Heating System

Filter System / Refiner
