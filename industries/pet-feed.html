Pet Feed Processing Plant & Machinery Solutions

Turnkey plant · Machinery · Automation · India · UAE · Thailand

Pet feed manufacturing turns cereals, meat and poultry meals, fats and vitamin premixes into dry kibble for dogs and cats. The ground mash is cooked and shaped in an extruder, dried, coated with fat and palatant, cooled and then filled into retail bags or pouches.

How it works

Complete Pet Feed Process Flow

Raw Material Intake & Grinding

Cereals, meat meal and poultry meal are ground to a fine, even meal. Fine grinding matters here because kibble shape and surface finish depend on a uniform dough.

HopperHammer Mill MachineMicro Pulverizer MachineSifter
Batching & Mixing

The ground meal is weighed to recipe with vitamin and mineral premixes and blended. Recipes are held in the control system, so a brand's variants can be run in sequence without manual reset.

Weighing & DosingRibbon MixerPaddle MixerCentralised Automation System
Extrusion Cooking & Shaping

The mash is cooked with steam under pressure in the extruder, which gelatinises the starch and expands the dough as it leaves the die. A rotating cutter at the die face shapes the kibble into the biscuit form the brand sells.

Extruder MachineSteam GeneratorKnife CutterGranulator
Drying & Cooling

Kibble leaves the extruder wet and is dried on a moving bed of hot air until it is shelf-stable. It is then cooled before coating, so the fat does not simply run off.

Continuous Conveyor, Tunnel DryerHot Air GeneratorCooling TunnelHeat Exchanger
Fat & Palatant Coating

Fat and palatant are sprayed onto the dried kibble in a tumbling drum so every piece is coated. This outer coat carries most of the aroma and is what decides whether the animal accepts the food.

Coating Pan MixerDrum MixerJacketed Tank
Retail Filling & Inspection

Coated kibble is weighed out and filled into consumer bags, pouches or stand-up packs. Every pack is checked for metal contamination and for correct fill weight before it leaves the line.

Multi Head WeigherPremade PouchVertical Form Fill Seal (Vffs)Metal DetectionCheckweigher With Printer
Coding & Secondary Packing

Packs are coded with batch and date details, then collated into shippers. Cases are stacked and wrapped for retail distribution.

Master Carton PackingBatch CodingRobotic Case PackerStretch Wrapping
