How it works

Working Principle of Pouch Stacking Machine

Pouch Receiving from Packaging Machine

Finished pouches discharge continuously from: ✔ VFFS machines ✔ Multi-lane packaging machines ✔ Sachet packing machines ✔ Rotary pouch packaging systems

Pouch Alignment & Synchronization (CORE PROCESS)

Conveyor and sensor systems align pouches automatically

➜ Machine synchronizes incoming pouch flow for smooth stacking and batching operations.

Automatic Counting & Batching

Machine handles: ✔ Pouches ✔ Sachets ✔ Packets ✔ Pillow packs ✔ Stand-up pouches ✔ FMCG packs accurately and continuously.

Pouch Stacking Process

Machine performs: Pouch collection Packet stacking Batch formation Grouping operations for efficient secondary packaging and cartoning operations.

PLC & Automation Control

System controls: Counting accuracy Conveyor synchronization Stack quantity Pouch flow management Production speed

Finished Stack Discharge

Stacked pouches discharged automatically for: Carton packing Mono carton filling Shrink wrapping Dispatch operations
