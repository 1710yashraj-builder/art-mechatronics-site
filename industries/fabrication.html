Steel Fabrication Plant & Machinery Solutions

Turnkey plant · Machinery · Automation · India · UAE · Thailand

Fabrication in the iron and steel industry covers cutting, forming, welding and finishing steel plates and sections into finished structures and assemblies. Fabrication workshops generate cutting fume, welding fume and grinding dust, and move heavy material between bays all day.

How it works

Complete Fabrication Process Flow

Material Receipt & Handling

Plates, sections, pipes and bar stock are received, stacked and moved to the cutting bay. Handling equipment carries the load between bays so material is not dragged across the shop floor.

Roller ConveyorGoods LiftScissor LiftTrolleyMaterial Handling Robot
Cutting & Profiling

Plate and section are cut to size and profiled by gas, plasma or laser cutting. Cutting releases metal fume and fine particulate, which is drawn off at the cutting table.

Fume Extraction SystemCartridge Dust Collector MachineWet ScrubberBlower Fan
Grinding & Edge Preparation

Cut edges are ground, bevelled and deburred before the parts are fitted up. Grinding throws off metallic dust and sparks, so the bay is kept under extraction.

Grinding MachineDust Extraction SystemPulse Jet Dust Collector MachineCyclone Separator
Welding & Assembly

Parts are fitted, tacked and welded into finished assemblies. Welding fume rises off the arc and is taken away through ducting that serves the whole shop.

Centralised Dust Collection SystemBag House Dust Collector MachineAir Handling UnitAir Filter
Surface Preparation & Painting

Fabricated assemblies are cleaned, primed and painted before they leave the shop. Booth air carries paint mist and solvent vapour, which is treated before discharge.

Dry ScrubberHot Air GeneratorAir Pollution Control EquipmentContinuous Conveyor, Tunnel Dryer
Storage & Dispatch

Finished assemblies are staged, protected and loaded for dispatch to site.

Loading & Unloading ConveyorTelescopic ConveyorSlat ConveyorStretch Wrapping
