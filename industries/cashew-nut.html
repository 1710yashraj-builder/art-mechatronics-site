Cashew Nut Processing Plant & Machinery Solutions (Raw to Kernel Processing)

Turnkey plant · Machinery · Automation · India · UAE · Thailand

Cashew processing is a high-value agro-processing industry where raw cashew nuts (in shell) are converted into edible cashew kernels. The process requires careful handling, controlled steaming, precision shelling, peeling, and grading to ensure maximum whole kernel recovery and premium product quality.

How it works

Complete Cashew Process Flow

Raw Cashew Handling

Raw cashew nuts (with shell) are received and fed into the processing system.

Material Handling SystemsFeeding Systems

➜ Ensures smooth and controlled flow.

Cleaning & Destoning

Raw cashews are cleaned to remove dust, stones, and foreign materials.

Pre CleanerDestoner

➜ Ensures product purity and protects machinery.

Steaming / Cooking

Raw cashews are steamed to soften the shell for easy removal.

Steam Cooker / Boiler System

➜ Ensures:

  • Easy shell separation
  • Reduced kernel breakage
Shelling

Shell is removed to extract cashew kernels.

Cashew Shelling Machine

➜ Ensures:

  • High shelling efficiency
  • Minimal kernel damage
  • High yield
Drying

Shelled kernels are dried to reduce moisture and prepare for peeling.

Tray DryerHot Air Drying System

➜ Improves peeling efficiency.

Peeling (SKIN REMOVAL)

Outer testa (skin) is removed from cashew kernels.

Peeling Machine

➜ Ensures clean, white kernels.

Grading & Sorting

Cashew kernels are graded based on size, color, and quality.

Grading MachineVibratory GraderColor Sorter (Optional)

➜ Produces:

  • Whole kernels
  • Broken grades

➜ Critical for export quality.

Quality Inspection

Final inspection ensures product consistency and premium quality.

Inspection Conveyor
Packaging (BULK WHOLESALE OUTPUT)

Processed cashew kernels are packed into bulk bags for supply to wholesalers.

Weighing SystemBag Filling MachineVacuum / Nitrogen Flushing System (Optional)

➜ Suitable for:

  • Bulk export
  • Wholesale distribution
