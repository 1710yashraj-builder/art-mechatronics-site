Complete Beverage Process Flow

Raw material intake and cleaning

Green coffee, cocoa bean, tea leaf and dry ingredients are taken in and cleaned of stones, dust and tramp metal. Liquid lines take in water and concentrate instead, and treat it before use.

Product Pathways

Beverage plants are laid out by product route rather than as a single line. The five routes below are the common beverage products, each with its main stages and the machines used at each stage.

Route A — Hot beverages: tea, coffee and cocoa. Dry-side route producing blended tea, roasted and ground coffee and cocoa powder for hot drinks.

Cleaning and sorting of leaf and bean

Green coffee bean, cocoa bean and made tea are cleaned of stones, dust and tramp metal. Tea is graded by leaf size and colour-sorted so that stalk and off-colour leaf are taken out before blending.

Pre-CleanerDe-StonerMagnetic SeparatorColor SorterGrader
Roasting, winnowing and grinding

Coffee bean is roasted, cooled and ground to coffee powder. Cocoa bean is roasted, cooled and winnowed free of shell; the nib is ground to cocoa mass, and cocoa powder is milled from the press cake left after the butter has been pressed out. Tea leaf is not roasted and goes straight from sorting to blending.

RoasterCooling ConveyorWinnowerGrinding Mill MachineMicro Pulverizer Machine
Blending and packing

Coffee powder, tea leaf and cocoa powder are blended to the recipe and sifted, then filled into tea bags, pouches or jars and coded.

Ribbon MixerSifterTea BagAuger FillerJar PackingBatch Coding

Instant coffee — extraction, concentration and spray or freeze drying — is a separate route and is described on the Coffee industry page.

Route B — Fruit juice and pulp. Liquid route for juice, nectar and pulp from fresh fruit, packed in bottles, cups and pouches.

Fruit washing, sorting and preparation

Fruit is washed, sorted for soundness and size, and stemmed, peeled or de-seeded as the fruit requires before it is cut.

WasherGraderStem RemovalPeelerSeed Removal
Crushing, pulping and juice separation

Prepared fruit is crushed and the juice or pulp is screened away from skin, fibre and seed. Pulp for nectar and pulpy drinks is passed through a finer screen so that coarse fibre is reduced.

CrusherColloid MillScreener
Blending, homogenising and pasteurising

Juice or pulp is blended with sugar syrup and water in tank where the recipe calls for it, homogenised, and pasteurised before filling.

Jacketed TankAgitatorHomogenizerPasteurizer
Filling, sealing and cooling

Hot-filled bottles are capped and cooled through a tunnel; cups and pouches are filled and sealed on their own lines, then coded and cased.

Piston FillingCappingCooling TunnelCup & Tray FillingCup & Tray Sealing

Route C — Carbonated soft drinks and energy drinks. Liquid route for carbonated and still drinks made from treated water, sugar syrup, flavour and, for energy drinks, a caffeine and vitamin concentrate.

Syrup preparation

Sugar is dissolved in treated water with heating, the syrup is filtered and cooled, and flavour, acid and colour are added to make the finished syrup.

Jacketed TankAgitatorHeat ExchangerChillerStorage Tank
Blending and carbonation

Finished syrup is dosed into chilled treated water at the recipe ratio and carbon dioxide is dissolved into the chilled blend before it goes to the filler. Still drinks and non-carbonated energy drinks skip the carbonation stage.

Weighing & DosingChillerStorage Tank
Rinsing, filling and closing

Bottles and cans are rinsed, filled under counter-pressure so that the gas stays in solution, and then capped or seamed.

Container RinsingBottle Packing (Spray, Pet, Glass)Can PackingCappingSeaming
Inspection, coding and secondary packing

Filled containers are checked for leaks and fill level, coded and labelled, shrink-wrapped into packs and palletised for despatch.

Leak DetectorBatch CodingLabellingL Sealer With Heat Shrink TunnelPalletizer

Route D — Packaged drinking water. Liquid route for bottled water, cup and pouch water and large returnable jars.

Treated water storage and container handling

Treated water is held in a closed tank and piped to the filler; the water treatment plant sits ahead of the packing line. Empty bottles and jars are conveyed to the rinser and rinsed before filling.

Storage TankRoller ConveyorContainer Rinsing
Filling and capping

Bottles are filled and capped on one line; cups and pouches are filled and sealed on another; large returnable jars are filled and capped on a jar line.

Bottle Packing (Spray, Pet, Glass)CappingCup & Tray FillingCup & Tray SealingJar Packing
Coding, labelling and inspection

Each container is date coded, labelled and checked for leaks and correct fill before it leaves the line.

Batch CodingWrap Around LabellingLeak DetectorVision Inspection
Shrink packing and palletising

Bottles are collated into shrink packs, stacked on pallets and stretch-wrapped for storage and despatch.

L Sealer With Heat Shrink TunnelPalletizerStretch Wrapping

Route E — Powder premixes. Dry route for tea and coffee premix, cocoa and malt drink powders and instant drink mixes made from milk powder, sugar, creamer and flavour.

Ingredient intake and sifting

Milk powder, sugar, creamer, tea or coffee powder, cocoa and flavour are received in bags, tipped through a dump station, sifted and passed over magnets to catch tramp metal.

Bag Dump StationSifterMagnetic SeparatorVacuum Conveyor
Weighing and blending

Each ingredient is weighed to the recipe and blended to a dry mix. Sugar may be milled finer first so that it does not separate from the lighter powders in the pack.

Weighing & DosingMicro Pulverizer MachineRibbon MixerDouble Cone Mixer
Agglomeration (optional)

Where the premix has to disperse in hot water without lumps, the blend is moistened and dried into porous granules in a fluidised bed, with fines drawn off the air stream and returned to the bed.

Fluidized Bed Dryer – FBDCyclone SeparatorBag House Dust Collector Machine
Filling and packing

The finished premix is auger filled by weight into sachets, sticks, pouches or jars, nitrogen flushed where the recipe calls for it, then coded, weight checked and cased.

Auger FillerSachetStickNitrogen PackingCheckweigher With Printer
