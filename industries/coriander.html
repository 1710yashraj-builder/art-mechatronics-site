Coriander Processing Plant & Machinery Solutions

Turnkey plant · Machinery · Automation · India · UAE · Thailand

Coriander processing takes harvested coriander seed and turns it into cleaned whole seed, split coriander and ground coriander powder. Coriander seed is never boiled or cured; it is screened, de-stoned, graded and then either split for dal or milled to powder.

How it works

Complete Coriander Process Flow

Seed Cleaning

Incoming seed is screened to take out straw, dust and undersize, then passed over a de-stoner and gravity separator so stones and light chaff leave the stream. Magnets catch tramp metal picked up during threshing and transport.

Air Screen CleanerDe-StonerGravity SeparatorMagnetic Separator
Grading & Sorting

Cleaned seed is separated by size and the discoloured or immature grains are taken out optically. Grading keeps whole-seed lots uniform, which matters for buyers who sell coriander unmilled.

GraderColor SorterScreener
Splitting & Husk Removal

Part of the crop is split into halves for coriander dal and for easier milling. The loosened husk is drawn off by aspiration so it does not carry through to the mill.

SplitterRoller Mill MachineAir AspiratorWinnower
Grinding & Sifting

Seed is milled to the required mesh and the sifter returns oversize to the mill until the whole batch passes. The powder is separated from the conveying air in a cyclone before it reaches the packing floor.

Grinding Mill MachineHammer Mill MachinePlan SifterCyclone Separator
Packing

Whole, split and ground coriander are packed in bags or pouches, stitched or sealed, and batch coded.

Bag PackingStitchingForm Fill Seal (Ffs)Batch Coding
