How it works

Working Principle of Cup Packaging Machine

  • 1. Empty Cup Feeding Empty cups are automatically fed into packaging line through synchronized indexing systems.
  • 2. Product Filling Process Machine automatically fills products into cups using precision dosing and filling systems.

➜ Servo synchronization ensures accurate filling and smooth product handling.

  • 3. Cup Filling Operation Machine handles: ✔ Yogurt ✔ Instant noodles ✔ Beverages ✔ Desserts ✔ Sauces ✔ Dairy products ✔ Snack products

accurately and continuously.

  • 4. Cup Sealing & Lid Placement Process Machine performs: Heat sealing Foil sealing Lid placement Leak-proof sealing Nitrogen flushing (optional)

for hygienic and airtight retail packaging quality.

  • 5. Coding & Inspection Integration Optional systems include: Batch coding Date printing QR code printing Vision inspection Leak testing systems
  • 6. Finished Cup Discharge Finished cups discharged automatically for: Cartoning Case packing Retail distribution Cold chain logistics
