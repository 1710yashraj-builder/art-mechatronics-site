Coconut Powder Processing Plant & Machinery Solutions

Turnkey plant · Machinery · Automation · India · UAE · Thailand

Coconut powder, or desiccated coconut, is made from the white kernel of mature nuts. The coconuts are dehusked and shelled, the brown paring removed, and the kernel washed, cut or disintegrated, blanched, dried and then sifted into grades from fine to medium before packing.

How it works

Complete Coconut Process Flow

Dehusking and shell removal

Mature nuts are taken in, the fibrous husk is stripped off and the hard shell is cracked away from the kernel.

HopperDehuskingShell RemovalLoading & Unloading Conveyor
Paring and washing

The brown testa is pared off the kernel so only white meat goes forward, and the pared kernel is washed and rinsed.

Skin RemovalPeelerWasherRinsing
Cutting and disintegrating

White kernel is cut and then broken down to the particle the grade calls for — fine, medium or chip. Oversize is returned to the cutter.

Blade CutterCutter With SifterDisintegrator MachineGranulator
Blanching and sterilising

The cut meat is blanched with steam and then cooled before it enters the dryer.

BlancherSteam GeneratorSterilizationCooling Conveyor
Drying

Moisture is driven off in a hot-air dryer until the desiccated coconut is stable for storage and trade.

Continuous Conveyor, Tunnel DryerBatch Type - Hot Air DryerHot Air GeneratorBlower Fan
Sifting into grades

Dried coconut is screened and classified into the fine, medium and coarse grades that are sold separately.

SifterScreenerVibroAir Classifier
Weighing and packing

Each grade is weighed out, vacuum or bulk packed, metal checked and cased for despatch.

Weighing & DosingVacuumBag PackingMetal DetectorMaster Carton Packing
