Grams Processing Plant & Machinery Solutions

Turnkey plant · Machinery · Automation · India · UAE · Thailand

Gram processing covers black gram (urad) and green gram (moong), whose husk clings tightly to the kernel. The seed is cleaned, scratched or pitted, treated with oil and water, tempered and dried, then dehusked and split in repeated light passes rather than one heavy pass. Dal and flour are then sorted and packed.

How it works

Complete Grams Process Flow

Intake & Cleaning

Urad and moong arrive with field trash, dust, stones and metal, all of which are removed before milling. Cleaned grain is held in bins until the conditioning stage is ready to take it.

Pre-CleanerAir Screen CleanerDe-StonerMagnetic SeparatorDay Bin
Pitting & Oil-Water Conditioning

The husk of gram sticks to the kernel, so the seed surface is first scratched, or pitted, to open a path for the treatment. Oil and water are then mixed through the grain so that the husk lifts away in the milling that follows.

ScourerRibbon MixerJacketed TankScrew Conveyor
Tempering & Drying

Treated grain is held so the moisture can move into the seed, then dried before it goes to the huller. Moisture is checked on each batch as it comes off the dryer.

SiloBatch Type - Hot Air DryerTray Dryer, Tray OvenMoisture Analyzer
Dehusking & Splitting

Grain passes through the huller in repeated light passes rather than in a single heavy pass, and is lifted back to the huller between passes. Husk is drawn off between passes and the dehusked kernel is then split to give dhuli dal.

DehuskingWinnowerBucket ElevatorSplitterAir Aspirator
Polishing & Grading

Split gram dal is polished and then separated into whole, split and broken fractions. Powder and small brokens are carried away on their own stream.

PolisherGraderPlan SifterVibratory Conveyor
Flour Milling

Part of the urad and moong dal is ground into flour for papad, vada and batter use. The ground stock is sifted and the coarse fraction is returned to the mill.

Impact Pulverizer MachineHammer Mill MachinePlan SifterDust Extraction System
Sorting & Packing

Colour sorting removes husk-bearing, discoloured and off-grade grains before packing. Dal goes into consumer pouches and bulk sacks, and every pack is coded.

Color SorterMetal DetectorStand Up PouchBag PackingBatch Coding
