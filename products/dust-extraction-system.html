How it works

Working Principle of Dust Extraction System

Dust Generation

Dust is generated from processes such as:

GrindingCuttingMixingConveyingPackagingSandingCrushing
Dust Capture (AT SOURCE)

Dust is captured using:

Suction HoodPickup NozzlesFlexible Connections

➜ Prevents dust from spreading

Air Conveying System

Dust-laden air is transported through:

Ducting NetworkIndustrial Blowers / Fans
Filtration

Dust is separated using:

Bag FiltersCartridge Filters

➜ Ensures high-efficiency dust removal

Dust Collection & Disposal

Collected dust is discharged through:

HopperRotary Airlock ValveCollection Bin
Clean Air Discharge

Filtered air is safely released or recirculated.
