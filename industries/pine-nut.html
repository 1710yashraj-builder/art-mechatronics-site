Pine Nut Processing Plant & Machinery Solutions

Turnkey plant · Machinery · Automation · India · UAE · Thailand

Pine nut processing extracts the seed from the pine cone, removes the hard seed shell and the thin inner skin, and grades the kernel for retail and food service. Cones are dried until the scales open, seeds are separated from cone debris, and the kernel is then shelled, peeled, dried gently, colour sorted and sealed.

How it works

Complete Pine Nut Process Flow

Cone Drying & Seed Extraction

Harvested cones are dried until the scales open and the seeds can be shaken free and separated out. Cone scales, resin and stalk waste leave with the seed stream and are removed on the same pass.

Rotary Dryer, Drum DryerBatch Type - Hot Air DryerSeed RemovalShakerWinnower
Cleaning & Empty Seed Removal

Empty and part-filled seeds, grit and stones are separated out and the seed is sized so shelling can be set to each band. Empty seed taken out here does not travel through the rest of the line or dilute the shelling yield.

Air Screen CleanerDe-StonerGravity SeparatorGrader
Shelling

The hard outer shell is cracked and separated from the kernel by air and density, with shell fragments drawn off. Pine nut kernel is small and soft, so the handling through this stage is deliberately gentle.

Shell RemovalAir AspiratorGravity SeparatorVibratory Conveyor
Skin Removal & Drying

The thin skin left clinging to the kernel is rubbed off and the kernel is dried back to a stable condition. Skin flakes and dust are drawn away by suction as they are released.

Skin RemovalPeelerBatch Type - Hot Air DryerDust Collector MachineMoisture Analyzer
Sorting & Packing

Kernels are colour sorted to remove dark, broken and shrivelled seed, checked for metal, then weighed and filled. The kernel is high in oil, so vacuum and gas-flushed packs are used for retail and export.

Color SorterVision InspectionMetal DetectorVacuumNitrogen Packing
