How it works

Complete Tobacco Process Flow

Leaf intake and cleaning

Cured leaf arrives in bales and is opened out, fed onto the line and separated from sand, string and metal. Loose dust and light trash are drawn off before the leaf goes forward.

HopperPre-CleanerAir AspiratorMagnetic SeparatorDe-Dusting System
Conditioning

Leaf is brought to a workable moisture with warm, humidified air so it flexes instead of shattering when cut. Moisture is checked on the way through.

Steam GeneratorAir Handling UnitHot Air GeneratorMoisture Analyzer
Cutting and shredding

Conditioned leaf and stem are cut to the width the product calls for — fine cut for cigarette and rolling tobacco, coarser for shisha and cigar filler. Cut material is screened to take out fines.

Shredder MachineBlade CutterKnife CutterCutter With Sifter
Drying and cooling

Cut tobacco is dried to bring the moisture back down after conditioning, then cooled before it is stored or blended. The dryer type follows the batch or continuous layout of the plant.

Continuous Conveyor, Tunnel DryerRotary Dryer, Drum DryerTray Dryer, Tray OvenHot Air GeneratorCooling Conveyor
Blending, casing and flavouring

Different grades are weighed out and mixed to a recipe, and casing or flavour is applied where the product uses it — shisha, dokha and flavoured rolling tobacco branch here. Chewing blends such as khaini and zarda take their additives at this stage.

Weighing & DosingRibbon MixerPlough Shear MixerDrum Mixer
Grinding for powder and pouch products

Snuff, tobacco powder, filter khaini and oral pouch fillings need the blend reduced to a fine particle and then classified. This branch runs under dust extraction because the material is airborne.

Micro Pulverizer MachineAir Classifier Mill MachineSifterDust Collector Machine
Filling, packing and coding

Finished tobacco is filled by weight into pouches, tins or pouch sachets, sealed, coded and cased. Oral pouch products are formed and filled on their own dedicated equipment.

Oral PouchesPouch FillingForm Fill Seal (Ffs)Printer (Cij, Inkjet, Co2 Laser, Hand)Master Carton Packing
