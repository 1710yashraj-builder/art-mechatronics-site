Industrial Dust Collector Machine & Dust Collection System for Thailand

A Dust Collector Machine captures dust at grinders, mixers, dryers and bag-tipping points and returns filtered air to the plant. Thai rice mills, sugar refineries, tapioca starch works, spice and seasoning plants and animal feed mills use dust collection at such points. ART Mechatronics has an in-plant dust collector with ducting installed at a Thai customer site and supports plants from its Pattaya office.

Supplied to plants in Thailand from ART's Thailand Marketing Office in Pattaya City, with a service team in Chonburi; designed and built in Kanpur, India.

Why Thai processors choose the Industrial Dust Collector Machine & Dust Collection System

  • Humid Thai air makes flour, starch and sugar dust cling and cake; extraction at source keeps it off floors and motors.
  • ART offers Bag House Dust Collector Machine and Cyclone Dust Collector Machine variants for rice bran, cassava flour, chilli and feed dust.
  • A single Centralised Dust Collection System (Multi-Point) can serve ART's Hammer Mill Machine, Bag Dump Station and Fluidized Bed Dryer – FBD.
  • ART's service team in Chonburi handles filter changes, ducting extensions and commissioning for Thai plants.
