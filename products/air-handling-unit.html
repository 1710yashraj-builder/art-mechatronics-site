How it works

Working Principle of Air Handling Unit Machine

  • 1. Air Intake & Fresh Air Circulation Process Fresh air and return air are drawn into air handling unit through intake systems.
  • 2. Air Filtration & Conditioning Process Machine filters air using pre-filters, fine filters, and HEPA filtration systems while cooling, heating, or humidifying air as required.

➜ Advanced air conditioning technology ensures clean, temperature-controlled, and balanced airflow operations.

  • 3. Air Processing & Environmental Control Operation Machine handles: ✔ Air circulation ✔ Air filtration ✔ Temperature control ✔ Humidity management ✔ Ventilation operations ✔ Dust control ✔ Pressure balancing ✔ Environmental stabilization

accurately and continuously.

  • 4. Conditioned Air Distribution Process Machine performs: Air purification Cooling and heating Humidity control Dust removal Fresh air supply Industrial ventilation

for efficient industrial environmental management operations.

  • 5. Intelligent Automation & Energy Management Integration Optional systems include: HEPA filtration systems UV sterilization systems Variable frequency drives (VFD) Remote monitoring systems Automatic damper controls
  • 6. Conditioned Air Supply & Recirculation Processed air discharged automatically for: Cleanrooms Production halls Packaging areas HVAC duct systems Industrial process environments
