How it works

Working Principle of Spiral Conveyor Machine

Product Loading

Products or packages are fed onto spiral conveyor inlet section

Spiral Belt Movement

Motor-driven conveyor belt or modular chain moves continuously along spiral track

➜ Products travel upward or downward in spiral path

Vertical Product Transportation

Machine transports:

  • Food products
  • Bottles
  • Packets
  • Cartons
  • Boxes
  • Containers
  • Industrial products between different elevations efficiently.
Space-Saving Spiral Transfer

Spiral structure minimizes floor space usage while maximizing vertical conveying height

Product Accumulation & Buffering

Spiral conveyor can provide temporary product accumulation during production flow balancing

Product Discharge

Products discharged automatically at desired upper or lower transfer point
