Durum Processing Plant & Machinery Solutions

Turnkey plant · Machinery · Automation · India · UAE · Thailand

Durum wheat processing mills hard amber grain into semolina for pasta and couscous rather than into fine flour. Durum is cleaned, scoured and tempered with water so the bran peels away cleanly, then reduced on corrugated rolls and sifted so the coarse endosperm granules are recovered.

How it works

Complete Durum Process Flow

Intake, Cleaning & Storage

Durum is received into silo storage and cleaned of straw, dust, stones and metal before milling. Lots are kept separate so grain of different vitreousness can be blended deliberately.

Air Screen CleanerDe-StonerMagnetic SeparatorZ Type ElevatorSilo
Scouring & Tempering

The kernel surface is scoured, then water is added and the grain is rested in tempering bins so the bran toughens and the endosperm mellows. Moisture is checked before the grain goes to first break.

ScourerWasherDay BinScrew ConveyorMoisture Analyzer
Break Milling on Corrugated Rolls

Tempered durum is opened gradually between corrugated rolls so the endosperm is released as coarse granules instead of being crushed to flour. Stock is elevated between passages and re-sifted each time.

Roller Mill MachineBucket ElevatorPlan Sifter
Purification & Granulation Control

Sifted stock is classified by size and by air so bran flakes and germ are lifted away from the heavier semolina granules. This purification step is what sets a durum mill apart from a bread-flour mill.

Plan SifterAir ClassifierAir AspiratorSifter
Speck Sorting & Blending

Semolina is sorted to remove dark specks of bran, then blended to a consistent grade before storage.

Color SorterVision InspectionConical Screw MixerSilo
Packing & Bulk Despatch

Finished semolina is dosed into bags for retail and trade, or loaded in bulk for pasta plants. Bags are weighed, stitched, coded and palletised.
