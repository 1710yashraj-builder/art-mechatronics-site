Granite Processing Plant & Machinery Solutions

Turnkey plant · Machinery · Automation · India · UAE · Thailand

Granite processing turns hard quarried blocks into slabs and cut-to-size pieces. Blocks are squared and sawn, slabs are reinforced with epoxy and mesh and cured, then finished as polished, honed or flame-textured surfaces before edge cutting and grading. Cutting water carries stone slurry that must be settled.

How it works

Complete Process Flow

Block Inspection & Sawing

Granite blocks are inspected for colour and cracks, squared, and sawn into slabs. Cutting runs with water, which cools the cutting edge and flushes the cut.

Blade CutterTrolleyRoller ConveyorVision Inspection
Epoxy Coating & Curing

Slabs are coated with epoxy, and fractured or softer material is backed with mesh, so that the slab survives handling and polishing. The coating is set in a drying tunnel before the surface is worked.

Continuous Conveyor, Tunnel DryerHot Air GeneratorRoller ConveyorStirrer
Surface Finishing

Granite is finished as polished, honed or flame-textured, depending on where it will be laid. Flaming uses a burner to spall the surface, while polishing and honing use abrasive heads.

PolisherSurface GrinderGrinding MachineFurnace Burner
Cutting to Size & Inspection

Slabs are edge-trimmed and cut into the sizes ordered, then checked for colour match and finish. Pieces are numbered so that a job can be laid in the sequence it was cut.

GraderBlade CutterVision InspectionSlat Conveyor
Slurry & Dust Management

Sawing and polishing produce a stone slurry that has to be settled out so the water can be used again. Dry cutting and flaming points are kept under extraction.

Storage TankAgitatorWet ScrubberDust Extraction System
Packing & Loading

Finished slabs are loaded into A-frames or crates with separators between them and labelled by lot. Crates are wrapped and moved to despatch.

Stretch WrappingPalletizerLabellingTelescopic Conveyor
