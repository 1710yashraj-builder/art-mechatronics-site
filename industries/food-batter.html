Food Batter Processing Plant & Machinery Solutions

Turnkey plant · Machinery · Automation · India · UAE · Thailand

Food batter processing produces the dry coating and breading mixes used on snacks, fritters and nuggets, and the wet fermented rice and pulse batters sold for idli and dosa. Grains and pulses are cleaned, then milled dry or wet ground, blended with starches, salt and spices, and filled.

How it works

Complete Food Batter Process Flow

Grain & Pulse Cleaning

Rice, urad dal, maize and wheat are cleaned of husk, dust, stones and tramp metal. Clean intake protects the mill and keeps grit out of the finished batter.

Air Screen CleanerDe-StonerGravity SeparatorMagnetic SeparatorBucket Elevator
Dry Milling & Sifting

For dry batter and breading premixes, cleaned grain is milled into flour and sifted to separate the coarse fraction. Particle size is what controls how the batter picks up and clings to the product.

Grinding Mill MachineRoller Mill MachinePlan SifterSieverPneumatic Conveyor
Soaking, Wet Grinding & Fermentation

For wet batter, soaked rice and dal are wet ground into a smooth slurry. The slurry is held in tanks to ferment, which is what develops the sour flavour and the rise.

Wet GrinderStone Mill MachineStorage TankJacketed TankAgitator
Weighing & Blending

Flours, starches, leavening, salt and spices are weighed to recipe and blended into a uniform mix. The blend is passed over a sieve and a metal check before filling.

Weighing & DosingRibbon MixerPlough Shear MixerSifterMetal Detector
Filling & Packing

Dry premix is filled by auger into pouches, bags or sacks; wet batter is filled by volume into pouches or jars. Filled packs are weight-checked and coded.

Auger FillerForm Fill Seal (Ffs)Piston FillingStand Up PouchCheckweigher With Printer
Chilled Holding & Dispatch

Wet fermented batter is chilled and held cold, because fermentation carries on at room temperature. Packs are moved through a cold room and dispatched on the cold chain.

ChillerCold Storage/ Cold RoomRoller ConveyorBatch Coding
