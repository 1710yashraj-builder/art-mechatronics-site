How it works

Working Principle of Multi Track Packing Machine

Film Feeding Across Multiple Lanes

Laminated film roll feeds into multiple packaging tracks simultaneously

Multi Lane Pouch Forming Process

Machine forms multiple sachets side-by-side in synchronized operation

➜ Each track independently performs pouch formation and filling operations simultaneously.

Product Filling Operation

Machine handles:

  • Powders
  • Granules
  • Liquids
  • Paste products
  • Pharma powders
  • Beverage premixes
  • Cosmetic products accurately and continuously.
Simultaneous Sealing & Cutting

Multi-lane sealing systems perform: 3 side seal 4 side seal Center seal Stick pack sealing at ultra high production speeds.

PLC & Servo Automation Control

System controls: Multi-track synchronization Filling accuracy Packaging speed Film tracking Sealing temperature

High-Speed Sachet Discharge

Finished sachets discharged automatically for: Collection Cartoning Secondary packaging Dispatch operations
