How it works

Complete Groundnut Process Flow

Raw Groundnut Handling

Groundnuts (in-shell) are received and transferred into the processing system.

Material Handling SystemsFeeding Systems

➜ Ensures smooth and continuous input.

Cleaning & Destoning

Groundnuts are cleaned to remove dust, stones, and foreign particles.

Pre CleanerDestonerAir Classifier

➜ Ensures product purity and protects machinery.

Shelling / Decortication

Shells are removed to obtain clean peanut kernels.

Groundnut Shelling MachineDecorticator

➜ Ensures:

  • High shelling efficiency
  • Minimal kernel breakage
Grading & Sorting

Kernels are graded based on size and quality.

Grading MachineVibratory GraderColor Sorter (Optional)

➜ Ensures uniform and premium output.

Blanching (OPTIONAL)

Skin is removed from peanuts for further processing.

Blanching Machine
Roasting / Frying
For Roasted Peanuts:Dry roasting using hot air

Machines Used:

  • Roaster
  • Continuous Roasting System
  • For Fried Peanuts:
  • Oil frying for crispy texture

Machines Used:

  • Batch Fryer
  • Continuous Fryer

➜ Ensures:

  • Uniform roasting/frying
  • Enhanced taste and texture
De-Oiling (FOR FRIED PRODUCTS)

Excess oil is removed after frying.

De-Oiling Machine
Coating & Flavoring (VALUE ADDITION)

Peanuts may be coated with spices, flour, or flavors.

Coating DrumBatter Mixing SystemSeasoning Drum

  • Masala peanuts
  • Coated snack products
Grinding / Processing (OPTIONAL VALUE ADDITION)
Peanut Butter Production:Grinding peanuts into pasteOil Extraction:Pressing peanuts to extract oil

Cooling

Cooling Conveyor
Quality Inspection
