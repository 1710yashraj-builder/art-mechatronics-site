Eraser Manufacturing Plant & Machinery Solutions

Turnkey plant · Machinery · Automation · India · UAE · Thailand

Eraser manufacturing blends synthetic rubber or vinyl compound with mineral filler, a mild abrasive and pigment, forms the blend into slabs or extruded strip, and cuts it into individual erasers that are trimmed, sleeved in printed card and packed.

How it works

Complete Eraser Process Flow

Powder Intake & Sieving

Mineral filler, abrasive and pigment are tipped from sacks, screened and conveyed to the mixing floor. Screening removes lumps that would otherwise show as hard spots in the finished eraser.

Bag Dump StationSifterVibroPneumatic ConveyorFlat Bag Dust Collector Machine
Weighing & Compound Mixing

Rubber or vinyl, plasticiser, filler, abrasive and pigment are weighed to recipe and mixed into a uniform compound. Colour and abrasive distribution are set here and cannot be corrected further down the line.

Weighing & DosingSigma MixerHigh Shear MixerMixer Automation SystemDust Collector Machine
Forming & Cooling

The compound is extruded as strip or rolled into slabs, then cooled so it becomes firm enough to cut.

Extruder MachineRoller Mill MachineCooling ConveyorCooling TunnelBlower Fan
Cutting to Size & Trimming

Slab or strip is cut into individual erasers and the edges are trimmed to the finished shape. Cutting fines are extracted at the knife so they do not mark the eraser faces.

Blade CutterKnife CutterCube CutterDe-Dusting SystemVibratory Conveyor
Sleeving, Printing & Inspection

Erasers are sleeved in printed card or wrapped, marked with brand and grade, and checked for shape and colour.

Over WrappingPrinter (Cij, Inkjet, Co2 Laser, Hand)Vision InspectionAuto Reject SystemRotary Collecting Table
Counting, Packing & Despatch

Sleeved erasers are counted into inner packs, flow-wrapped or boxed, and collated into master cartons.

Count & StackFlow WrapMono Carton PackingLabellingMaster Carton Packing
