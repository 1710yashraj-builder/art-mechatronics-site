How it works

Working Principle of Day Bin Machine

Material Loading

Powders, granules, grains, flakes, or bulk materials are loaded into day bin using: ✔ Conveyors ✔ Pneumatic conveying systems ✔ Bucket elevators ✔ Manual feeding systems

Intermediate Material Storage (CORE PROCESS)

Day bin temporarily stores material to maintain continuous supply for downstream production process

➜ Prevents production interruptions and ensures smooth factory operation.

Material Buffering & Flow Management

Machine handles: ✔ Supari ✔ Pan masala ✔ Tobacco powder ✔ Spices ✔ Flour ✔ Pellets ✔ Granules with controlled and efficient storage.

Controlled Material Discharge

Material discharged through: Hopper bottom outlet Screw feeder Vibratory feeder Rotary airlock valve Pneumatic discharge system

Automation Integration

Day bin integrates with: Mixers Packaging machines Conveyors Weighing systems Processing lines

Continuous Production Feeding

Material supplied continuously to process equipment automatically
