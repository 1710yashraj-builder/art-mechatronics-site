How it works

Working Principle of Bag Packing Machine

  • 1. Empty Bag Feeding Empty bags are manually or automatically positioned into bag holding section.
  • 2. Product Weighing Process Machine automatically weighs product using precision load cell and dosing systems.

➜ Servo synchronization ensures accurate weighing and smooth material flow.

  • 3. Bag Filling Operation Machine handles: ✔ Powders ✔ Granules ✔ Snacks ✔ Grains ✔ Chemicals ✔ Animal feed ✔ FMCG products

accurately and continuously.

  • 4. Bag Sealing & Closing Process Machine performs: Heat sealing Bag stitching Valve sealing Leak-proof bag closing operations

for secure transportation and storage packaging quality.

  • 5. Conveyor & Dispatch Integration Filled bags transferred automatically through: Conveyors Bag flatteners Check weighers Metal detectors Palletizing systems
  • 6. Finished Bag Discharge Finished bags discharged automatically for: Warehouse storage Truck loading Dispatch operations Export shipment handling
