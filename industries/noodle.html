How it works

Complete Noodle Process Flow

Raw Material Handling

Raw materials such as wheat flour (maida/semolina), water, starch, and additives are received and fed into the system.

Material Handling SystemsStorage Silos / Feeders

➜ Ensures continuous and controlled feeding.

Mixing & Dough Preparation

Flour and water are mixed to form dough with proper elasticity.

Dough MixerPre-Mixer

➜ Ensures:

  • Uniform hydration
  • Consistent dough structure
  • High-quality noodle texture
Sheeting / Extrusion
For Long & Flat Noodles (Hakka, Chowmein):Dough is passed through rollers to form sheets

Machines Used:

  • Sheeting Machine
  • Roller System
  • For Round / Extruded Types (Spaghetti, Vermicelli):
  • Dough is extruded through dies

Machines Used:

  • Noodle Extruder

➜ Ensures:

  • Uniform thickness
  • Consistent shape
Cutting & Shaping

Sheets or extruded dough are cut into required noodle shapes.

Cutting Machine

➜ Produces:

  • Flat noodles
  • Round noodles
  • Thin vermicelli strands
Steaming / Cooking

Noodles are partially cooked using steam.

SteamerContinuous Steam Cooker

➜ Improves:

  • Texture
  • Product stability
Drying or Frying
For Instant Noodles:Fried for quick cooking

Machines Used:

  • Continuous Fryer
  • For Dried Noodles:
  • Dried using controlled air

Machines Used:

  • Dryer
  • Multi-Stage Drying System

➜ Ensures:

  • Long shelf life
  • Product strength
Cooling

Product is cooled before packaging.

Cooling Conveyor
Quality Inspection

Final inspection ensures consistency in shape, size, and quality.

Inspection System
