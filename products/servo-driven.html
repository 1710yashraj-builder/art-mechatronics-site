How it works

Working Principle of Servo Driven Packing Machine

Product Feeding

Products are fed into machine through:

  • Multihead weighers
  • Auger fillers
  • Cup fillers
  • Conveyors
  • Vibratory feeders
Servo Motion Synchronization

High-precision servo motors control:

  • Film movement
  • Pouch positioning
  • Filling timing
  • Sealing operations
  • Cutting mechanisms

➜ Servo synchronization ensures ultra-accurate packaging with smooth continuous operation.

Precision Product Filling

Machine handles:

  • Powders
  • Granules
  • Pan masala
  • Tobacco
  • Spices
  • Snacks
  • FMCG products with high-speed precision.
Intelligent Sealing & Cutting

Servo-controlled sealing systems perform: Center sealing Side sealing Sachet sealing Precision cutting accurately and continuously.

PLC & HMI Automation Control

System manages: Packaging speed Servo synchronization Product flow Film tracking Temperature control Production monitoring

Continuous High-Speed Packaging

Packed products discharged automatically for: Collection Cartoning Secondary packaging Dispatch operations
