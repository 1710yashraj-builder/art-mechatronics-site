Nuts Processing Plant & Machinery Solutions

Turnkey plant · Machinery · Automation · India · UAE · Thailand

Nut processing covers groundnut, cashew, almond, walnut, pistachio, areca and betel nut, coconut, foxnut and dates. Although each kernel is handled differently, the route is common: cleaning and grading, shell and skin removal, drying or roasting, sorting, and packing.

How it works

Complete Groundnut Process Flow

Intake, cleaning and de-stoning

Nuts in shell are taken in and cleaned of soil, stones, sticks and tramp metal on a screen and aspiration cleaner. Cleaned stock is held in bins ahead of shelling.

HopperPre-CleanerAir Screen CleanerDe-StonerMagnetic Separator
Shelling and dehusking

The shell or husk is cracked and opened, then kernel and shell are separated by air and by weight. Areca, betel and coconut go through their own dehusking route.

DehuskingShell RemovalWinnowerGravity Separator
Skin removal and polishing

The testa or skin is loosened and taken off, and kernels are polished where the product is sold blanched. Almonds and groundnuts are the main lines that use this stage.

BlancherSkin RemovalPeelerPolisher
Drying or roasting

Kernels are dried down for storage, or roasted where the product is a roasted nut, and then cooled before sorting. Dates and foxnut branch to their own drying schedules.

RoasterBatch Type - Hot Air DryerTray Dryer, Tray OvenCooling Conveyor
Grading and sorting

Kernels are graded to size, then sorted to remove discoloured, split and foreign pieces. Sorting is done optically and by hand pick on the belt.

GraderColor SorterVibratory FeederSorting Robot
Value-added branches

Some kernels go on to be split, milled into butter or paste, or pressed for oil rather than sold whole. Groundnut is the usual candidate for all three.

SplitterColloid MillWet GrinderExpeller Machine
Weighing and packing

Finished kernels are weighed, checked for foreign body and filled into pouches, vacuum packs or jars. Pack format follows whether the nut goes to retail or to bulk trade.

Multi Head WeigherX Ray InspectionVacuumPremade PouchJar Packing
