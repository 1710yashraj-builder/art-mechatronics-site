Industrial Chopper Machine for Thailand

A Chopper is a single large knife working over a conveyor that chops vegetables, herbs, roots and fibrous agro material into coarse pieces. Thai curry paste, seasoning, frozen vegetable, pet food and biomass pellet lines use it as the first size-reduction step. Thai users of the Chopper are served by ART Mechatronics' marketing office in Pattaya.

Supplied to plants in Thailand from ART's Thailand Marketing Office in Pattaya City, with a service team in Chonburi; designed and built in Kanpur, India.

For Thai plants

Why Thai processors choose the Industrial Chopper Machine

  • Lemongrass, galangal, chilli and kaffir lime leaf for Thai curry paste are chopped before the Colloid Mill or Wet Grinder.
  • Sugarcane trash, corn stalk and palm waste for Thai biomass pellet plants pass through the Chopper before a Hammer Mill Machine.
  • Vegetable and fish material for Thai pet, aqua and poultry feed is cut to a size the Extruder Machine accepts.
  • Knife changes and Spares for Thai plants are arranged by ART's service team in Chonburi; the Pattaya office takes enquiries.
