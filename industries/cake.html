How it works

Complete Cake Process Flow

Raw Material Handling & Dosing

Ingredients such as flour, sugar, eggs, oil, milk powder, flavors, and additives are received and fed into the system.

Material Handling SystemsIngredient Dosing System

➜ Ensures accurate formulation and consistency.

Batter Preparation

Ingredients are mixed to form a smooth batter.

Cake MixerPlanetary Mixer

➜ Ensures:

  • Uniform mixing
  • Proper aeration
  • Soft cake texture
Depositing

Batter is deposited into moulds or trays.

Automatic Depositor

➜ Ensures:

  • Uniform portioning
  • Consistent product size
Baking

Batter is baked under controlled temperature.

Industrial Oven (Tunnel / Rotary Oven)

➜ Ensures:

  • Even baking
  • Soft and fluffy texture
Cooling

Baked cakes are cooled before further processing.

Cooling Conveyor

➜ Prevents condensation and maintains structure.

De-moulding

Cakes are removed from trays or moulds.

Cream Filling / Injection (OPTIONAL)

Cream, chocolate, or jam may be injected or layered.

Cream Filling MachineInjection System

➜ Produces:

  • Cream-filled cakes
  • Chocolate-filled cakes
Decoration (OPTIONAL)

Toppings or coatings may be applied.

Packaging (HIGH-SPEED FMCG)

Finished cakes are packed into individual packs.

Flow Wrap MachineHorizontal Packaging Machine

➜ Ensures:

  • Hygienic packaging
  • Extended shelf life
