Ravioli Processing Plant & Machinery Solutions

Turnkey plant · Machinery · Automation · India · UAE · Thailand

Ravioli is a filled pasta: a savoury filling is enclosed between two thin dough sheets, then sealed and cut into individual pockets. The filling is prepared and cooled separately, deposited onto a moving sheet, covered, crimped and cut, after which the pockets are frozen or chilled and packed.

How it works

Complete Ravioli Process Flow

Filling Preparation & Cooling

Cheese, vegetable or meat ingredients are size-reduced, cooked and blended into a filling, then cooled before it meets the dough. A warm filling would soften the sheet and break the seal, so the filling is chilled first.

ChopperCooking Kettle, Boiling TankJacketed TankChiller
Dough Mixing & Sheeting

A separate dough is mixed and formed into two thin, even sheets, one for the base and one for the cover.

Weighing & DosingSpiral MixerExtruder Machine
Depositing, Sealing & Cutting

Filling is deposited at fixed intervals on the base sheet, the cover sheet is laid over it, and the pockets are pressed, sealed and cut apart. Seal integrity decides whether the ravioli survives boiling without bursting.

FormingCrimpingSealingConveyor Belt
Freezing

Formed ravioli is chilled and frozen piece by piece so the pockets do not fuse together in the bag.

Cooling TunnelIQF FreezerCold Storage/ Cold Room
Packing & Inspection

Frozen pockets are weighed into bags, sealed, checked for metal and pack weight, and cased for cold despatch.

Multi Head WeigherVertical Form Fill Seal (Vffs)Metal DetectorMaster Case Packing
