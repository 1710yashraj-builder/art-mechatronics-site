Lassi Processing Plant & Machinery Solutions

Turnkey plant · Machinery · Automation · India · UAE · Thailand

Lassi processing blends set curd with water, sugar and flavouring into a drinkable cultured beverage. Sugar syrup is prepared and filtered, the curd base is broken down and mixed to the recipe, and the blend is homogenised, heat treated and chilled before filling into bottles, cups or pouches.

How it works

Complete Lassi Process Flow

Curd Base Preparation

Set curd is produced in house or drawn from chilled storage and broken down into a smooth, pumpable base. The acidity of this base sets the taste profile of the finished drink.

Jacketed TankStirrerStorage TankChiller
Sugar Syrup Preparation & Filtration

Sugar is dissolved in water, boiled and passed through a sieve so no undissolved crystals or bag fibres reach the blend. Sweet lassi lines run this stage continuously alongside the curd base.

Bag Dump StationCooking Kettle, Boiling TankAgitatorSifterSteam Generator
Blending & Standardisation

Curd base, syrup, water, salt or fruit pulp are metered into the blend tank and mixed to the recipe. Recipe control at this stage is what keeps batch-to-batch taste consistent.

High Shear MixerWeighing & DosingStorage TankMixer Automation System
Homogenisation & Heat Treatment

The blend is homogenised so the drink pours evenly and does not separate in the bottle, then heat treated. Ambient shelf-stable lassi runs a sterilisation step in place of simple chilling.

HomogenizerPasteurizerSterilizationHeat Exchanger
Chilling & Filling

The drink is chilled and filled into rinsed bottles, cups or pouches. Bottle lines rinse, fill and cap in sequence, while pouch lines form, fill and seal from film.

ChillerRinsingBottle Packing (Spray, Pet, Glass)CappingForm Fill Seal (Ffs)
Labelling, Coding & Crating

Filled packs are labelled, date coded and checked for leaks. They are then shrink sleeved or crated and moved onto the cold chain.

Wrap Around LabellingBatch CodingLeak TesterHeat Shrink TunnelTray Packing
