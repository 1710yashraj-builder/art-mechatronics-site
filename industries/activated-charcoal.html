Coconut Shell Activated Charcoal Processing Plant & Machinery Solutions

Turnkey plant · Machinery · Automation · India · UAE · Thailand

Activated charcoal from coconut shell is a porous carbon used in water treatment, air purification, gold recovery and food and pharmaceutical applications. Shell is carbonised into charcoal, crushed and sized, activated with steam in a furnace, then cooled, milled and screened to the grade required.

How it works

Complete Activated Charcoal Water Process Flow

Shell Intake & Carbonisation

Coconut shell is separated from residual kernel and fibre, then carbonised in a closed kiln so that volatiles are driven off and charcoal remains. The plant is laid out so that carbonisation off-gas is taken to the emission control section rather than released.

Shell RemovalFurnace BurnerApron ConveyorChain Conveyor
Crushing & Sizing

Charcoal is crushed and screened into the particle bands the activation stage needs, since granular and powdered grades start from different feed sizes. Undersize fines are collected separately for the powdered grade instead of going into the activation feed.

CrusherGranulatorScreenerVibroBucket Elevator
Steam Activation

Sized charcoal is heated in the activation furnace and steam is injected, which opens the pore structure that gives the carbon its adsorption capacity. Steam supply and furnace heating are run from the utility section serving the activation line.

Furnace BurnerSteam GeneratorBoilerRotary Tube Dryer
Cooling & Emission Control

Activated carbon is cooled after discharge, and the flue and process gases pass through scrubbing and dust collection before venting. Emission control equipment is treated as part of the process line, not an afterthought, because carbonisation and activation both generate gas and dust.

Cooling ConveyorWet ScrubberDry ScrubberCyclone Dust Collector MachineAir Pollution Control Equipment
Milling & Grading

Cooled carbon is milled and classified into granular and powdered grades against the mesh specification ordered. Off-spec material is returned to the mill rather than blended into the graded product.

Grinding Mill MachineBall Mill MachineAir ClassifierPlan SifterPulverizer With Dust Collector
De-dusting, Bagging & Despatch

Graded carbon is de-dusted so that loose fines do not travel with the pack, then filled into sacks, drums or bulk bags. Filled packs are weighed, sealed and palletised, with the packing hall kept under dust extraction.
