Rice Milk Processing Plant & Machinery Solutions

Turnkey plant · Machinery · Automation · India · UAE · Thailand

Rice milk is a plant-based drink made by cooking cleaned rice, breaking down its starch into simpler sugars, then milling the mash, separating the solids and blending the liquid with water and oil before homogenising, heat treatment and filling.

How it works

Complete Rice Milk Process Flow

Grain Cleaning

Incoming rice is passed over screens and a de-stoner to take out chaff, dust, stones and stray metal. Clean grain protects the mill and keeps grit out of the finished drink.

Air Screen CleanerDe-StonerMagnetic SeparatorVibratory Feeder
Washing & Cooking

Cleaned rice is washed and then cooked in a jacketed vessel until the starch granules swell and gelatinise. Gelatinised starch is what the enzymes in the next stage are able to act on.

WasherCooking Kettle, Boiling TankJacketed TankAgitator
Enzymatic Conversion

Enzymes are dosed into the cooked mash, which is held under gentle stirring while the starch is broken into simpler sugars. This conversion is what gives rice milk its sweetness.

StirrerWeighing & DosingHeat ExchangerPLC Automation Control Panel
Wet Milling & Separation

The converted mash is milled down to a fine slurry and the insoluble rice solids are drawn off. What remains is the liquid base of the drink.

Wet GrinderColloid MillSeparatorSifter
Blending & Homogenising

Water, oil and mineral blends are mixed into the base to set body and mouthfeel. The mix then passes through a homogeniser so it stays uniform and does not settle in the pack.

High Shear MixerStorage TankHomogenizerAgitator
Heat Treatment & Filling

The drink is heat-treated, cooled and filled into cartons, bottles or pouches on a closed line. Sealed packs are coded and collated into cases for dispatch.

PasteurizerSterilizationFillingForm Fill Seal (Ffs)Master Carton Packing
