Fruits Processing Plant & Machinery Solutions

Turnkey plant · Machinery · Automation · India · UAE · Thailand

Fruit processing takes harvested fruit from the orchard crate to a graded, packed or further-processed product. Fruit arrives loose, is tipped onto a collecting table, washed and surface dried, then sorted and graded by size, colour and defect.

How it works

Complete Fruits Process Flow

Reception & Tipping

Crates and bins are emptied onto a collecting table that spreads the fruit into a single, even flow. A holding bin lets the line keep running between vehicle deliveries.

Loading & Unloading ConveyorRotary Collecting TableDay BinRoller Conveyor
Washing & Surface Drying

Fruit is washed and rinsed to remove field dust and residues, then surface dried with moving air. Brushing gives the skin an even finish before the fruit reaches the graders.

WasherRinsingBlower FanPolisher
Sorting & Grading

Fruit is separated by colour, size and visible defect so each grade goes to the market that pays for it. Rejected and second-grade fruit is diverted rather than discarded, and feeds the processing lines.

Color SorterGraderVision InspectionAuto Reject System
Peeling, De-Seeding & Cutting

Fruit routed to processing is peeled, halved or de-seeded and cut to the shape the downstream line needs. Whole fruit for the fresh market bypasses this stage entirely.

PeelerSplitterSeed RemovalSlicer
Onward Processing Routes

Cut fruit goes to the dryer, to the freezer or to crushing for pulp and juice, depending on the order being run. A short blanch is used ahead of drying and freezing for the varieties that need it.

BlancherTray Dryer, Tray OvenIQF FreezerCrushing Machine
Packing & Dispatch

Graded fruit is packed into trays, punnets or films, then labelled and date coded. Packs are collated into cartons and palletised for transport.

Tray PackingFlow WrapPrinter (Cij, Inkjet, Co2 Laser, Hand)Master Carton PackingPalletizer
