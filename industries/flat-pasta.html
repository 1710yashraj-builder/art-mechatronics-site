Flat Noodle Processing Plant & Machinery Solutions

Turnkey plant · Machinery · Automation · India · UAE · Thailand

Flat noodles are wide, ribbon-shaped noodles slit from a rolled dough sheet rather than cut short at a die face. The dough is mixed and rested, sheeted down to the required thickness, slit into ribbons of even width, dried with controlled air, then cut and packed.

How it works

Complete Flat Noodle Process Flow

Dough Mixing & Resting

Sifted flour and water are dosed and mixed, then the dough is held so the gluten relaxes before sheeting. Resting is what allows a wide sheet to be rolled thin without tearing at the edges.

SifterWeighing & DosingPaddle MixerHopper
Sheeting to Thickness

The rested dough is formed into a continuous sheet and reduced in stages to the thickness the finished ribbon requires.

Extruder MachineFormingRoller Conveyor
Slitting into Ribbons

The sheet is slit lengthways into flat ribbons of even width, and the edge trim is returned to the dough system.

Roller CutterKnife CutterConveyor BeltScrew Conveyor
Drying

The ribbons are dried with controlled air and humidity so the wide flat section dries without curling.

Continuous Conveyor, Tunnel DryerAir Duct HeaterDehumidifierMoisture Analyzer
Cutting & Packing

Dried ribbons are cut to pack length, weighed into pouches and inspected before despatch.

Blade CutterMulti Head WeigherPremade PouchMetal Detector
