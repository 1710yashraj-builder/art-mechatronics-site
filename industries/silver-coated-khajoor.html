How it works

Complete Silver Coated Khajoor Process Flow

Raw Dates Cleaning & Preparation

Raw dates are cleaned to remove dust, foreign particles, and surface impurities.

Pre-CleanerAir Aspirator

➜ Ensures hygienic and clean raw material.

Grading & Size Sorting

Dates are graded based on size and quality to ensure uniform processing and premium output.

GraderColor Sorter

➜ Uniform size improves coating consistency and presentation.

Seed Removal (If Required)

In certain product variants, seeds are removed for better processing and consumer convenience.

Seed Removal
Drying / Moisture Control

Dates are conditioned or dried to achieve the ideal moisture level for coating and flavouring.

Batch Type - Hot Air Dryer

➜ Ensures better coating adhesion and shelf stability.

Flavouring & Infusion

Flavors, syrups, and aromatic additives are infused into the dates to enhance taste and variety.

Coating Pan Mixer

➜ Ensures uniform flavor distribution.

Silver Coating Process

Edible silver coating is applied to create a premium metallic finish and enhance product appeal.

Coating Pan Mixer

➜ Provides:

  • Premium appearance
  • Uniform coating
  • High visual value
Drying & Coating Fixation

The coated product is dried to stabilize the coating and improve durability.

Continuous Conveyor, Tunnel Dryer
Polishing & Final Finishing

The product is polished to enhance shine and improve final presentation.

Polisher
Final Inspection & Quality Control

The finished product is inspected to ensure uniform coating, size, and quality.

Vision Inspection
Packaging

Finished coated dates are packed into pouches, trays, or premium containers.

Pouch FillingJar PackingForm Fill Seal (Ffs)

➜ Suitable for retail and export markets.
