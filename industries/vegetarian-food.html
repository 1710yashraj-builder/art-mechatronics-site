Overview

About Vegetarian Food processing

The process requires strict hygiene, controlled processing conditions, dependable freezing and cooking technologies, and automated packaging systems to ensure food safety, shelf life and consistent quality. ART Mechatronics provides complete turnkey solutions for vegetarian food processing plants, offering systems for washing, peeling, cutting, mixing, blanching, cooking, freezing and packaging — engineered as dedicated, fully vegetarian production lines.

How it works

Complete Vegetarian Food Process Flow

Raw Material Intake & Handling

Fruits, vegetables, potatoes, pulses, grains and plant-based ingredients are received from farms and suppliers and fed into the line.

HopperConveyor BeltVibratory Feeder

➜ Ensures smooth and controlled material flow.

Cleaning, Washing & Peeling

Raw materials are cleaned and washed to remove soil, dust and surface impurities, then peeled where the product requires it.

WasherAir AspiratorPeeler

➜ Ensures:

  • Hygienic raw material
  • Removal of foreign matter
  • Food-safe input to the process
Sorting, Grading & Inspection

Discoloured, damaged or undersized material is separated so only sound produce continues into the process.

Color SorterGraderVision Inspection

➜ Ensures uniform, consistent input quality.

Cutting, Slicing & Dicing

Cleaned produce is cut into the shapes required by the finished product — fries, cubes, slices, shreds or chunks.

SlicerDicerKnife CutterShredder

➜ Produces:

  • Uniform piece size
  • Ready-to-process material
  • Product shapes suited to fries, frozen snacks and canned packs
Mixing, Blanching, Cooking & Frying

Depending on the product, material is mixed or batter-coated, blanched, cooked in kettles or fried before it moves forward.

Ribbon MixerPlanetary MixerBlancherCooking Kettle, Boiling TankFryer

➜ Supports:

  • Batter and coating preparation
  • Blanched frozen vegetables and fruits
  • Pickle, instant and ready-to-eat cooking
  • Plant-based and vegetarian snack products

➜ All contact surfaces are food-grade and dedicated to vegetarian production.

Freezing & Cooling (FOR FROZEN FOODS)

Processed product is frozen or cooled under controlled conditions before it reaches the packing line.

IQF FreezerBlast FreezerCooling ConveyorCold Storage/ Cold Room

➜ Ensures:

  • Free-flowing frozen pieces
  • Retained colour and texture
  • Stable product for storage and transport
Inspection & Packaging (HIGH-SPEED FMCG)

Finished product is weighed, inspected and packed into pouches, trays, jars or cartons as required.

Multi Head WeigherMetal DetectorForm Fill Seal (Ffs)Map Tray SealingCartoning

➜ Suitable for:

  • Frozen foods and frozen snacks
  • Ready-to-eat and instant food packs
  • Pickle and canned fruit and vegetable packs
  • Retail and bulk packaging
