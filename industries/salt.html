Salt Processing Plant & Machinery Solutions

Turnkey plant · Machinery · Automation · India · UAE · Thailand

Salt processing converts raw solar, rock or brine derived salt into clean, graded edible and industrial salt. The salt is washed to take out sand and insoluble matter, crushed and screened to crystal size, dried, dosed with iodine and free flow additives, then blended and packed.

How it works

Complete Salt Process Flow

Raw Salt Intake & Washing

Raw salt arrives carrying sand, clay and other insoluble matter, which is washed out with saturated brine. The washed salt is drained and carried forward for size reduction.

Bag Dump StationWasherDe-StonerScrew ConveyorBucket Elevator
Crushing & Sizing

Lumps are broken down and the salt is screened into the crystal sizes used for table, food grade and industrial salt. Oversize goes back to the crusher and the fines are drawn off separately.

CrusherRoller Mill MachineScreenerGraderVibratory Feeder
Drying & Cooling

Washed salt still holds surface moisture, which is driven off in a hot air dryer before any additive is applied. The salt is cooled after drying so that moisture does not settle back into the crystal bed.

Fluidized Bed Dryer – FBDHot Air GeneratorRotary Dryer, Drum DryerCooling Conveyor
Iodisation & Additive Dosing

Iodine solution and free flow or anti caking agents are dosed onto the dried salt and worked through the bed. Iodine is dosed steadily and worked through the whole batch rather than added at one point in the bed.

Weighing & DosingPlough Shear MixerRibbon MixerStirrer
Storage & Final Screening

Treated salt is held in silos and given a final screen before packing to remove lumps formed in storage. Salt draws moisture from the air, so storage and transfer are kept enclosed.

SiloDay BinSifterPneumatic ConveyorMagnetic Separator
Packing

Salt is filled into consumer pouches, jars or bulk sacks, then weighed, sealed and stitched or coded as the pack calls for. Retail packs are check weighed and inspected before they go into cartons.

Vertical Form Fill Seal (Vffs)Auger FillerSack PackingStitchingCheckweigher With Printer
