Coconut Oil Processing Plant & Machinery Solutions

Turnkey plant · Machinery · Automation · India · UAE · Thailand

Coconut oil is pressed from dried coconut kernel, or copra, and is used in cooking, food manufacturing, cosmetics and oleochemicals. Cleaned copra is sized, conditioned with heat, pressed in expellers, and the crude oil is settled and clarified before refining, deodorising and filling.

How it works

Complete Coconut Process Flow

Copra Cleaning & Sizing

Incoming copra is cleaned of dust, fibre, stones and tramp metal, then broken down to a uniform size for the press. Cleaning ahead of the mill protects the expeller worms from stones and metal carried in with the copra.

Pre-CleanerMagnetic SeparatorDe-StonerCrusherBucket Elevator
Conditioning & Cooking

Sized copra is heated and conditioned so that the oil cells are opened and the meal enters the press in a workable state. Conditioning is run on indirect steam heat from the plant boiler.

Cooking Kettle, Boiling TankSteam GeneratorScrew ConveyorBoiler
Expelling

Conditioned meal is pressed in screw expellers, which separate crude oil from the solid cake. Crude oil runs to a settling tank while the cake is carried off on a separate conveyor.

Expeller MachineScrew ConveyorStorage TankInclined Screw Conveyor
Settling & Clarification

Crude oil is settled and clarified so that fines and press sediment carried over from the expeller are removed. Recovered sediment is returned to the press feed rather than discarded.

SeparatorStorage TankJacketed TankAgitator
Refining & Deodorising

Where refined oil is being made, the clarified oil is neutralised, bleached and deodorised in heated, agitated vessels to give a bland, light-coloured oil. Virgin oil lines skip this stage, since virgin coconut oil is sold on its natural aroma.

Jacketed TankAgitatorHeat ExchangerThermic Fluid HeaterStorage Tank
Cake Handling

Press cake is cooled, broken down and milled into copra meal for animal feed, which is a saleable co-product of the mill. Meal is bagged and stitched on its own packing line.

Hammer Mill MachineCooling ConveyorBaggingSack PackingStitching
Filling & Packing

Finished oil is filled into pouches, bottles, jerry cans or drums according to the pack ordered. Filled packs are capped, labelled, coded and case packed for despatch.

Servo FillingBottle Packing (Spray, Pet, Glass)CappingJerry Can PackingLabelling
