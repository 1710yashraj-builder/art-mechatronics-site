Gnocchi Processing Plant & Machinery Solutions

Turnkey plant · Machinery · Automation · India · UAE · Thailand

Gnocchi processing turns potatoes and flour into soft, ridged dumplings rather than a dried pasta. Potatoes are washed, peeled, cooked and reduced to a smooth mass, blended with flour, formed and ridged, then cooled and either chilled or frozen for distribution.

How it works

Complete Gnocchi Process Flow

Potato Washing & Peeling

Incoming potatoes are washed, stones and soil are separated out, and the skin is removed. Gnocchi begins with a root crop, so the front of the line is a vegetable line rather than a flour line.

WasherDe-StonerPeelerRoller Conveyor
Cooking

Peeled potatoes are cooked through with steam or water until soft enough to break down.

Cooking Kettle, Boiling TankSteam GeneratorBlancher
Dough Preparation

The cooked potato is reduced to a smooth mass and blended with flour and the remaining recipe ingredients. The blend is worked only as far as needed, since over-working makes gnocchi rubbery.

GrinderSigma MixerWeighing & Dosing
Forming & Ridging

The soft dough is extruded, cut into short pieces and given the surface ridges that hold sauce.

Extruder MachineFormingKnife Cutter
Cooling & Freezing

Formed gnocchi is cooled and then frozen individually so the pieces stay separate, or held chilled for short shelf-life products. Unlike dried pasta, gnocchi carries moisture and depends on the cold chain.

Cooling TunnelIQF FreezerBlast FreezerCold Storage/ Cold Room
Tray Sealing & Packing

Product is filled into trays or pouches, sealed under a modified atmosphere where required, weighed and inspected.

MAP Tray SealingThermoforming SealingCheckweigher With PrinterMetal Detection
