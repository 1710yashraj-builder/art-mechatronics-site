How it works

Working Principle of Siever Machine

Material Feeding
Powders, granules, particles, or bulk materials are fed onto sieve mesh surface
Vibratory Screening Action
Vibratory motor generates controlled vibrationMaterial spreads evenly across screening surface

➜ Creates efficient sieving and separation action

Particle Size Classification
Fine particles pass through mesh openingsOversized particles remain above mesh
Continuous Material Movement
Vibratory motion keeps material flowing continuouslyPrevents clogging and improves throughput
Multi-Stage Sieving (OPTIONAL)
Multiple mesh decks allow separation into different particle sizes simultaneously
Product Discharge
Different grades of material discharged separately
