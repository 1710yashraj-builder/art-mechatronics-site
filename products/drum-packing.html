How it works

Working Principle of Drum Packaging Machine

  • 1. Empty Drum Feeding Empty drums are automatically positioned into filling station through conveyor or roller handling systems.
  • 2. Liquid Filling Process Machine automatically fills liquids into drums using precision weighing and dosing systems.

➜ Servo synchronization ensures accurate filling and smooth drum handling.

  • 3. Drum Filling Operation Machine handles: ✔ Lubricants ✔ Industrial oils ✔ Chemicals ✔ Solvents ✔ Paints ✔ Agrochemicals ✔ Liquid fertilizers

accurately and continuously.

  • 4. Drum Sealing & Capping Process Machine performs: Cap placement Bung tightening Leak-proof sealing Tamper-proof sealing Nitrogen flushing (optional)

for secure and transportation-safe packaging quality.

  • 5. Labeling & Inspection Integration Optional systems include: Batch coding Date printing QR code printing Leak testing systems Weight verification systems
  • 6. Finished Drum Discharge Finished drums discharged automatically for: Warehouse storage Palletizing Industrial shipment handling Export logistics
