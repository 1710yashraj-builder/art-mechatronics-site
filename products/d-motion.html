How it works

Working Principle of D Motion Packing Machine

Continuous Film Feeding

Laminated packaging film continuously moves through machine without intermittent stopping

Servo Motion Synchronization

Servo-driven synchronized motion systems control: ✔ Film movement ✔ Filling timing ✔ Sealing jaws ✔ Cutting mechanisms

➜ Continuous motion technology allows uninterrupted high-speed packaging operation.

Accurate Product Filling

Machine handles: ✔ Pan masala ✔ Tobacco ✔ Spices ✔ Snacks ✔ Granules ✔ Powders ✔ FMCG products smoothly and efficiently.

Continuous Sealing & Cutting

Sealing systems perform: Center sealing Side sealing Sachet sealing High-speed cutting with synchronized continuous motion.

PLC & HMI Automation Control

System controls: Packaging speed Servo synchronization Product flow Film tracking Sealing temperature

High-Speed Finished Pouch Discharge

Packed products discharged automatically for: Collection Cartoning Secondary packaging Dispatch operations
