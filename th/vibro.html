Vibro Machine for Thailand

The Vibro machine is a vibrating sieve that grades powders and granules by particle size, removes lumps and strains liquids through mesh screens. Thai lines use sieves of this type for tapioca starch, desiccated coconut, sugar, rice flour, seasoning blends, MSG and fish meal, and for straining coconut milk and sauces. Enquiries from Thailand go to the ART Mechatronics office in Pattaya.

Supplied to plants in Thailand from ART's Thailand Marketing Office in Pattaya City, with a service team in Chonburi; designed and built in Kanpur, India.

For Thai plants

Why Thai processors choose the Vibro Machine

  • In Thai humidity, starch, sugar and coconut powder can cake in storage; sieving before packing removes lumps and foreign matter.
  • Strains coconut milk and sauces for fibre and shell fragments; screens oversize out of dry seasoning, salt and MSG.
  • Sits under the Impact Pulverizer Machine or Ribbon Mixer outlet and ahead of the Auger Filler or Multi Head Weigher.
  • Mesh changes and motor servicing are done by ART's Chonburi team; the Pattaya office takes enquiries by phone or LINE.
