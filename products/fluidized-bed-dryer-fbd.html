Overview

About the Fluidized Bed Dryer (FBD)

In this process, material is suspended in an upward flow of hot air, creating a fluid-like state, which ensures excellent heat transfer, uniform drying, and precise moisture control.

FBD dryers are widely used in industries such as pharmaceuticals, food processing, chemicals, nutraceuticals, agro products, and minerals, where consistent product quality and controlled drying are critical.

How it works

Working Principle of Fluidized Bed Dryer

Material Loading
Wet powder or granules are loaded into the drying chamber
Hot Air Flow
Heated air is passed upward through the material bedAir velocity lifts and suspends particles
Fluidization
Material behaves like a fluidEach particle is surrounded by hot air

➜ Ensures uniform heat transfer

Moisture Evaporation
Moisture evaporates quicklyCarried away with exhaust air
Uniform Drying
All particles dry evenlyPrevents overheating or under-drying
Product Discharge
Dried material is dischargedReady for further processing
