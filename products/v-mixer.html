Overview

About the V Mixer

The machine features a uniquely designed V-shaped mixing chamber, where materials divide and merge continuously during rotation, creating an efficient three-dimensional tumbling mixing action that ensures:

  • Homogeneous blending
  • Excellent batch uniformity
  • Minimal product degradation
  • Low heat generation
  • Gentle handling of sensitive materials

V mixers are widely used in industries such as:

• Pharmaceuticals

• Nutraceuticals

• Food processing

• Chemicals

• Cosmetics

• Agro products

• Fine powder industries

where precision blending, contamination-free processing, and high mixing accuracy are essential.

The machine is especially suitable for:

  • Pharmaceutical powders
  • Nutraceutical blends
  • Food premixes
  • Spice mixing
  • Chemical powder blending
  • Heat-sensitive and fragile materials

How it works

Working Principle of V Mixer Machine

Material Loading
Powders, granules, crystals, or dry materials are loaded into V-shaped chamber
Rotational Tumbling Action
V-shaped vessel rotates at controlled speedMaterials continuously divide and merge during rotation

➜ Creates efficient three-dimensional blending action

Gravity-Based Mixing
Material flows naturally due to gravity inside chamberEnsures homogeneous mixing throughout batch
Gentle Low-Shear Blending
Minimal friction and heat generationPrevents damage to sensitive materials
Uniform Product Formation
Produces highly consistent and contamination-free blends
Product Discharge
Finished blended material discharged through outlet valve
