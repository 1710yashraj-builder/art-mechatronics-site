Aqua Feed Processing Plant & Machinery Solutions

Turnkey plant · Machinery · Automation · India · UAE · Thailand

Aqua feed production makes sinking pellets for shrimp, prawn and other farmed aquatic species. Ingredients such as fishmeal, soya and wheat flour are ground to a fine powder, batched, mixed with binders, steam-conditioned and pressed into small-diameter pellets that must hold together in water.

How it works

Complete Aqua Feed Process Flow

Ingredient Intake & Cleaning

Fishmeal, soya, squid meal and wheat flour are tipped from sacks and screened for string, husk and metal. Because aquaculture feed is fed into open water, foreign matter is removed before it can reach the mill.

Bag Dump StationAir Screen CleanerMagnetic SeparatorScrew Conveyor
Fine Grinding & Sieving

Aqua feed is ground far finer than livestock feed, because a shrimp takes small bites and the pellet must not shed particles into the pond. The ground powder is sifted and coarse fractions are sent back for regrinding.

Micro Pulverizer MachineAir Classifier Mill MachinePlan SifterDust Collector Machine
Batching & Premixing

Vitamins, minerals and binder are weighed into a premix, then combined with the bulk ingredients for the batch. The binder is what allows the finished pellet to stay intact once it is submerged.

Weighing & DosingHopperRibbon MixerMixer Automation System
Steam Conditioning & Pelleting

The mix is conditioned with steam so the starch gelatinises and binds the pellet. Conditioned mash is pressed into small-diameter sinking pellets sized for the species and growth stage.

Steam GeneratorExtruder MachineGranulator
Drying & Cooling

Moisture picked up during conditioning is driven off so the pellets store without spoiling. Dried pellets are cooled to ambient temperature and moisture is checked before coating.

Fluidized Bed Dryer – FBDCooling ConveyorMoisture Analyzer
Vacuum Oil Coating

Fish oil, attractants and heat-sensitive additives are applied after pelleting rather than before, so they are not damaged by the conditioning heat. Vacuum coating draws the oil into the pellet instead of leaving it on the surface, where it would wash off in water.

Coating Pan MixerVacuumStorage TankPaddle Mixer
Grading & Packing

Pellets are graded into size bands, since feed is matched to the size of the stock being fed. Graded feed is checked for metal, filled and closed for despatch.

GraderForm Fill Seal (Ffs)Metal DetectorStitching
