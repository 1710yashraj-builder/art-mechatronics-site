How it works

Working Principle of Strip Packaging Machine

  • 1. Film Roll Feeding Packaging film roll is loaded into feeding section.
  • 2. Strip Forming Process Machine automatically forms strip cavities using synchronized film feeding systems.

➜ Servo synchronization ensures smooth film movement and accurate strip formation.

  • 3. Product Feeding & Filling Operation Machine handles: ✔ Tablets ✔ Capsules ✔ Powders ✔ Nutraceutical products ✔ Healthcare products

accurately and continuously.

  • 4. Strip Sealing Process Machine performs: Heat sealing Airtight sealing Leak-proof sealing Moisture-protected sealing

for hygienic and premium packaging quality.

  • 5. Cutting & Coding Integration Optional systems include: Batch coding Date printing QR code printing Automatic strip cutting Inspection systems
  • 6. Finished Strip Discharge Finished strips discharged automatically for: Cartoning Retail display Secondary packaging Dispatch operations
