About the X Ray Inspection

Unlike conventional metal detectors, X-ray inspection systems can detect:

  • Metal contaminants
  • Glass particles
  • Stones
  • Bones
  • Dense plastics
  • Rubber fragments
  • Product defects
  • Missing product pieces

inside packaged or unpackaged products.

How it works

Working Principle of X-Ray Inspection Machine

Product Feeding

Products move through X-ray inspection tunnel using conveyor system

X-Ray Imaging Generation

X-ray generator emits controlled X-ray beam through product

➜ Different materials absorb X-rays differently based on density

Digital Image Detection

High-resolution sensors capture internal image of product Machine detects:

  • Metal contaminants
  • Glass particles
  • Stones
  • Bones
  • Missing products
  • Product defects
AI-Based Image Analysis

Intelligent software analyzes product image in real time Detects abnormalities and contamination instantly

Automatic Rejection System

Contaminated or defective products are removed automatically using:

  • Air reject system
  • Pusher reject mechanism
  • Diverter system
  • Drop reject system
Clean Product Discharge

Approved products continue safely toward packaging or dispatch line
