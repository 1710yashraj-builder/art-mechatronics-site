Paddle Blender / Paddle Mixer Machine for Thailand

A Paddle Mixer blends powders, granules, flakes and semi-moist material with paddles on a horizontal shaft, lifting and folding the batch rather than shearing it. Thai seasoning and MSG blenders, animal and aqua feed mills, tapioca and rice flour plants and desiccated coconut lines use this type of mixer. ART Mechatronics builds it in Kanpur and supports Thai plants from its Pattaya marketing office.

Why Thai processors choose the Paddle Blender / Paddle Mixer Machine

  • Fish meal, rice bran and coconut residue pick up moisture in Thai humidity; paddle action keeps damp batches moving without balling.
  • Thai feed premixes and seasoning blends often take oil or molasses mid-cycle; a paddle mixer handles wetted powder.
  • It sits between ART's Hammer Mill Machine and the Auger Filler or Multi Head Weigher on a packed-powder line.
  • ART's Chonburi service team supplies replacement paddles and seals and attends commissioning in Thailand.
