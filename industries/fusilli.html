Fusilli Processing Plant & Machinery Solutions

Turnkey plant · Machinery · Automation · India · UAE · Thailand

Fusilli is a spiral pasta made from durum semolina, formed by extruding dough through a die that gives the strand its twist before it is cut short. The cut spirals are shaken loose, dried slowly to avoid cracking, cooled and packed.

How it works

Complete Fusilli Process Flow

Raw Material Preparation

Semolina is tipped, screened for foreign matter and conveyed to the mixing station under dust control.

Bag Dump StationVibroMagnetic SeparatorPneumatic Conveyor
Mixing & Kneading

Semolina and water are combined and kneaded into a plastic dough suitable for a shaped die. Recipe dosing is held to a fixed ratio so the spiral pitch stays consistent through the run.

Paddle MixerWeighing & DosingPLC Automation Control Panel
Spiral Forming & Cutting

The dough is extruded through a spiral-forming die and cut into short pieces, then separated so the freshly cut spirals do not stick to one another.

Extruder MachineCutter With SifterVibratory Feeder
Slow Drying & Stabilisation

Fusilli is spread on trays and dried slowly, then held so moisture equalises across the twisted section. The thick and thin parts of a spiral dry at different rates, which is why the hold stage matters.

Tray Dryer, Tray OvenTrolleyAir Duct Heater
Cooling & Packaging

Cooled fusilli is weighed, wrapped and inspected before secondary packing.

Cooling ConveyorMulti Head WeigherHorizontal Form Fill Seal (Hffs)X Ray InspectionMaster Carton Packing
