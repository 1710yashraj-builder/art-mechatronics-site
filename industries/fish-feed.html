Fish Feed Processing Plant & Machinery Solutions

Turnkey plant · Machinery · Automation · India · UAE · Thailand

Fish feed production makes floating extruded pellets for carp, tilapia, catfish and other farmed fish. Fishmeal, soya, rice bran and vitamin premixes are ground fine, batched and mixed, then cooked under steam and pressure in an extruder so the pellet expands and floats.

How it works

Complete Animal Fish Feed Process Flow

Raw Material Intake & Screening

Fishmeal, soya, rice bran and maize are tipped, screened and freed of tramp metal. Material is fed forward at a steady rate so the mill runs evenly.

Bag Dump StationVibratory FeederMagnetic SeparatorBucket Elevator
Fine Grinding

Ingredients are ground to a fine, uniform powder, because coarse particles stop the pellet expanding evenly in the extruder. The powder is sieved and the mill runs under dust extraction.

Pin Mill MachineImpact Pulverizer MachineSieverDust Extraction System
Batching & Mixing

The powder is weighed to formula with vitamin and mineral premixes and blended dry. Moisture in the mix is checked before extrusion, as it governs how the pellet expands.

Weighing & DosingConical Screw MixerRibbon MixerMoisture Analyzer
Extrusion & Cutting

The mix is cooked with steam under pressure and forced through a die; the sudden drop in pressure expands the pellet and traps air inside it, which is what makes the pellet float. A cutter at the die face sizes the pellet for the species being fed.

Extruder MachineBoilerBlade CutterVibratory Conveyor
Drying & Cooling

Expanded pellets carry a lot of moisture and are dried until they will store without moulding. They are then cooled so that the oil applied next is absorbed rather than shed.

Rotary Dryer, Drum DryerThermic Fluid HeaterCooling ConveyorBlower Fan
Oil Coating & Sizing

Fish oil and heat-sensitive additives are sprayed onto the cooled pellets after extrusion. Coated pellets are screened into size grades for fry, fingerling and grow-out feeding.

Coating Pan MixerStorage TankScreener
Inspection & Packing

Bags are filled, coded with batch details and inspected before despatch. Filled bags are stacked for storage and transport.

Premade BagPrinter (Cij, Inkjet, Co2 Laser, Hand)Metal DetectorPalletizer & De Palletizer Robot
