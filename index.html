How We Work

From Understanding Your Requirement to Long-Term Partnership

At ART Mechatronics, every project follows a structured engineering and execution process designed to deliver the right technical solution, consistent quality, reliable performance and long-term value. From the first technical discussion to commissioning and ongoing support, our teams work closely with the client at every stage.

01 / 16
Step 01

Understand the Requirement

We begin by understanding your process, production requirements, challenges, material characteristics, capacity, site conditions and desired outcome. Our objective is to identify the actual technical requirement and develop the right solution for your application.

01 We Listen Before We Engineer.
Step 02

Technical Evaluation & Planning

Our experienced engineers and technical team study the requirement in detail and evaluate process parameters, equipment configuration, capacity, material flow, utilities, automation and site constraints. Different engineering approaches are considered before finalising the most suitable direction.

02 Engineering the Right Approach.
Step 03

R&D & Material Testing

Where required, our R&D team conducts practical material trials in our in-house laboratory using small-scale test equipment and models. Different machine configurations and process parameters can be tested to validate the most effective solution for the specific material and application.

03 Test. Analyse. Optimise. Deliver with Confidence.
Step 04

Customisation & Solution Configuration

Our equipment can be customised to meet specific process and plant requirements. From complete SS construction and SS contact parts to customised dimensions, preferred component brands, automation, instrumentation, controls and other special requirements, we engineer the equipment around your needs.

04 Built Around Your Requirement — Not the Other Way Around.
Step 05

Techno-Commercial Proposal

Once the technical approach is established, we prepare a detailed techno-commercial proposal covering equipment specifications, configuration, scope of supply, utilities, commercial terms, delivery requirements and other relevant project parameters.

05 A Clear Solution with a Clear Scope.
Step 06

Technical Finalisation & Approval

Our technical and commercial teams work closely with the client to review specifications, capacities, materials, automation, accessories and other requirements. Necessary amendments are incorporated until the final solution is mutually agreed and approved.

06 Every Detail Aligned Before Production Begins.
Step 07

3D Drawing & Site Layout

Detailed engineering drawings, 3D models and equipment layouts are prepared for approval. Where required, our team also studies the client’s site, available space, material flow, existing equipment and utility connections to integrate the solution accurately into the plant layout.

07 Visualise It. Fit It. Approve It.
Step 08

In-House Manufacturing

Once the design is approved, the equipment moves into production through our in-house manufacturing process. Skilled fabricators, technicians, engineers and assembly teams work together to maintain dimensional accuracy, workmanship and equipment reliability.

08 Engineered. Fabricated. Assembled Under Our Control.
Step 09

Quality Control & Inspection

Materials, components and major assemblies are checked against approved specifications. Our engineering and quality teams inspect fabrication, dimensions, assembly, electrical systems, safety provisions and other critical parameters before the equipment proceeds to final testing.

09 Quality Checked at Every Critical Stage.
Step 10

Factory Testing & Material Trials

Where applicable, the completed equipment undergoes functional testing and material trials at our facility. Actual material is tested through the equipment where practical to verify process flow, operating parameters and performance before shipment.

10 Performance Is Proven Before Dispatch.
Step 11

Final Approval, Packaging & Dispatch

Following final inspection and approval, clients can review the completed equipment where applicable before dispatch. Equipment and critical components are professionally prepared for transportation with appropriate transit protection, documentation, logistics arrangements and insurance as required.

11 Protected, Documented & Ready for Delivery.
Step 12

Site Installation & Commissioning

Our service engineers support the installation, assembly and commissioning of the equipment at the customer’s facility. Connections, positioning, integration and operating parameters are checked to ensure proper functioning within the production environment.

12 From Our Factory to Your Production Floor.
Step 13

Production Trial & Client Handover

Multiple trial runs are conducted using the client’s material to verify equipment performance under actual production conditions. Once satisfactory performance is achieved and approved, the equipment is formally handed over along with relevant technical documentation.

13 Proven Under Actual Operating Conditions.
Step 14

Operator Training & Technical Support

Our engineers provide practical training to operators and maintenance personnel covering operation, controls, safety, cleaning, routine maintenance and basic troubleshooting. Our technical support team remains available for operational assistance whenever required.

14 Empowering Your Team for Confident Operation.
Step 15

Spares, Service & Maintenance

We provide support through genuine or approved spare parts, wear components, consumables, servicing, preventive maintenance and AMC solutions. Our objective is to help customers maintain reliable equipment performance and minimise unnecessary downtime.

15 Keeping Your Equipment Performing for the Long Run.
Step 16

Continuous Support & Long-Term Partnership

Our relationship does not end with delivery. We continue supporting customers with optimisation, upgrades, automation, capacity expansion, new equipment and future engineering requirements — building a dependable partnership for the long term.

16 We Build Relationships, Not Just Machines.
