How it works

Working Principle of Vertical Form Fill Seal

PACKING MACHINE

Roll Film Feeding

Laminated packaging film roll is mounted onto film unwinding system

Vertical Pouch Forming Process

Film passes through forming collar and tube to create vertical pouch shape

➜ Machine continuously forms pouches automatically from roll-fed laminate film.

Product Filling Operation

Machine fills: ✔ Powders ✔ Granules ✔ Pan masala ✔ Tobacco ✔ Supari ✔ Spices ✔ Snacks ✔ Dry fruits accurately into formed pouches.

Sealing & Cutting Process

Vertical and horizontal sealing jaws seal pouch securely: Center seal Pillow pouch Gusset pouch Side seal pouch Automatic cutting system separates finished packets.

Coding & Printing Integration

Optional systems include: Batch coding Date printing QR code printing Barcode printing

Finished Pouch Discharge

Packed pouches discharged automatically to: Rotary collecting table Conveyor systems Cartoning systems Secondary packaging operations
