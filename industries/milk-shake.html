Milk Shake Processing Plant & Machinery Solutions

Turnkey plant · Machinery · Automation · India · UAE · Thailand

Milk shake is a flavoured dairy drink made by blending milk with sugar, stabilisers and flavouring, then homogenising the mix, heat treating it, cooling it and filling it into bottles, pouches or cans.

How it works

Complete Milk Shake Process Flow

Milk reception and chilled storage

Incoming milk is received, chilled and held in agitated tanks so it does not separate before the batch is called.

Storage TankChillerCooling TowerAgitator
Dosing and blending

Sugar, stabiliser, cocoa or fruit preparation and flavour are weighed out and blended into the milk in a jacketed vessel until they are fully in solution.

Weighing & DosingHigh Shear MixerJacketed TankStirrer
Homogenising and heat treatment

The blended mix is homogenised so the fat is broken down and stays dispersed, then heat treated and cooled through a heat exchanger. Pasteurised and sterilised routes both start here, depending on the shelf life the product is sold on.

HomogenizerColloid MillPasteurizerHeat ExchangerSteam Generator
Filling and sealing

The treated shake is filled into bottles, cups, pouches or cans and closed. Containers are rinsed and the fill is sealed or seamed straight after.

Servo Nozzle Tracking FillingBottle Packing (Spray, Pet, Glass)Cup FillerCappingSeaming
Coding, checking and cold despatch

Packs are date coded, checked for seal integrity, trayed and moved into cold store for despatch.

Printer (Cij, Inkjet, Co2 Laser, Hand)Leak TesterTray PackingCold Storage/ Cold Room

Machinery

Product-Wise Process & Machinery

🥛 1. MILK (PACKAGED MILK) Process: Pasteurization → Homogenization → Cooling → Packaging Machines:

  • Pasteurizer
  • Homogenizer
  • Chilling System
  • Milk Storage Tank
  • Pouch Packing Machine / Bottle Filling Machine

➜ Output: Standardized milk (full cream / toned / skimmed)

🍶 2. CURD / YOGURT Process: Pasteurization → Cooling → Culture Addition → Incubation → Cooling → Packaging Machines:

  • Pasteurizer
  • Fermentation Tank
  • Incubation Chamber
  • Cooling System
  • Cup Filling Machine

➜ Output: Set curd / stirred yogurt

🧈 3. BUTTER Process: Cream Separation → Cream Pasteurization → Churning → Washing → Packaging Machines:

  • Cream Separator
  • Cream Pasteurizer
  • Butter Churner
  • Butter Working Machine
  • Butter Packing Machine

🧈 4. GHEE Process: Butter / Cream → Heating → Clarification → Filtration → Packaging Machines:

  • Ghee Boiler
  • Clarifier
  • Filtration Unit
  • Storage Tank
  • Filling Machine

🧀 5. PANEER Process: Milk Heating → Coagulation → Curd Formation → Pressing → Cutting → Cooling → Packaging Machines:

  • Paneer Vat
  • Coagulation Tank
  • Paneer Press
  • Cutting Machine
  • Cooling Tank
  • Vacuum Packing Machine

🧀 6. CHEESE Process: Milk Standardization → Pasteurization → Culture Addition → Coagulation → Cutting → Whey Removal → Pressing → Ripening → Packaging Machines:

  • Cheese Vat
  • Curd Cutter
  • Whey Drain System
  • Cheese Press
  • Ripening Chamber
  • Cheese Cutting Machine
  • Packaging Machine

🥤 7. MILK SHAKE Process: Milk → Mixing with flavor → Homogenization → Pasteurization → Cooling → Packaging Machines:

  • Mixing Tank
  • Homogenizer
  • Pasteurizer
  • Bottle Filling Machine

🥛 8. BUTTERMILK Process: Milk / Curd → Dilution → Mixing → Homogenization → Pasteurization → Packaging Machines:

  • Mixing Tank
  • Homogenizer
  • Pasteurizer
  • Pouch Packing Machine

🥛 9. LASSI Process: Curd → Mixing with water/sugar → Homogenization → Cooling → Packaging Machines:

  • Mixing Tank
  • Homogenizer
  • Chilling System
  • Bottle / Cup Filling Machine

🥛 10. MILK POWDER Process: Milk → Evaporation → Concentration → Spray Drying → Cooling → Packaging Machines:

  • Multi Effect Evaporator
  • Spray Dryer
  • Cyclone Separator
  • Fluid Bed Dryer
  • Powder Packing Machine

➜ Output:

  • Skim Milk Powder
  • Whole Milk Powder
