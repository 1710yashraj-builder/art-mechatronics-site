How it works

Working Principle of Container Overwrapping Machine

  • 1. Container Feeding Containers are automatically fed into overwrapping section through synchronized conveyor systems.
  • 2. Film Feeding & Positioning Process Machine automatically dispenses wrapping film and positions it around containers before sealing operation.

➜ Servo synchronization ensures accurate wrapping alignment and smooth container handling.

  • 3. Overwrapping Operation Machine handles: ✔ PET bottles ✔ Glass jars ✔ Jugs ✔ Jerry cans ✔ Canisters ✔ Drums ✔ Gallons ✔ Industrial containers

accurately and continuously.

  • 4. Film Wrapping & Sealing Process Machine performs: Protective film wrapping Heat sealing Fold wrapping Tamper-proof overwrapping Shrink wrapping integration

for premium packaging appearance and secure product protection.

  • 5. Inspection & Automation Integration Optional systems include: Batch coding Date printing Vision inspection systems Film alignment detection Reject systems
  • 6. Finished Container Discharge Wrapped containers discharged automatically for: Cartoning Case packing Warehouse storage Retail distribution Export shipment handling
