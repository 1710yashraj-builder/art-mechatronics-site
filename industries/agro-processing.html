Raw Material Intake & Handling

Agricultural produce is received from farms and fed into the system.

Intake HopperConveyor SystemsBucket Elevators

Cleaning

Raw materials are cleaned to remove dust, husk, stones, and impurities.

Pre CleanerDestonerAir ClassifierMagnetic Separator

  • Product purity
  • Protection of machinery
Grading & Sorting

Materials are sorted based on size, weight, and quality.

Grading MachineVibro SifterColor Sorter

➜ Ensures uniform and premium output.

Washing (FOR FRUITS & VEGETABLES)

Produce is washed to remove dirt and contaminants.

Washing System
Peeling / Cutting / Size Reduction

Raw materials are processed into desired sizes.

Cutting MachineSlicing MachineCrusher / Grinder

➜ Prepares material for further processing.

Drying (FOR SHELF LIFE)

Moisture is reduced to improve storage stability.

Dryer (Hot Air / Fluid Bed / Tray Dryer)

➜ Ensures:

  • Longer shelf life
  • Reduced spoilage
Processing (PRODUCT-SPECIFIC)

Further processing based on product type.

➜ Examples:

  • Milling (grains → flour)
  • Crushing (seeds → oil extraction prep)
  • Roasting (nuts, seeds)
  • Mixing / blending (spices, snacks)
