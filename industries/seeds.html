  • Oil yield
  • Processing efficiency
Cracking / Size Reduction

Seeds are cracked or broken to prepare for further processing.

Cracker / Crusher

Conditioning

Conditioning SystemCooker / Heating Unit

  • Proper softening

Flaking (OPTIONAL FOR OIL INDUSTRY)
