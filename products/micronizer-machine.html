Overview

About the Micronizer Machine

Unlike conventional grinding machines, micronizers use high-velocity air streams or fluid energy to achieve grinding without mechanical contact, resulting in extremely fine, contamination-free, and uniform particle size distribution.

Micronizers are widely used in industries such as pharmaceuticals, chemicals, pigments, minerals, cosmetics, food additives, and advanced materials, where precision and purity are critical.
