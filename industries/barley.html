Barley Processing Plant & Machinery Solutions

Turnkey plant · Machinery · Automation · India · UAE · Thailand

Barley processing turns field barley into pearl barley, flakes, flour and malting-grade grain. The crop is cleaned and graded, then pearled so the husk and outer bran layers are abraded away, or steeped and germinated where malt is the product. Pearled and flaked barley is dried, sized and packed.

How it works

Complete Barley Process Flow

Intake & Cleaning

Barley is tipped into a receiving hopper, fed forward at a steady rate and cleaned of straw, awns, chaff and dust on screens and aspiration. Magnetic separation removes ferrous material carried in from the field.

HopperVibratory FeederAir Screen CleanerMagnetic SeparatorZ Type Elevator
Sizing & De-Stoning

Grain is graded into size fractions and de-stoned, since both pearling and malting are carried out on an even kernel. Thin and broken corns are separated out for feed.

GraderDe-StonerGravity SeparatorScreener
Pearling & Bran Removal

Abrasive pearling removes the husk and successive bran layers from the kernel to give pot barley and pearl barley. The bran dust released is captured at the machine.

ScourerPolisherDehuskingDust Extraction SystemCyclone Separator
Steeping, Germination & Kilning

Malting is a separate route rather than a stage after pearling: cleaned, graded barley is steeped in water, allowed to germinate in a conditioned bed, and then kilned dry. Conditioned, cooled air is passed through the germination bed to hold it steady.

Jacketed TankAir Handling UnitChillerBatch Type - Hot Air DryerHot Air Generator
Flaking & Milling

Pearled barley is steamed and rolled into flakes, or milled and sifted into barley flour and grits.

Steam GeneratorRoller Mill MachinePlan SifterGrinding Mill Machine
Weighing & Packing

Pearl barley, flakes and flour are weighed and filled into bags or sacks, stitched, coded and checked.
