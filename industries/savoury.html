How it works

Complete Namkeen Process Flow

Raw Material Handling

Ingredients such as flour, pulses, spices, oils, and additives are received and transferred into the system.

Material Handling SystemsStorage Bins

➜ Ensures organized and continuous production flow.

Dough Preparation / Mixing (CORE PROCESS)

Ingredients are mixed to form dough suitable for extrusion or shaping.

Dough MixerPaddle Mixer

➜ Ensures:

  • Uniform dough consistency
  • Proper hydration
  • Consistent product texture
Extrusion / Forming

Dough is shaped into different snack forms such as sev, bhujia, or other extruded shapes.

ExtruderNamkeen Making Machine

➜ Enables:

  • Multiple product shapes
  • Continuous production
Frying (CRITICAL STAGE)

The shaped product is fried in oil under controlled temperature.

Continuous FryerBatch Fryer

➜ Ensures:

  • Crispy texture
  • Uniform cooking
  • Consistent color
De-Oiling

Excess oil is removed to improve product quality and shelf life.

De-Oiling MachineCentrifugal System
Mixing & Blending (FOR MIXTURE PRODUCTS)

Different fried components are mixed to create namkeen mixtures.

Ribbon BlenderMixing Drum

➜ Ensures uniform distribution of ingredients.

Seasoning & Flavor Coating

Spices and seasoning are applied to enhance taste.

Seasoning DrumCoating System

➜ Ensures consistent flavor across batches.

Cooling

The product is cooled before packaging to maintain quality.

Cooling Conveyor
Quality Inspection

Final inspection ensures product consistency and quality.

Inspection Conveyor
