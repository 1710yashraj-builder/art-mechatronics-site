Mouth Freshener (Mukhwas) Processing Plant & Machinery Solutions

Turnkey plant · Machinery · Automation · India · UAE · Thailand

Mouth fresheners, commonly known as mukhwas, are widely consumed FMCG products made using a blend of seeds, sugar-coated ingredients, herbs, and flavoring agents. These products are known for their refreshing taste, aroma, and digestive benefits.

How it works

Process Flow

Raw Material Handling & Storage

Ingredients such as fennel seeds, coriander seeds, sesame seeds, sugar balls, and herbal additives are received and stored.

Material Handling SystemsStorage Bins
Seed Cleaning & Grading

Raw seeds are cleaned to remove dust, stones, and impurities and graded based on size.

Seed CleanerDestonerVibratory Grader

➜ Ensures uniformity and hygiene.

Sugar Coating / Candy Processing

Certain ingredients are coated with sugar or flavored syrups to enhance taste and appearance.

Coating Drum / Coating PanSyrup Preparation System

➜ Creates attractive, colorful, and sweet components.

Drying / Conditioning

Coated materials are dried to stabilize moisture and improve shelf life.

Tray Dryer / Continuous Dryer
Blending & Mixing

All ingredients are mixed in precise proportions to achieve consistent taste and texture.

Ribbon BlenderPaddle Mixer

➜ Ensures homogeneous mixing.

Flavoring & Perfume Application

Flavors, menthol, and aromatic agents are applied for enhanced sensory appeal.

Spray SystemCoating Drum
Final Sieving & Quality Control

Final screening removes fine particles and ensures consistent product quality.

Vibro Sifter
Dust Collection & Hygiene Control

Dust generated during mixing and handling is controlled using advanced systems. Systems Used:

Dust CollectorCyclone Separator
