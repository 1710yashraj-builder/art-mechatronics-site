How it works

Complete Coconut Process Flow

Raw Coconut Handling

Raw coconuts are received and fed into the processing line.

Material Handling SystemsFeeding Systems

➜ Ensures smooth and continuous flow.

Dehusking (CRITICAL STEP)

Outer fibrous husk is removed from the coconut.

Coconut Dehusking Machine

➜ Ensures:

  • Efficient husk removal
  • Reduced manual labor
Shell Breaking / Cutting

Hard shell is broken to extract coconut halves.

Coconut Shelling MachineCutting Machine

➜ Ensures clean and efficient separation.

Paring (BROWN SKIN REMOVAL)

The brown testa layer is removed to obtain white coconut kernel.

Coconut Paring Machine

➜ Ensures clean, white kernel suitable for food-grade applications.

Washing & Cleaning (FOOD-GRADE STEP)

Coconut kernels are washed to remove impurities and contaminants.

Washing SystemCleaning Equipment

➜ Ensures hygiene and product safety.

Cutting / Sizing (OPTIONAL)

Kernels may be cut into required sizes or shapes.

Coconut Cutting Machine
Drying / Moisture Control (VERY IMPORTANT)

Coconut is dried to achieve desired moisture level for storage and transportation.

Tray DryerHot Air DryerContinuous Drying System

➜ Ensures:

  • Longer shelf life
  • Prevention of spoilage
Grading & Sorting

Processed coconut is graded based on size and quality.

Grading MachineVibratory Sifter

➜ Ensures uniform bulk supply.

Quality Inspection

Final inspection ensures consistency and premium quality.

Inspection Conveyor
Bulk Packaging (WHOLESALE SUPPLY)

Processed coconut is packed into large bags for supply to wholesalers and exporters.

Weighing SystemBag Filling Machine
