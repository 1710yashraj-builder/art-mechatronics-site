How it works

Working Principle of Gallon Packaging Machine

  • 1. Empty Gallon Feeding Empty gallon containers are automatically fed into packaging line through synchronized conveyor systems.
  • 2. Rinsing & Cleaning Process Machine automatically rinses and sanitizes gallon containers before filling operation.

➜ Servo synchronization ensures hygienic container handling and smooth processing.

  • 3. Gallon Filling Operation Machine handles: ✔ Drinking water ✔ Beverages ✔ Edible oils ✔ Chemicals ✔ Cleaning liquids ✔ Industrial liquids ✔ Liquid food products

accurately and continuously.

  • 4. Capping & Sealing Process Machine performs: Cap placement Screw capping Leak-proof sealing Tamper-proof sealing Neck sealing operations

for hygienic and transportation-safe packaging quality.

  • 5. Labeling & Inspection Integration Optional systems include: Wraparound labeling Batch coding Date printing QR code printing Leak testing systems
  • 6. Finished Gallon Discharge Finished containers discharged automatically for: Warehouse storage Retail distribution Bulk shipment handling Export logistics
