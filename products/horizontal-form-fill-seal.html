A Horizontal Form Fill Seal (HFFS) Packing Machine, also known as a Horizontal Packaging Machine, Flow Wrap Machine, Horizontal Flow Wrapper, or HFFS Packaging Machine, is an advanced automatic packaging system designed for horizontal pouch formation, product feeding, wrapping, filling, sealing, cutting, and high-speed packaging operations using roll-fed packaging film.

How it works

Working Principle of Horizontal Form Fill Seal

PACKING MACHINE

Product Feeding

Products are fed horizontally through conveyor or automatic feeding system

Film Feeding & Horizontal Wrapping

Packaging film unwinds from roll and wraps product horizontally

➜ Machine continuously forms packaging around moving products automatically.

Product Positioning & Filling

Machine handles:

  • Biscuits
  • Chocolates
  • Soaps
  • Cakes
  • Noodles
  • Snack bars
  • FMCG products smoothly and accurately.
Sealing & Cutting Process

Horizontal sealing jaws perform: End sealing Center sealing Pillow pack sealing Automatic cutting system separates finished packs.

Coding & Printing Integration

Optional systems include: Batch coding Date printing QR code printing Barcode printing

Finished Product Discharge

Finished packs discharged automatically to: Conveyor systems Rotary collecting tables Cartoning systems Secondary packaging lines
