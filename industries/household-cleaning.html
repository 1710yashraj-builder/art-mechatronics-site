Household Cleaning Processing Plant & Machinery Solutions

Turnkey plant · Machinery · Automation · India · UAE · Thailand

Household cleaning products cover soap and detergent, fabric softener, dishwash liquid, floor cleaner and toilet cleaner. Powder lines blend and dry the solid ingredients; liquid lines dose, mix and thicken in tanks before filling.

How it works

Complete Household Cleaning Process Flow

Raw material intake and dosing

Powders, salts and builders are tipped or drawn from silo and weighed out, and liquid raws are metered from tank. Material is moved between floors in closed conveying.

Bag Dump StationSiloWeighing & DosingScrew ConveyorPneumatic Conveyor
Powder blending — the detergent branch

Dry ingredients are blended to the recipe and sifted so lumps and oversize do not carry through.

Ribbon MixerPlough Shear MixerConical Screw MixerSifter
Slurry drying and granulation

Detergent slurry is atomised and dried into hollow granules, or the blend is granulated and dried in a bed, then the fines are recovered.

Spray DryerFluidized Bed Dryer – FBDRapid Mixer Granulator - RMGCyclone Dust Collector Machine
Liquid mixing — the softener and cleaner branch

Fabric softener, dishwash liquid, floor cleaner and toilet cleaner are made up in jacketed vessels, sheared until the surfactants are in solution, then held in tank ahead of filling.

Jacketed TankHigh Shear MixerAgitatorStorage Tank
Filling, capping and sealing

Liquid is filled into bottles, jerry cans or gallon packs and closed; powder is filled by auger into pouches and sacks.

Servo FillingPiston FillingBottle Packing (Spray, Pet, Glass)Jerry Can PackingCapping
Labelling, coding and case packing

Filled packs are labelled, batch coded, cartoned and cased for despatch.

Wrap Around LabellingBatch CodingLoad CartonerRobotic Case Packer
Dust and fume control

Powder handling, drying and mixing points are kept under extraction, and the exhaust is scrubbed before it leaves the plant.

Dust Extraction SystemPulse Jet Dust Collector MachineWet ScrubberAir Pollution Control Equipment
