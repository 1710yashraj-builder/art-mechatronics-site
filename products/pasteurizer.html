Overview

About the Pasteurizer Machine

Pasteurization is essential for ensuring food safety, extending shelf life, and maintaining nutritional value, making it a key process in industries such as dairy, beverage, food processing, and nutraceutical manufacturing.

These machines are designed for precise temperature control, uniform heating, and hygienic operation, ensuring consistent product quality and compliance with food safety standards.

How it works

Working Principle of Pasteurizer Machine

Product Feeding
Liquid product (milk, juice, etc.) is fed into system
Heating Stage (CORE PROCESS)
Product is heated to specific temperatureUsing heat exchanger / steam / electric heating
Holding Time
Product is held at temperature for required durationEnsures microbial destruction
Cooling Stage
Product is rapidly cooledMaintains quality and taste
Controlled Processing
Temperature and time are precisely controlled
Output
Pasteurized product ready for packaging
