Snuff Processing Plant & Machinery Solutions

Turnkey plant · Machinery · Automation · India · UAE · Thailand

Snuff is a dry powdered tobacco made by ageing and drying cured leaf, then grinding it to a fine powder and sieving it to a consistent particle size. The powder is scented, allowed to mature and filled into tins, sachets or small containers.

How it works

Complete Snuff Process Flow

Leaf Conditioning & Ageing

Cured leaf is opened out, de-stemmed and held to age, which is what develops the character of snuff. Batches move through the ageing area in bins and trolleys.

Bag Dump StationStem RemovalAir Screen CleanerDay BinTrolley
Drying

Aged leaf is dried down before grinding, since snuff is a dry powder and damp leaf will not mill cleanly. Moisture is checked on the batch before it is released to the mill.

Batch Type - Hot Air DryerRotary Dryer, Drum DryerHot Air GeneratorMoisture Analyzer
Fine Grinding

Dried leaf is ground to a fine powder, the defining step for snuff. Grinding is enclosed and vented because the powder is very light.

Micro Pulverizer MachineImpact Pulverizer MachinePulverizer With Dust CollectorStone Mill Machine
Sieving & Grading

The ground powder is sieved and classified so the fineness stays consistent from batch to batch. Oversize is returned to the mill.

Plan SifterSieverAir ClassifierVibroScreener
Scenting & Maturation

Scents and flavour oils are blended through the powder, and the blend is then left to mature. Blending is kept gentle so the powder is not compacted.

Ribbon MixerDouble Cone MixerHanda MixerDay Bin
Filling & Packing

Matured snuff is dosed into tins, sachets or small containers, sealed and coded. Filled packs pass metal detection before they are cartoned.

Servo Auger FillingTin Box PackingSachetBatch CodingMetal Detector
Dust Control Across the Line

Snuff is handled as a fine powder throughout, so extraction points are taken at the mill, sifter, mixer and filler. Collected dust is returned or disposed of according to the plant's own procedure.

Centralised Dust Collection SystemPulse Jet Dust Collector MachineCyclone Dust Collector MachineDe-Dusting System
