How it works

Working Principle of Zip Lock / Zipper Pouch Packaging Machine

  • 1. Premade Pouch Feeding Empty zipper pouches are loaded into pouch magazine section.
  • 2. Pouch Pick-Up & Opening Process Automatic system picks and opens zipper pouch accurately.

➜ Servo synchronization ensures smooth pouch handling and positioning.

  • 3. Product Filling Operation Machine handles: ✔ Powders ✔ Granules ✔ Liquids ✔ Paste products ✔ Snacks ✔ Dry fruits ✔ Pan masala products

accurately and continuously.

  • 4. Zipper Sealing & Airtight Packaging Process Machine performs: Heat sealing Zipper sealing Airtight sealing Leak-proof sealing Nitrogen flushing (optional)

for hygienic and premium packaging quality.

  • 5. Coding & Inspection Integration Optional systems include: Batch coding Date printing QR code printing Metal detection Check weighing
  • 6. Finished Pouch Discharge Finished pouches discharged automatically for: Cartoning Retail display Secondary packaging Dispatch operations
