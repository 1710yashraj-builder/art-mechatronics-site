How it works

Working Principle of Retort Pouch Packing Machine

Retort Pouch Feeding

Empty retort pouches are manually or automatically fed into machine

Product Filling Process

Machine fills products accurately into retort pouches using:

  • Liquid filling systems
  • Gravy filling systems
  • Paste dosing systems
  • Solid product feeding systems

➜ Servo-controlled synchronization ensures accurate filling and hygienic operation.

Airtight Sealing Operation

Machine performs:

  • Heat sealing
  • Airtight sealing
  • Leak-proof sealing
  • Heat-resistant sealing for safe retort processing applications.
Sterilization Compatibility

Packed retort pouches are suitable for: Retort sterilization High-temperature processing Pressure cooking sterilization Long shelf-life storage

PLC & Automation Control

System controls: Filling accuracy Sealing temperature Pouch synchronization Packaging speed Production cycle

Finished Pouch Discharge

Finished retort pouches discharged automatically for: Sterilization processing Cartoning Export packaging Dispatch operations
