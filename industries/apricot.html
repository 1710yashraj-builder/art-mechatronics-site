Apricot Processing Plant & Machinery Solutions

Turnkey plant · Machinery · Automation · India · UAE · Thailand

Apricot processing turns fresh apricots into dried halves and diced fruit. Fruit is washed and graded, split along the seam and the stone removed, treated so the flesh holds its orange colour, then dried in warm air, sorted and sized; part of the crop is diced or sliced instead of being packed as whole halves.

How it works

Complete Apricot Process Flow

Washing & Grading

Fruit is washed to remove orchard dust and spray residue and graded by size, with soft and damaged fruit sorted out. Even-sized fruit dries at the same rate, which is why grading is done before the fruit is cut.

WasherRinsingGraderColor SorterLoading & Unloading Conveyor
Halving & Stone Removal

Apricots are split along the natural seam and the stone is pushed out, leaving the two halves. Stones are collected separately because apricot kernel is a saleable by-product rather than waste.

SplitterSeed RemovalConveyor BeltVibratory Conveyor
Colour Treatment

The halves are treated before drying so the fruit keeps its orange colour; untreated apricot dries to a dark brown. The treatment chamber is enclosed and vented, with extraction handling the fumes.

SterilizationFume Extraction SystemTrolleyAir Handling Unit
Drying

Halves are dried cut side up in warm air until the flesh is leathery and the moisture is stable for storage. Air flow is kept even across the load so the trays dry to the same condition.

Tray Dryer, Tray OvenContinuous Conveyor, Tunnel DryerBatch Type - Hot Air DryerHot Air GeneratorMoisture Analyzer
Sorting & Sizing

Dried halves are colour sorted and graded into trade sizes, with broken and darkened fruit diverted. Camera inspection removes stone fragments and stalk that survived the earlier stages.

Color SorterVision InspectionGraderScreener
Cutting & Packing

Part of the crop is diced or sliced for bakery, cereal and confectionery buyers and the rest is packed as whole halves. Packs are weighed, metal checked and sealed before casing.

DicerSlicerMulti Head WeigherMetal DetectorStand Up Pouch
