How it works

Complete Corn / Maize Process Flow

Raw Maize Handling & Storage

Maize grains are received, stored, and fed into the processing system.

Material Handling SystemsStorage Silos

➜ Ensures efficient bulk handling.

Cleaning & Pre-Processing

Maize is cleaned to remove dust, stones, and foreign materials.

Pre CleanerDestonerMagnetic SeparatorAir Classifier

➜ Ensures:

  • Product purity
  • Protection of downstream equipment
Conditioning (MOISTURE CONTROL)

Maize is conditioned to prepare it for degermination and milling.

Conditioning SystemDampening Unit

➜ Ensures efficient processing and better yield.

Degermination

Germ is separated from the maize kernel.

Degerminator

➜ Produces:

  • Germ (used for oil extraction)
  • Endosperm (used for flour)

➜ Improves:

  • Flour quality
  • Shelf life
Milling / Grinding

Maize is ground into flour or grits.

Roller MillPulverizerGrinding Machine

➜ Produces:

  • Corn flour
  • Maize flour
  • Grits
Sieving & Separation

Ground product is separated into different grades.

PlansifterVibro Sifter

➜ Ensures:

  • Uniform particle size
  • Multiple product outputs
Blending (OPTIONAL)

Different grades may be blended as per requirement.

Ribbon Blender
Dust Collection & Air Handling

Fine dust generated during milling is controlled. Systems Used:

Dust CollectorCyclone SeparatorCentralized Dust Extraction System

➜ Ensures:

  • Clean working environment
  • Product recovery
Quality Inspection

Final inspection ensures consistent product quality.

Inspection Systems
Packaging (BULK & RETAIL)

Finished maize flour is packed into bags or retail packs.

Bag Filling MachineWeighing SystemForm Fill Seal (FFS) Machine

➜ Suitable for:

  • Bulk supply
  • Retail distribution
