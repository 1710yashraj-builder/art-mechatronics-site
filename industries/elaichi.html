Elaichi (Cardamom) Processing Plant & Machinery Solutions

Turnkey plant · Machinery · Automation · India · UAE · Thailand

Elaichi, or green cardamom, is processed into a mouth freshener by cleaning and grading the dried capsules, separating the seeds from the husk, and coating those seeds with sugar syrup before drying, blending and packing.

How it works

Process Flow

Intake & Pre-Cleaning

Dried cardamom capsules arriving from the curing house are lifted into the line and screened to remove dust, stalk pieces, stones and tramp metal. Cleaning at intake protects the grading, dehusking and coating machines further down the line.

Bucket ElevatorPre-CleanerAir Screen CleanerDe-StonerMagnetic Separator
Grading & Colour Sorting

Cleaned capsules are graded by size and sorted by colour, since the bold green capsules are held back for whole-spice sale and the smaller or discoloured lots are routed to the mouth freshener line. Light and immature capsules are pulled out here as well, and keeping the seed size uniform matters for even coating later.

Vibratory FeederGraderColor SorterGravity Separator
Dehusking & Seed Separation

The capsule is cracked open and the inner seeds are separated from the light outer husk, which is drawn off by air. Husk fragments and fines released here are captured by the extraction system so the working area stays clean.

DehuskingShell RemovalWinnowerAir AspiratorDust Extraction System
Sugar Syrup Preparation & Coating

Sugar syrup is prepared in a heated vessel and applied to the tumbling cardamom seeds in a coating pan, building up the coated layer in successive passes. Silver leaf, colour or menthol is worked in at this stage for the mishri-elaichi and silver-coated varieties.

Cooking Kettle, Boiling TankJacketed TankCoating Pan MixerHanda Mixer
Drying & Conditioning

Coated seeds are dried to set the sugar shell and bring the moisture down, then cooled before they are handled again. Cardamom loses its aroma if it is overheated, so drying is kept gentle and the room is held dry.

Fluidized Bed Dryer – FBDTray Dryer, Tray OvenCooling ConveyorDehumidifierMoisture Analyzer
Blending & Final Sieving

Coated elaichi is blended with the other mukhwas ingredients and given a final screening to take out broken coating, fines and any loose husk. Dust drawn off at the blender and sifter is collected rather than released into the hall.

Ribbon MixerDrum MixerSifterCyclone Dust Collector Machine
Weighing, Packing & Inspection

The finished product is weighed out and filled into sachets, pouches or jars, then checked for weight and for metal contamination before dispatch. Sachet and pouch formats are used for retail counters, while jars serve bulk and catering supply.
