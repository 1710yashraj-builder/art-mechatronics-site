How it works

Complete Cocoa Process Flow

Raw Cocoa Bean Handling & Cleaning

Cocoa beans are received and cleaned to remove dust, stones, and impurities.

Material Handling SystemsPre CleanerDestonerMagnetic Separator

➜ Ensures clean raw material input.

Roasting

Cocoa beans are roasted to develop flavor and aroma.

Cocoa Roaster

➜ Ensures:

  • Flavor development
  • Moisture reduction
Cooling

Roasted beans are cooled before further processing.

Cooling System
Winnowing (Shell Removal)

Shells are removed to obtain cocoa nibs.

Winnowing Machine

➜ Produces clean cocoa nibs.

Grinding

Cocoa nibs are ground into cocoa liquor.

Cocoa Grinder / Ball Mill

➜ Produces:

  • Cocoa liquor (paste form)
Pressing

Cocoa liquor is pressed to separate cocoa butter and cocoa cake.

Hydraulic Cocoa Press

➜ Produces:

  • Cocoa butter
  • Cocoa cake
Pulverizing (POWDER FORMATION)

Cocoa cake is ground into fine powder.

Pulverizer / Pin Mill

➜ Produces:

  • Cocoa powder
Alkalization (OPTIONAL – DUTCH PROCESS)

Cocoa powder is treated to modify color and taste.

➜ Produces:

  • Dark cocoa powder
  • Smooth flavor
