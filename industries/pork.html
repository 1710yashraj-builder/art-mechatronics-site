Pork Processing Plant & Machinery Solutions

Turnkey plant · Machinery · Automation · India · UAE · Thailand

Pork processing breaks chilled carcasses into primal cuts and converts trimmings into bacon, ham, sausage and other cured products. Pork is chilled, cut and boned, cured or brined, minced and mixed, filled into casings or moulds, cooked, cooled, sliced and packed.

How it works

Complete Pork Process Flow

Chilling & Primal Cutting

Chilled sides are broken into shoulder, loin, belly and leg on the cutting line. Belly and loin go to bacon and ham lines, while trim is collected for sausage and emulsion products.

Cold Storage/ Cold RoomChillerBlade CutterChain Conveyor
Brine Preparation & Curing

Curing brine of salt, curing salt, sugar and phosphate is mixed in a jacketed tank and dosed to the cuts. Cured cuts are held chilled so the brine distributes evenly through the muscle before cooking.

Jacketed TankStirrerWeighing & DosingStorage Tank
Mincing & Emulsifying

Trim is minced, then chopped or milled with ice, salt and spice into a sausage batter or fine emulsion. The mix is blended to a uniform batter so that fat and lean are evenly spread through the product.

GrinderChopperColloid MillPlough Shear Mixer
Filling & Forming

Batter is filled into casings for sausage or pressed into moulds for ham and luncheon meat. Filled product is racked onto trolleys ready for the cook and smoke stage.

Piston FillingFormingExtruder MachineTrolley
Cooking, Smoking & Cooling

Product is cooked with steam or hot air, smoked where the recipe calls for it, then cooled. Cooking sets the protein and colour, and cooling before slicing keeps the cut face clean.

Cooking Kettle, Boiling TankSteam GeneratorHot Air GeneratorCooling Tunnel
Slicing & Vacuum Packing

Chilled ham, bacon and sausage are sliced and laid into vacuum or thermoform packs. Seals are leak tested and packs are labelled before chilled dispatch.

SlicerVacuumThermoforming SealingLeak TesterLabelling
