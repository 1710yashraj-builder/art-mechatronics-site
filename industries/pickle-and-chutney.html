About Pickle & Chutney processing

The process requires efficient raw material preparation, precise cutting, controlled mixing with spices and oils, and hygienic packaging to ensure consistent taste, preservation, and product quality.

ART Mechatronics provides complete turnkey solutions for pickle and chutney manufacturing plants, offering advanced systems for cleaning, cutting, mixing, blending, maturation, and packaging.
