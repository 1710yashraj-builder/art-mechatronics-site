How it works

Working Principle of Shell Removal Machine

  • 1. Material Feeding & Loading Raw nuts, seeds, pulses, or agro materials are automatically fed into shelling chamber for processing operation.
  • 2. Shell Cracking & Separation Process Machine automatically cracks shells and separates husks using rollers, impact systems, vibration mechanisms, and airflow separation technology.

➜ Intelligent synchronization ensures smooth product handling and accurate shell removal operations.

  • 3. Shelling & Kernel Extraction Operation Machine handles processing of: ✔ Groundnuts ✔ Almonds ✔ Walnuts ✔ Sunflower seeds ✔ Pulses ✔ Coconut products ✔ Agro materials ✔ Industrial food products

accurately and continuously.

  • 4. Material Cleaning & Classification Process Machine performs: Shell removal Kernel extraction Husk separation Impurity removal Product grading Material cleaning

for efficient industrial agro and food processing operations.

  • 5. Intelligent Automation & Hygiene Integration Optional systems include: Dust extraction systems Air separation systems Automatic feeding systems Food-grade stainless steel construction Safety guarding systems
  • 6. Finished Product Discharge Processed kernels and cleaned materials discharged automatically for: Packaging operations Oil extraction systems Roasting operations Warehouse storage Export shipment handling
