Hookah Tobacco Processing Plant & Machinery Solutions

Turnkey plant · Machinery · Automation · India · UAE · Thailand

Hookah tobacco, also called moassel or shisha, is made by cutting cleaned tobacco leaf into fine strands and blending it with molasses, glycerine and flavouring until the mixture is uniformly moist. The blended product is matured and then filled into tins, jars or pouches.

How it works

Complete Raw Hookah Process Flow

Leaf Intake & Cleaning

Baled or bagged tobacco leaf is opened and fed to the line, and stones, metal and loose trash are taken out before cutting. Stems are separated so that only the lamina goes forward to the cutter.

Bag Dump StationAir Screen CleanerDe-StonerMagnetic SeparatorStem Removal
Cutting & Shredding

The cleaned leaf is cut into fine strands, which is the form hookah tobacco is sold in. Oversize pieces are returned for re-cutting through the sifter.

Shredder MachineCutter With SifterKnife CutterVibratory Feeder
Washing & Conditioning

Many hookah blends are made from washed leaf, so the cut tobacco is rinsed and then brought back to working moisture. Steam and jacketed vessels are used to condition the leaf before it meets the syrup.

WasherRinsingJacketed TankSteam Generator
Syrup Preparation & Blending

Molasses, glycerine and flavouring are prepared as a syrup and then mixed through the cut leaf until the strands are evenly coated. Mixing continues until colour and moisture read the same across the batch.

Cooking Kettle, Boiling TankRibbon MixerPlough Shear MixerAgitatorStorage Tank
Maturation & Holding

The blended tobacco is held so the syrup soaks right through the strands before packing. Batches move in bins and trolleys, and are kept cool where the recipe calls for it.

Day BinTrolleyCold Storage/ Cold RoomHvac System
Filling, Sealing & Packing

Matured product is filled into tins, jars or pouches, sealed and coded. Sealed units are collated into cartons for despatch.

FillingTin Box PackingSeamingJar PackingPremade PouchBatch CodingMaster Carton Packing
