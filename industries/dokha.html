Dokha Tobacco Manufacturing Plant & Machinery Solutions

Turnkey plant · Machinery · Automation · India · UAE · Thailand

How it works

Complete Dokha Tobacco Process Flow

Raw Tobacco Receiving & Handling

Tobacco leaves are received from suppliers and transferred into the processing system.

Conveyor SystemsMaterial Handling Equipment

➜ Ensures smooth and efficient material movement.

Cleaning & Dust Removal

Tobacco is cleaned to remove dust, sand, and unwanted impurities.

Pre CleanerAir ClassifierCyclone Separator

➜ Improves product purity and protects machinery.

Conditioning & Moisture Control

Tobacco is conditioned to achieve the ideal moisture level required for fine cutting.

Conditioning ChamberHumidification System

➜ Ensures flexibility and prevents material breakage.

Fine Grinding / Cutting

Tobacco is processed into very fine particles suitable for dokha use.

Fine Cutting MachinePulverizer / Grinder

➜ Ensures:

  • Uniform fine texture
  • Consistent product strength
  • Smooth usability
Blending (If Required)

Different tobacco types may be blended to achieve desired strength and characteristics.

Ribbon BlenderPaddle Mixer
Drying / Moisture Stabilization

The processed tobacco is stabilized to maintain consistent moisture and shelf life.

Tray Dryer / Controlled Drying System
Dust Collection & Air Handling

Dust CollectorCyclone SeparatorCentralized Dust Extraction System

➜ Ensures:

  • Safe working environment
  • Product recovery
  • Compliance with industrial safety standards
Screening & Quality Control

Final screening ensures uniform particle size and consistent quality.

Vibro SifterScreening System

Pouch Packing MachineJar Filling MachineWeighing System
