A Pneumatic Packing Machine, also known as an Air Operated Packaging Machine, Pneumatic Pouch Packing Machine, or Pneumatic Filling & Sealing Machine, is an industrial packaging system designed for automatic filling, pouch handling, sealing, cutting, dosing, and packaging operations using compressed air-powered pneumatic mechanisms.

How it works

Working Principle of Pneumatic Packing Machine

Product Feeding

Powders, granules, snacks, spices, or products are fed into machine through:

  • Cup filler systems
  • Auger fillers
  • Multihead weighers
  • Conveyors
  • Vibratory feeders
Pneumatic Operation Activation

Compressed air powers pneumatic cylinders and actuators

➜ Pneumatic systems control pouch movement, sealing jaws, cutting, and filling synchronization automatically.

Accurate Product Filling

Machine handles:

  • Powders
  • Granules
  • Pan masala
  • Tobacco
  • Spices
  • Snacks
  • FMCG products efficiently and continuously.
Pouch Sealing & Cutting

Pneumatic sealing jaws perform: Heat sealing Center sealing Side sealing Pouch cutting with synchronized precision.

PLC & Sensor Automation Control

System controls: Filling timing Pneumatic synchronization Packaging speed Sealing temperature Product flow

Continuous Packaging Line Operation

Packed products discharged automatically for: Collection Cartoning Secondary packaging Dispatch operations
