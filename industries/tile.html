Tile Manufacturing Plant & Machinery Solutions

Turnkey plant · Machinery · Automation · India · UAE · Thailand

Tile manufacturing presses prepared ceramic powder into green tiles, dries them, applies glaze and printed decoration, and fires them in a kiln. Fired tiles are polished or rectified where the finish calls for it, sorted by shade and size, then boxed and palletised.

How it works

Complete Tile Process Flow

Powder Storage & Ageing

Spray-dried powder is held in silos so that moisture spreads evenly through it before pressing. Feeders draw powder from the silos to the press hoppers.

SiloDay BinScrew ConveyorVibratory Feeder
Pressing & Drying

Powder is pressed in a die to form the green tile, which still holds moisture and has little strength. The green tile is dried before any glaze is applied to it.

Hydraulic Press / Baling PressFormingContinuous Conveyor, Tunnel Dryer
Glazing & Decoration

Engobe and glaze are applied to the dried tile and the pattern is printed onto the surface. Glaze is kept stirred and screened so that it stays uniform along the line.

Printer (Cij, Inkjet, Co2 Laser, Hand)AgitatorSieverRoller Conveyor
Kiln Firing & Cooling

Decorated tiles pass through the kiln, where the body vitrifies and the glaze fuses, then through a cooling section. Waste heat from the cooling zone is taken back to the dryers.

Furnace BurnerHot Air GeneratorCooling ConveyorHeat Recovery Unit - HRU
Polishing & Rectification

Vitrified tiles are polished and squared on the edges where the finish calls for it. The tile is washed after grinding so that no slurry dries on the face.

PolisherSurface GrinderBlade CutterWasher
Shade Sorting & Inspection

Fired tiles vary in tone and in size, so they are sorted into shade and calibre groups and checked for surface faults. Rejects are taken off the line before packing.

Color SorterVision InspectionSorting RobotGrader
Boxing & Palletising

Tiles of one shade and calibre are boxed together, coded, and stacked on pallets. Pallets are wrapped and labelled for the warehouse.

Master Carton PackingBatch CodingPalletizerStretch WrappingLabelling
