How it works

Working Principle of Pouch Sealing Machine

Pouch Feeding

Filled pouches are manually or automatically fed into sealing section

Pouch Positioning & Transfer

Conveyor or guide system positions pouch accurately for sealing operation

➜ Machine synchronizes pouch movement with sealing system for consistent seal quality.

Heat Sealing Operation

Machine seals: ✔ Laminated pouches ✔ Stand-up pouches ✔ Zipper pouches ✔ Pillow pouches ✔ Center seal pouches ✔ Sachets with strong and hygienic sealing quality.

Temperature Controlled Sealing

Precision temperature systems maintain: Uniform sealing temperature Seal strength consistency Leak-proof packaging quality

Coding & Printing Integration

Optional systems include: Batch coding Date printing QR code printing Barcode printing

Finished Pouch Discharge

Sealed pouches discharged automatically for: Collection Cartoning Secondary packaging Dispatch operations
