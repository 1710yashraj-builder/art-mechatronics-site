Turnkey plant · Machinery · Automation · India · UAE · Thailand

Meat processing converts chilled carcasses and primal cuts into deboned portions, mince, marinated cuts and cooked products. Meat is chilled, broken down into cuts, trimmed, minced or diced, blended with brine and seasoning, then cooked or frozen and packed.

How it works

Complete Meat Process Flow

Chilling & Carcass Handling

Carcasses and primals are held chilled and moved into the deboning hall on overhead and floor conveying. Keeping the meat cold from intake onward limits microbial growth and makes cutting cleaner.

Chain ConveyorChillerCold Storage/ Cold RoomAir Handling Unit
Cutting & Deboning

Primals are broken down into cuts, boned out and trimmed of fat and connective tissue. Trim is separated by grade so that lean and fat streams can be blended to a set recipe later.

Blade CutterKnife CutterTrolleyConveyor Belt
Mincing & Dicing

Trim and selected cuts are minced through plates or diced to the cube size the product calls for. Particle size decides the texture of the finished mince, kebab, patty or curry cut.

GrinderDicerChopperScrew Conveyor
Mixing & Marination

Minced or diced meat is blended with salt, brine, spice and binders in a mixer, often under vacuum. Vacuum mixing helps the brine take up into the meat and reduces air pockets in the batch.

Paddle MixerRibbon MixerWeighing & DosingVacuum
Cooking & Cooling

Cooked lines are heated in steam-jacketed kettles or cook tanks, then brought down in temperature on a cooling line. Rapid cooling after cooking keeps the product out of the range where spoilage organisms grow quickly.

Cooking Kettle, Boiling TankSteam GeneratorCooling TunnelCooling Conveyor
Freezing, Inspection & Packing

Portions are frozen where required, then vacuum or thermoform packed, checked and cased. Metal detection and checkweighing take place before cartoning so that only correct packs are dispatched.
