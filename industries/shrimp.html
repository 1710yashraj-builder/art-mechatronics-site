Shrimp Processing Plant & Machinery Solutions

Turnkey plant · Machinery · Automation · India · UAE · Thailand

Shrimp processing turns iced raw shrimp into peeled, graded and frozen product in raw or cooked form. Shrimp are washed, de-headed, peeled and deveined, graded by count size, cooked or left raw, then frozen as individual pieces or blocks and packed for cold storage.

How it works

Complete Shrimp Process Flow

Receiving, Washing & Chilling

Iced shrimp are tipped, washed and held in chilled water while they wait for the peeling line. Keeping the catch cold and clean from the moment it lands protects both texture and shelf life.

Loading & Unloading ConveyorWasherRinsingChiller
De-heading, Peeling & Deveining

Heads and shells are removed and the vein is stripped to give headless, peeled and deveined product. Shell and head waste is taken away on a separate line so it does not mix with the edible stream.

Shell RemovalPeelerCleat ConveyorScrew Conveyor
Count Grading & Sorting

Shrimp are graded into count sizes and sorted so that each pack holds pieces of one grade. Count size is what the buyer orders against, so grading decides where the batch is sold.

GraderVibratory ConveyorVision InspectionSorting Robot
Blanching or Cooking

Cooked lines are blanched or cooked in brine, then chilled straight after. Raw lines skip this stage and go directly from grading to the freezer.

BlancherCooking Kettle, Boiling TankSteam GeneratorCooling Tunnel
IQF or Block Freezing & Glazing

Shrimp are frozen as separate pieces for IQF packs or frozen in blocks for bulk trade. A water glaze is applied over frozen pieces to protect them during cold storage.

IQF FreezerSpiral ConveyorBlast FreezerTrolleyCold Storage/ Cold Room
Weighing, Inspection & Export Packing

Graded frozen shrimp are weighed into consumer packs or cartons and inspected for foreign matter. Cartons are palletised for container loading to export buyers.

Multi Head WeigherMetal DetectionX Ray InspectionMaster Carton PackingPalletizer
