Dehumidifier Machine for Thailand

A Dehumidifier pulls water vapour out of room or process air so powders, dried goods and packaging stay dry. In Thailand it is used in snack, rice cracker, seasoning, sugar, tapioca starch, desiccated coconut and pharmaceutical plants where humid air makes powders cake, snacks soften and cold-room stock sweat. ART Mechatronics supports Thai plants from its South-East Asia marketing office in Pattaya.

Why Thai processors choose the Dehumidifier Machine

  • Seasoning powders, sugar, MSG and tapioca starch cake in Thailand's humid months; dry air in the packing area keeps them free-flowing.
  • Seaweed snacks, rice crackers and desiccated coconut soften in humid air between the dryer and the Vertical Form Fill Seal (Vffs).
  • Stock leaving ART's Cold Storage/ Cold Room sweats in monsoon air; a Dehumidifier and Air Handling Unit keep the ante-room dry.
  • ART's service team in Chonburi, on the Eastern Seaboard industrial belt, handles after-sales support for Thai plants.
