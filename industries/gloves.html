Rubber Gloves Manufacturing Plant & Machinery Solutions

Turnkey plant · Machinery · Automation · India · UAE · Thailand

Rubber glove manufacturing dips cleaned ceramic formers into coagulant and then into compounded latex, and the deposited film is leached, beaded, vulcanised and surface-treated before the gloves are stripped, dried and leak-tested.

How it works

Complete Gloves Process Flow

Latex Compounding & Storage

Curing chemicals and dispersions are blended into the latex and the compound is held under gentle agitation until it is drawn to the line. Steady agitation keeps the dispersion uniform so the deposited film stays even.

Storage TankJacketed TankAgitatorHomogenizerWeighing & Dosing
Former Cleaning & Coagulant Dipping

Ceramic formers are washed, rinsed and dried before entering the coagulant tank, which prepares the surface for latex pick-up. Any residue left on a former shows as a defect in the finished glove.

WasherRinsingHot Air GeneratorChain ConveyorPLC Automation Control Panel
Latex Dipping, Leaching & Vulcanising

Formers pass through the latex tank to build up the film, then through leaching tanks that wash out water-soluble residues, and finally through the curing ovens. Oven and tank heating is carried by the plant hot water, steam and air handling systems.

Continuous Conveyor, Tunnel DryerHot Water GeneratorHot Air GeneratorBoilerAir Handling Unit
Surface Treatment, Stripping & Drying

Gloves are chlorinated or polymer-coated so they can be donned easily, then stripped from the formers and tumble-dried. Chlorination exhaust is scrubbed before release and the working area is kept under extraction.

WasherWet ScrubberFume Extraction SystemBatch Type - Hot Air DryerBlower Fan
Leak Testing & Inspection

Gloves are tested for pinholes and checked visually for tears, thin patches and beading faults. Failures are removed from the flow before counting begins.

Leak TesterLeak DetectorVision InspectionAuto Reject SystemChain Conveyor
Counting, Boxing & Despatch

Gloves are counted into dispenser boxes by size, coded, collated into outers and wrapped for despatch.

Count & StackMono Carton PackingBatch CodingMaster Carton PackingStretch Wrapping
