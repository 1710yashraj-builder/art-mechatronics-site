Turmeric Processing Plant & Machinery Solutions

Turnkey plant · Machinery · Automation · India · UAE · Thailand

Turmeric processing converts freshly harvested rhizomes into dried fingers, polished bulbs and fine turmeric powder. The rhizomes are washed, boiled or cured, dried, and polished to remove the wrinkled outer skin, then graded and ground.

How it works

Complete Turmeric Process Flow

Washing & Sorting

Harvested rhizomes are fed from a hopper and washed to remove field soil, loose roots and stones. Damaged fingers and foreign matter are taken out before the material goes for curing.

HopperWasherDe-StonerInclined Conveyor Belt
Boiling & Curing

Cleaned rhizomes are boiled or steamed until they soften, which gelatinises the starch and spreads the colour evenly through the finger. Curing also shortens the drying time and gives the cooked aroma associated with turmeric.

Cooking Kettle, Boiling TankSteam GeneratorBoiler
Drying

Cured rhizomes are dried in warm air until the moisture is low enough for storage and milling. Even drying prevents case hardening, where the outside sets hard while the core stays wet.

Continuous Conveyor, Tunnel DryerTray Dryer, Tray OvenHot Air GeneratorMoisture Analyzer
Polishing & Grading

Dried fingers are polished so the rough outer skin is rubbed away and the smooth, bright surface buyers look for is exposed. Polished turmeric is then graded by size and separated by colour.

PolisherScourerGraderColor Sorter
Grinding & Sieving

Polished fingers are milled into turmeric powder, with the mill kept under extraction because the powder is fine and strongly staining. Sieving returns oversize particles to the mill so the finished mesh stays consistent.

Impact Pulverizer MachinePulverizer With Dust CollectorSifterMagnetic Separator
Packing

Powder is filled into consumer pouches or bulk bags, coded and passed through metal detection before dispatch.

Vertical Form Fill Seal (Vffs)Auger FillerMetal DetectorBatch Coding
