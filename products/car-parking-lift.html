How it works

Working Principle of Car Parking Lift / Car Stacker Machine

Vehicle Positioning

Vehicle is parked onto lift platform or parking tray

Lift System Activation

Hydraulic or electro mechanical system activates lifting mechanism

➜ Platform lifts vehicle vertically to create additional parking space below or above.

Vertical Vehicle Stacking

Machine handles:

  • Cars
  • SUVs
  • Luxury vehicles
  • Commercial vehicles
  • Sports cars safely and efficiently.
Safety Locking Mechanism

Automatic safety locks ensure: Stable vehicle holding Safe parking operation Load security Controlled lifting movement

Space Optimization

Multiple vehicles can be parked vertically within limited parking area

Vehicle Retrieval

Lift lowers platform smoothly for easy vehicle retrieval whenever required
