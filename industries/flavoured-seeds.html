How it works

Complete Flavoured Seeds Process Flow

Seed Intake & Pre-Cleaning

Raw seeds are received in bags or in bulk, tipped into the intake system and freed of straw, husk, string and oversized foreign matter.

Bag Dump StationHopperVibratory FeederPre-CleanerBucket Elevator

➜ Ensures a steady, controlled feed to the cleaning line.

Cleaning, De-Stoning & Grading

Seeds are cleaned of dust and light impurities, separated from stones and mud balls, checked for metal contamination and graded by size. Where the seed is washed, it is surface-dried before entering the roaster.

Air Screen CleanerAir AspiratorDe-StonerGravity SeparatorMagnetic SeparatorGraderWasherBatch Type - Hot Air Dryer

➜ Ensures:

  • Stone-, dust- and metal-free feed
  • Uniform seed size for even roasting
  • Protection of downstream machinery
Roasting

Cleaned seeds are roasted under controlled heat to develop crunch, colour and aroma.

RoasterHot Air GeneratorScrew Conveyor

➜ Delivers:

  • Uniform roasting across the batch
  • Consistent colour and texture
  • Controlled moisture reduction
Cooling

Roasted seeds are cooled in a controlled manner so that residual heat does not affect the coating stage or the finished pack.

Cooling ConveyorBlower Fan

➜ Prevents condensation inside packs.

Seasoning & Coating

Salt, spice blends, oil or sweet coatings are metered in and spread evenly over the roasted seeds.

Coating Pan MixerDrum MixerWeighing & Dosing

➜ Supports:

  • Salted and spiced variants
  • Oil- and slurry-coated variants
  • Uniform flavour pick-up
Blending & Batch Mixing

Seasoned seeds are blended into a uniform batch, and multi-seed or trail-style mixes are combined where required.

Ribbon MixerPaddle MixerSifter

➜ Ensures batch-to-batch consistency.

Weighing, Packing & Inspection

Finished seeds are weighed, filled, sealed, coded and inspected for retail and bulk formats.

Multi Head WeigherVertical Form Fill Seal (Vffs)Premade PouchZip Lock/ Zipper PouchNitrogen PackingBatch CodingMetal DetectorCheckweigher With Printer

➜ Suitable for standup pouches, zipper packs, jars and bulk cartons.

➜ Dust and loose husk released at intake, cleaning, roasting and packing points is captured at source by:

  • Dust Collector Machine
  • Cyclone Separator
  • Centralised Dust Collection System
