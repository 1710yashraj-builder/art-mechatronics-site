How it works

Complete Flour Mill Process Flow

Raw Wheat Handling & Storage

Wheat grains are received, stored, and fed into the processing system.

Material Handling SystemsStorage Silos

➜ Ensures efficient bulk handling and storage.

Cleaning & Pre-Processing

Wheat is cleaned to remove impurities such as dust, stones, husk, and metal particles.

Pre CleanerDestonerMagnetic SeparatorAir Classifier

➜ Ensures:

  • High product purity
  • Protection of milling equipment
Conditioning (MOISTURE CONTROL)

Wheat is conditioned with water to achieve optimal moisture for milling.

Dampening SystemConditioning Unit

➜ Ensures:

  • Improved flour quality
  • Efficient grinding
  • Better separation
Milling / Grinding

Wheat is ground into flour through multiple stages.

Roller MillGrinding Machine

➜ Produces:

  • Atta (whole wheat flour)
  • Maida (refined flour)
  • Semolina (suji)
  • Bran
Sieving & Separation

Ground material is separated into different grades.

PlansifterVibro Sifter

➜ Ensures:

  • Uniform particle size
  • Multiple product outputs
Purification (FOR SEMOLINA & MAIDA)

Fine purification ensures premium quality refined products.

Purifier
Blending (OPTIONAL)

Different flour grades may be blended as per requirements.

Ribbon Blender

Dust CollectorCyclone SeparatorCentralized Dust Extraction System

Inspection Systems
