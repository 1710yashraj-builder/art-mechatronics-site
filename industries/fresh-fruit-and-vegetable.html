Fresh Fruit & Vegetable Processing Plant & Machinery Solutions

Turnkey plant · Machinery · Automation · India · UAE · Thailand

Fresh fruit and vegetable processing prepares produce for sale without drying, freezing or cooking it. Incoming material is pre-cooled, washed, sorted and graded, then trimmed, peeled or cut into ready-to-use portions and packed in chilled, sealed formats.

How it works

Complete Fresh Fruit & Vegetable Process Flow

Reception & Pre-Cooling

Produce is unloaded and brought down to working temperature before anything else is done to it. Field heat carried into the plant shortens shelf life, so cooling comes first, not last.

Loading & Unloading ConveyorChillerCooling TunnelRoller Conveyor
Washing & Rinsing

The produce is washed and then rinsed in clean water to carry away soil and surface debris. Surface water is blown off before the produce moves on, and handling is kept gentle because bruised tissue browns once the pack is sealed.

WasherRinsingBlower FanConveyor Belt
Sorting & Grading

Damaged, misshapen and off-colour units are taken out and the rest is graded to the sizes the buyer has specified. Camera-based inspection supports the hand-sorting stations on the belt.

GraderColor SorterVision InspectionInclined Conveyor Belt
Trimming, Peeling & Cutting

Stems, cores and skins are removed and the produce is cut into the wedges, florets, batons or shreds the pack calls for. Clean blades give a clean cut face, which is what keeps a fresh-cut pack looking fresh.

Stem RemovalPeelerSlicerKnife Cutter
Chilled Packing

Portions are filled into trays, punnets or films and sealed, with modified atmosphere used where the product needs it. Packs are weight-checked and labelled with the pack and use-by details.

Tray PackingMAP Tray SealingFlow WrapCheckweigher With PrinterLabelling
Cold Storage & Dispatch

Finished packs are palletised and held in a cold room until the vehicle is loaded. The chain from the packing hall to dispatch is kept unbroken.

Cold Storage/ Cold RoomPalletizerStretch WrappingSpiral Conveyor
