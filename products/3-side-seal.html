How it works

Working Principle of 3 Side Seal Packing Machine

Film Roll Feeding

Laminated packaging film roll is mounted onto film unwinding system

Pouch Forming Process (CORE PROCESS)

Film is folded and shaped into pouch structure automatically

➜ Machine prepares pouch for three-side sealing operation.

Product Filling Operation

Machine handles: ✔ Powders ✔ Granules ✔ Liquids ✔ Paste products ✔ Spices ✔ Pharma products ✔ Cosmetic products accurately and continuously.

Three Side Sealing Process

Machine seals pouch from: Left side Right side Bottom or top side creating a secure and leak-resistant package.

Cutting & Coding Integration

Automatic cutting system separates finished sachets Optional systems include: Batch coding Date printing QR code printing Barcode printing

Finished Sachet Discharge

Finished pouches discharged automatically for: Collection Cartoning Secondary packaging Dispatch operations
