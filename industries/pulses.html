Pulses Processing Plant & Machinery Solutions (Dal Mill & Pulse Processing Units)

Turnkey plant · Machinery · Automation · India · UAE · Thailand

Pulses processing, commonly known as dal milling, is a critical segment in the global agro and food processing industry. It involves processing raw pulses such as lentils, chickpeas (chana), peas, beans, sesame, and other legumes into clean, dehusked, and split products suitable for consumption.

How it works

Complete Pulses Process Flow

Raw Pulses Handling

Raw pulses are received and fed into the processing system.

Material Handling SystemsIntake Hopper

➜ Ensures smooth and continuous input.

Cleaning & Destoning

Pulses are cleaned to remove dust, stones, and foreign particles.

Pre CleanerDestonerAir ClassifierMagnetic Separator

➜ Ensures:

  • Product purity
  • Protection of machinery
Grading & Size Separation

Pulses are graded based on size and quality.

Grading MachineVibratory Sifter

➜ Ensures uniform processing and output.

Pitting / Surface Treatment

Pulses are treated to loosen husk for easy removal.

Pitting Machine

➜ Improves dehusking efficiency.

Conditioning / Oil & Water Treatment

Pulses are conditioned using moisture or oil.

Conditioning System

➜ Ensures:

  • Better husk removal
  • Reduced breakage
Dehusking

Outer husk is removed from pulses.

DehuskerEmery Roll Machine

➜ Ensures efficient husk removal.

Splitting

Pulses are split into dal.

Splitter

➜ Produces:

  • Chana dal
  • Toor dal
  • Moong dal
  • Other split pulses
Polishing (OPTIONAL)

Dal is polished to improve appearance.

Dal Polishing Machine

➜ Enhances:

  • Shine
  • Market value
Drying (IF REQUIRED)

Moisture is reduced for storage stability.

DryerHot Air Drying System

Dust CollectorCyclone Separator

Inspection System
