About ART Mechatronics

An independent automation company

ART Mechatronics builds the machines — and the intelligence — that automate modern powder processing. We design, manufacture and integrate complete turnkey lines in-house, so one team is accountable from the first drawing to the first production batch.

Who we are

Mechanical, electrical and controls — as one discipline

The name says it: mechatronics is where mechanical engineering, electronics and software controls meet. That is exactly how we build. A single ART line combines precision stainless fabrication, pneumatic and drive systems, sensors and load cells, and PLC + HMI automation into one coordinated machine.

We focus on automatic powder handling: pneumatic conveying, storage and weighing, accurate batching and dosing, uniform mixing, sieving and dust-free collection. Every unit is engineered to stand alone — and to lock together into a fully automatic line that runs with minimal manpower.

Because design, manufacturing and integration all happen in-house, we control quality, timelines and the way each element fits the next — and we can customise any part to your product, capacity and hygiene requirements.
