How it works

Complete Supari Process Flow

Raw Supari Feeding & Pre-Cleaning

Raw betelnut is first fed into the system where initial impurities such as dust, leaves, and light foreign particles are removed.

Pre Cleaner Machine

➜ Ensures removal of large and light impurities before further processing.

Fine Cleaning & Destoning

The material is then passed through advanced cleaning systems to remove stones, sand, and heavy contaminants.

Destoner MachineCyclone Separator

➜ Critical step to protect downstream machinery and ensure product purity.

Supari Cutting / Size Reduction

Clean betelnuts are processed through cutting machines to achieve desired size and shape depending on product requirement (chips, granules, etc.).

Supari Cutting MachineBetelnut Slicer

➜ Precision cutting ensures uniform size for consistent mixing and roasting.

Grading & Air Classification

After cutting, the material is graded to separate different sizes and remove fine dust or unwanted particles.

Vibratory GraderAir Classifier

➜ Ensures uniformity and improves final product quality.

Roasting / Drying Process

The graded supari is roasted or dried to reduce moisture content and enhance flavor, texture, and shelf life.

Supari Roaster / DryerHot Air Generator System

➜ Controlled temperature ensures perfect crunchiness and consistency.

Cooling Process

Post roasting, the product is cooled to stabilize moisture and prevent condensation.

Cooling Conveyor / System

➜ Maintains product integrity before further processing.

Flavoring & Mixing (Optional)

Depending on the final product, supari is mixed with flavors, perfumes, and additives.

Ribbon Blender / Paddle MixerCoating Drum

➜ Ensures uniform coating and flavor distribution.

Final Sieving & Quality Check

Final screening is done to remove any inconsistencies and ensure premium quality output.

Vibro Sifter
Packaging

Finished product is packed into pouches or containers using automated packaging systems.

Form Fill Seal (FFS) MachinesPouch Packing Machines

➜ High-speed packing suitable for commercial-scale production.
