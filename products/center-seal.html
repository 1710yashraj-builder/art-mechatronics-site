How it works

Working Principle of Center Seal Packing Machine

Film Roll Feeding

Laminated packaging film roll is mounted onto film unwinding system

Pouch Forming Process

Film passes through forming collar to create tubular pouch shape

➜ Machine prepares pouch structure for vertical center sealing operation.

Product Filling Operation

Machine handles: ✔ Pan masala ✔ Tobacco ✔ Spices ✔ Namkeen ✔ Pulses ✔ Granules ✔ Powders accurately and continuously.

Center Sealing Process

Vertical sealing system creates strong center seal along backside of pouch Horizontal sealing jaws seal: Top side Bottom side forming finished pouch structure.

Cutting & Coding Integration

Automatic cutting system separates finished pouches Optional systems include: Batch coding Date printing QR code printing Barcode printing

Finished Pouch Discharge

Finished pouches discharged automatically for: Collection Cartoning Secondary packaging Dispatch operations
