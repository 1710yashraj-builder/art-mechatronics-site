How it works

Working Principle of Unit Carton Packing Machine

  • 1. Carton Feeding Flat cartons are loaded into carton feeding magazine.
  • 2. Carton Erection Process (CORE PROCESS) Machine automatically picks and erects cartons using synchronized carton handling systems.

➜ Servo synchronization ensures smooth carton movement and accurate carton positioning.

  • 3. Product Feeding & Carton Filling Operation Machine handles: ✔ Tobacco packs ✔ Spice packs ✔ Pouches ✔ Sachets ✔ Boxes ✔ FMCG products ✔ Pharmaceutical products

accurately and continuously.

  • 4. Carton Folding & Sealing Process Machine performs: Flap folding Carton locking Hot glue sealing (optional) Tuck-in sealing Barcode printing

for premium retail packaging quality.

  • 5. Coding & Inspection Integration Optional systems include: Batch coding Date printing QR code printing Vision inspection Check weighing
  • 6. Finished Carton Discharge Finished cartons discharged automatically for: Case packing Palletizing Dispatch operations Retail distribution
