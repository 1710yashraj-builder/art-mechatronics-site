How it works

Working Principle of Jar Packing Machine

  • 1. Empty Jar Feeding Empty jars are automatically fed into packaging line through synchronized conveyor systems.
  • 2. Product Filling Process Machine automatically fills products into jars using precision dosing and filling systems.

➜ Servo synchronization ensures accurate filling and smooth product handling.

  • 3. Jar Filling Operation Machine handles: ✔ Peanut butter ✔ Chocolate spreads ✔ Nutella ✔ Jam ✔ Pickles ✔ Honey ✔ Sauces and pastes

accurately and continuously.

  • 4. Capping & Sealing Process Machine performs: Cap placing Cap tightening Induction sealing Vacuum sealing (optional) Leak-proof sealing

for hygienic and airtight retail packaging quality.

  • 5. Coding & Labeling Integration Optional systems include: Batch coding Date printing QR code printing Labeling systems Vision inspection
  • 6. Finished Jar Discharge Finished jars discharged automatically for: Cartoning Case packing Retail distribution Export shipment handling
