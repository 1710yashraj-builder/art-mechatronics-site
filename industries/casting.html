Casting Plant & Machinery Solutions

Turnkey plant · Machinery · Automation · India · UAE · Thailand

Casting turns molten metal into finished components by pouring it into moulds or dies, letting it solidify, then cutting off gates and risers and cleaning the surface. A casting unit handles hot metal, cooling lines and heavy fettling dust before parts are inspected and dispatched.

How it works

Complete Casting Process Flow

Mould & Die Preparation

Moulds, cores and dies are cleaned, assembled and given a refractory coating. Coating slurry is kept in suspension, applied to the mould face and dried before the mould is closed.

AgitatorStorage TankHot Air GeneratorDust Extraction System
Metal Transfer & Pouring

Molten metal is tapped, carried to the casting station in ladles and poured into the mould or die cavity. Fume from the pouring bay is captured over the pouring line.

Furnace BurnerFume Extraction SystemMulticlone Dust Collector MachineBlower Fan
Solidification & Cooling

The poured castings are held until the metal has set, then moved through a cooling line. Heat given off during cooling is carried away from the casting bay.

Cooling ConveyorCooling TunnelChain ConveyorHeat Recovery Unit - HRU
Gate & Riser Removal

Runners, gates, risers and flash are cut off and the parting lines are ground back. Fettling produces metallic dust and grit, so the fettling bay is put under extraction.

Grinding MachineCartridge Dust Collector MachineCyclone SeparatorVibratory Conveyor
Cleaning & Surface Finishing

Castings are washed clear of sand, scale and machining residue, then polished where the finish matters. Wash and finishing areas are ducted so mist and dust do not carry into the shop.

WasherPolisherConveyor BeltAir Pollution Control Equipment
Inspection & Dispatch

Castings are checked for dimensional accuracy, surface defects and internal soundness before they are accepted. Accepted parts are sorted, protected and loaded out.

X Ray InspectionVision InspectionRoller ConveyorStretch Wrapping
