How it works

Working Principle of Roller Conveyor Machine

Product Loading

Cartons, boxes, sacks, pallets, or products are placed on conveyor rollers

Roller Movement Generation

Depending on conveyor type:

  • Gravity conveyors use inclined movement
  • Powered conveyors use motor-driven rollers

➜ Rollers rotate to move products smoothly forward

Product Transportation

Machine transfers:

  • Boxes
  • Cartons
  • Bags
  • Pallets
  • Industrial products
  • Packages between industrial process stages efficiently.
Continuous Material Handling

Conveyor integrates with: Packaging lines Assembly systems Warehouses Dispatch lines Sorting systems

Direction & Flow Control

Rollers can be configured for: Straight transfer Curved transfer Accumulation systems Inclined transfer

Product Discharge

Products discharged automatically at required process point
