Industrial Process Overview

Raw Material Handling & Movement

Materials such as metal scrap, billets, castings, aluminum ingots, and raw inputs are transported within the plant. Equipment Supplied:

Belt ConveyorsScrew ConveyorsBucket ElevatorsChain Conveyors

➜ Ensures:

  • Efficient material flow
  • Reduced manual handling
Foundry & Casting Operations

Molten metal is poured into molds to create desired shapes. Processes:

Sand CastingDie CastingInvestment Casting

➜ Generates:

  • Dust
  • Fumes
  • Heat
Fabrication & Metal Processing

Includes cutting, welding, bending, and assembly.

➜ Requires:

  • Clean work environment
  • Efficient fume and dust extraction
Surface Treatment (SHOT BLAST / SAND BLAST)

Used to clean and finish metal surfaces.

➜ Generates:

  • Heavy dust
  • Abrasive particles
Finishing & Inspection

Final products are cleaned, inspected, and prepared for dispatch.
