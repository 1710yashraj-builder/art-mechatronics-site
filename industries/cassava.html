Cassava Processing Plant & Machinery Solutions

Turnkey plant · Machinery · Automation · India · UAE · Thailand

Cassava processing turns freshly harvested cassava roots into chips, flour and starch for food, feed and industrial use. The roots are washed, peeled, chipped or rasped, dewatered, dried and milled before sifting and packing.

How it works

Complete Cassava Water Process Flow

Reception & Root Washing

Harvested roots are tipped into reception and washed to remove field soil, grit and stones picked up during lifting. Because cassava deteriorates quickly after harvest, the intake section is sized to keep roots moving straight into peeling.

HopperWasherDe-StonerLoading & Unloading Conveyor
Peeling & Rinsing

The outer rind and inner peel are removed from the root, since the peel is where most of the bitter fraction sits. Peeled roots are rinsed again and the peel is taken away on a separate line for feed or disposal.

PeelerRinsingVibratory ConveyorScrew Conveyor
Chipping & Rasping

Peeled roots are cut into chips for the drying line, or rasped and crushed to a mash where starch is the end product. Reducing the root soon after peeling limits discolouration and gives the dryer an even feed.

ChipperWet GrinderCrusherCleat Conveyor
Dewatering

Wet mash is pressed so that free water is taken out mechanically rather than driven off in the dryer. The pressed cake is broken up before it is fed forward so that it dries evenly.

Hydraulic Press / Baling PressDisintegrator MachineShaftless Screw Conveyor
Drying

Chips or pressed cake are dried in a flash or rotary dryer until the moisture is low enough for milling and storage. Dried material is separated from the drying air in a cyclone before it goes to the mill.

Flash DryerRotary Dryer, Drum DryerHot Air GeneratorCyclone SeparatorMoisture Analyzer
Milling & Sifting

Dried cassava is milled into flour or starch powder and sifted so that oversize particles are returned to the mill. Milling is run under dust extraction because cassava powder is light and carries readily into the plant air.

Hammer Mill MachineAir Classifier Mill MachinePlan SifterPulverizer With Dust Collector
Bagging & Despatch

Flour, starch or dried chips are weighed into sacks, stitched and checkweighed for bulk despatch. Bagged output is palletised and wrapped for transport.

BaggingSack PackingStitchingCheckweigher With PrinterPalletizer
