Variants

Types of Separator Machines

  • 1. Vibro Separator Machine

Uses: ✔ Vibratory screening technology for powder and granule separation.

  • 2. Air Separator Machine

Uses: ✔ Controlled airflow for lightweight impurity separation.

  • 3. Gravity Separator Machine

Uses: ✔ Density difference for separating heavy and light particles.

  • 4. Grain Separator Machine

Designed for: ✔ Grain cleaning ✔ Seed classification ✔ Agro product processing

  • 5. Centrifugal Separator Machine

Uses: ✔ Rotational centrifugal force for liquid and slurry separation applications.

  • 6. Multi Deck Separator Machine

Allows: ✔ Multi-size particle grading ✔ Simultaneous classification
