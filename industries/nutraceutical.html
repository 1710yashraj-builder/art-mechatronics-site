Nutraceutical Processing Plant & Machinery Solutions

Turnkey plant · Machinery · Automation · India · UAE · Thailand

Nutraceutical processing turns vitamins, minerals, botanical extracts and functional ingredients into capsules, tablets, powders and gummies sold as health products. Premixes are weighed, sifted and blended, cooked or granulated where the format requires it, then filled into capsules, bottles or sachets and sealed.

How it works

Complete Nutraceutical Process Flow

Weighing & Premix Preparation

Vitamin, mineral and extract inputs are tipped and weighed to the formulation sheet, then conveyed to the mixing room. Minor ingredients are pre-blended with a carrier so they disperse evenly in the main batch.

Bag Dump StationWeighing & DosingScrew ConveyorDrum Mixer
Sifting & Micronising

Ingredients are screened and, where a fine and uniform particle size is needed, reduced in a mill. Finer particles help the blend stay uniform and fill consistently into capsules.

SifterMicronizer MachineAir Classifier Mill MachineMagnetic Separator
Blending

The full formulation is blended until the actives are evenly spread through the carrier base. Blend uniformity is what keeps the declared content the same from the first capsule to the last.

Conical Screw MixerCapsule MixerRibbon MixerDouble Cone Mixer
Gummy & Chewable Cooking

For gummy and chewable formats the gelling base, sweetener and actives are cooked in a jacketed vessel and deposited into moulds. The deposited pieces are cooled before they are demoulded and finished.

Cooking Kettle, Boiling TankJacketed TankFormingCooling Tunnel
Capsule & Bottle Filling

The blend is dosed into capsules or filled straight into bottles and jars, which are then closed and sealed. Fill weight is monitored through the run.

Auger FillerFillingBottle Packing (Spray, Pet, Glass)CappingCap Lid Pressing
Labelling, Inspection & Cartoning

Bottles are labelled, coded with batch and expiry details, and camera-checked for cap and label placement. Cleared packs are cartoned and cased for despatch.

Wraparound Labelling for Cans & JarsBatch CodingVision InspectionMono Carton PackingMaster Case Packing
