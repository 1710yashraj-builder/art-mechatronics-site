Beans Processing Plant & Machinery Solutions

Turnkey plant · Machinery · Automation · India · UAE · Thailand

Bean processing prepares whole pulses such as rajma, cowpea and other dry beans for retail and export sale. Unlike split dals, beans are handled whole: cleaned of stones and dust, separated by density, graded by size, polished, then optically sorted to remove discoloured and damaged seed before bagging.

How it works

Complete Beans Process Flow

Intake & Pre-Cleaning

Dry beans are tipped into intake and carried through cleaning to remove soil, pod pieces, straw and dust. Ferrous tramp metal picked up during harvest and transport is taken out at this stage.

Bag Dump StationPre-CleanerAir Screen CleanerMagnetic Separator
De-stoning & Density Separation

Beans sit close in size to field stones and soil clods, so this separation works on density rather than screen size alone. Light, hollow and insect-damaged beans are lifted away in the air stream.

De-StonerGravity SeparatorAir Aspirator
Size Grading

Whole beans are traded by size grade, so the cleaned lot is split into count fractions on graded screens. Undersized and broken beans are diverted into a separate stream.

GraderScreenerVibratory Conveyor
Polishing & De-dusting

Beans are polished to lift dust and field soil off the seed coat and give an even surface. Dust drawn off during polishing is collected rather than released into the plant.

PolisherScourerDe-Dusting SystemCyclone Separator
Optical Sorting & Inspection

Colour sorting takes out discoloured, spotted, mouldy and off-variety beans. Beans destined for export are then checked for stone, glass and metal before bagging.

Color SorterX Ray InspectionMetal Detector
Bagging & Export Packing

Graded beans go into retail packs or woven sacks for bulk and export despatch. Filled sacks are weighed, stitched, palletised and wrapped for transport.

BaggingSack PackingStitchingCheckweigher With PrinterPalletizer
