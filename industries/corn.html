Corn Processing Plant & Machinery Solutions

Turnkey plant · Machinery · Automation · India · UAE · Thailand

Corn processing covers the food lines that turn cleaned corn and corn grits into flakes, extruded snacks and corn flour. Grits are cooked with a flavour slurry, dried, tempered, rolled into flakes and toasted, while snack streams are extruded, coated and cooled before packing.

How it works

Complete Corn Process Flow

Cleaning & Grit Preparation

Corn is cleaned and de-stoned, then broken and graded to the grit size the flake or snack line needs. Off-size fractions are diverted to flour or feed.

Air Screen CleanerDe-StonerCrusherGraderBucket Elevator
Cooking with Flavour Slurry

Grits are cooked under steam with a slurry of sugar, salt and malt until the starch is gelatinised. The cooked mass is discharged and broken up so the pieces separate before drying.

Cooking Kettle, Boiling TankSteam GeneratorBoilerRibbon Mixer
Drying & Tempering

Cooked grits are dried and then held in tempering bins so moisture equalises through each piece. Even moisture is what allows a flake to roll without tearing.

Rotary Dryer, Drum DryerFluidized Bed Dryer – FBDDay BinMoisture Analyzer
Flaking & Toasting

Tempered grits are pressed between rolls into thin flakes and then toasted so they blister and crisp. Flakes are cooled on the way out of the oven.

Roller Mill MachineRoasterRotary Rack OvenCooling Conveyor
Extruded Snack & Coating Stream

Corn meal fed to an extruder expands into puffs and rings, which are then tumbled with oil and seasoning in a coating pan. Coated snacks are cooled before they reach the weigher.

Extruder MachineCoating Pan MixerCooling TunnelVibratory Conveyor
Weighing, Gas-Flush Packing & Inspection

Flakes and snacks are weighed on a multi-head weigher, filled into pillow packs or lined cartons under gas flush, coded and inspected.

Multi Head WeigherVertical Form Fill Seal (Vffs)Nitrogen PackingLined CartonX Ray Inspection
