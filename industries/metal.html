Metal Processing Plant & Machinery Solutions

Turnkey plant · Machinery · Automation · India · UAE · Thailand

Metal processing covers cutting, forming, machining, heat treatment and surface finishing of steel and non-ferrous components. Metal plants deal with oil mist, grinding dust, pickling fume, furnace heat and a continuous flow of swarf and offcuts leaving the machines.

How it works

Complete Metal Process Flow

Raw Material Intake & Storage

Coil, sheet, plate, bar and section are received, stacked and issued to the shop as jobs are released. Small components are fed forward from bulk storage rather than carried by hand.

Roller ConveyorGoods LiftTrolleyVibratory Feeder
Cutting & Forming

Material is cut to length and pressed, bent or formed into shape. Forming lines are fed and discharged on conveyors, and cutting fume is drawn off at source.

Hydraulic Press / Baling PressSlat ConveyorChain ConveyorFume Extraction System
Machining & Deburring

Turning, milling, drilling and grinding bring the part to its finished dimensions. Dry machining and grinding release fine metallic dust, which is captured at the machine.

Milling MachineSurface GrinderCartridge Dust Collector MachineDust Extraction System
Heat Treatment

Components are annealed, normalised, hardened or tempered in a furnace and then cooled in a controlled way. Furnace flue gas and radiated heat are taken away from the treatment bay.

Furnace BurnerHot Air GeneratorHeat ExchangerCooling TowerHeat Recovery Unit - HRU
Pickling, Cleaning & Drying

Scale, oil and machining residue are removed in acid or alkaline baths, then the parts are rinsed and dried. Acid mist from the pickling line is scrubbed before the air is released.

WasherWet ScrubberAir Pollution Control EquipmentContinuous Conveyor, Tunnel Dryer
Swarf & Scrap Handling

Turnings, offcuts and rejected parts are collected from under the machines and moved to the scrap yard. Ferrous and non-ferrous scrap are separated and reduced so the material can be sold or recharged.

Screw ConveyorDrag Chain ConveyorMagnetic SeparatorShredder Machine
