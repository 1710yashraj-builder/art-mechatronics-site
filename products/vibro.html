Overview

About the Vibro Machine

The machine uses controlled vibratory motion generated through a vibrating motor, which causes material to move across specially designed mesh screens. This enables efficient separation based on:

  • Particle size
  • Granule size
  • Density
  • Foreign impurities

Vibro machines are widely used in industries such as:

• Food processing

• Pharmaceuticals

• Chemicals

• Spices

• Agro products

• Minerals

• Ceramics

• Paints & coatings

• Recycling industries

where accurate screening, impurity removal, and particle size classification are essential.

The machine is suitable for:

  • Powder sieving
  • Granule grading
  • Liquid filtration
  • Dust separation
  • Particle classification
  • Impurity removal
  • Multi-stage screening operations

How it works

Working Principle of Vibro Machine

Material Feeding
Powders, granules, particles, or liquids are fed onto vibrating screen surface
Vibratory Motion Generation
Vibrating motor generates multi-directional vibrationMaterial spreads uniformly across mesh screen

➜ Creates efficient particle separation action

Particle Size Separation
Smaller particles pass through mesh openingsOversized particles move toward separate outlet
Multi-Deck Screening (OPTIONAL)
Multiple screens can classify material into different particle sizes simultaneously
Impurity & Dust Removal
Foreign particles and oversized impurities separated effectively
Product Discharge
Different particle grades discharged separately
