Footwear Sole Manufacturing Plant & Machinery Solutions

Turnkey plant · Machinery · Automation · India · UAE · Thailand

Footwear sole manufacturing mills rubber or EVA compound into sheet, blanks the sheet into preforms, and moulds each preform into a sole, which is then trimmed, buffed on the bonding face, washed and printed before pairing.

How it works

Complete Footwear Sole Process Flow

Compound Mixing & Sheeting

Rubber or EVA is mixed with fillers, blowing agents and pigment, then milled into sheet and cooled. Sheet is stacked flat so blanks cut from it stay to gauge.

Weighing & DosingSigma MixerRoller Mill MachineCooling ConveyorDust Collector Machine
Blank Cutting

Sheet is cut into blanks matched to sole size and cavity, and fed to the press stations in sorted trays.

Blade CutterRoller CutterVibratory FeederSlat ConveyorTrolley
Moulding & Expansion Utilities

Blanks are moulded under heat, and expanded grades are held and stabilised after release from the mould. Press heating and post-mould cooling are carried by the plant utility circuits.

BoilerThermic Fluid HeaterHot Air GeneratorChillerCooling Tunnel
Trimming & Buffing

Flash is trimmed from the sole edge and the bonding face is buffed so adhesive will key to it. Buffing releases fine rubber dust, which is drawn off at each machine hood.

Surface GrinderKnife CutterDe-Dusting SystemPulse Jet Dust Collector MachineDust Extraction System
Washing, Printing & Inspection

Soles are washed to remove release agent and dust, dried, printed with size and branding marks, and checked.

WasherBatch Type - Hot Air DryerPrinter (Cij, Inkjet, Co2 Laser, Hand)Vision InspectionAuto Reject System
Pairing, Packing & Despatch

Left and right soles are paired by size, counted, bagged and packed into cartons for the shoe line.

Count & StackPouchLabellingMaster Carton PackingPalletizer
