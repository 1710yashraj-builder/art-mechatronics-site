Chickpeas Processing Plant & Machinery Solutions

Turnkey plant · Machinery · Automation · India · UAE · Thailand

Chickpea processing turns raw chana into chana dal, roasted gram and besan flour. The seed is cleaned, de-stoned and graded, conditioned to loosen the skin, then dehusked and split into dal. Separate routes roast the gram or grind it into besan, after which the product is sorted, weighed and packed.

How it works

Complete Chickpeas Process Flow

Intake & Cleaning

Raw chana is cleaned of soil, chaff, straw, stones and metal as soon as it is received. Elevators and conveyors move the seed between cleaning stages without manual handling.

Pre-CleanerAir Screen CleanerDe-StonerMagnetic SeparatorZ Type Elevator
Grading & Conditioning

Chana is graded by size and density so that dehusking can be set separately for each fraction. The seed is then mixed with water, held, and dried under control so that the skin loosens from the cotyledons before milling.

GraderGravity SeparatorDrum MixerBatch Type - Hot Air DryerMoisture Analyzer
Dehusking & Splitting

The skin is abraded away and the kernel is split into chana dal. Husk is drawn off by air and collected apart from the dal stream.

DehuskingSplitterAir AspiratorCyclone Separator
Roasting Route for Roasted Gram

Part of the crop is roasted rather than milled, then cooled before the brittle skin is rubbed off. The loosened skin is winnowed away to leave clean roasted gram.

RoasterCooling ConveyorSkin RemovalWinnower
Besan Milling

Chana dal is ground into besan, the gram flour used throughout Indian cooking. Ground stock is sifted and the coarse tailings are sent back for regrinding.

Stone Mill MachineGrinding Mill MachinePlan SifterPulverizer With Dust Collector
Weighing & Packing

Dal and roasted gram are weighed and filled as grain, while besan, being a powder, is filled by auger into pouches and bags. Packs are checked for metal and weight before despatch.
