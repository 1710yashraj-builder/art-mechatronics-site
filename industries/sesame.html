Sesame Processing Plant & Machinery Solutions

Turnkey plant · Machinery · Automation · India · UAE · Thailand

Sesame processing turns raw til seed into hulled sesame, roasted seed and sesame oil. Seed is cleaned and de-stoned, washed and soaked to loosen the hull, hulled and parted from the hull by density and air, then dried, cooled and colour sorted. A separate route roasts and expels the seed for oil.

How it works

Complete Sesame Process Flow

Intake & Cleaning

Raw sesame carries a heavy load of field dust, sand and small stones, so cleaning is done in stages. Screens, air and de-stoning bring the seed to a state fit for washing.

Pre-CleanerAir Screen CleanerDe-StonerMagnetic Separator
Washing & Soaking

Seed is washed and then soaked so that the hull swells and lifts from the kernel. The wet section is kept apart from the dry side of the plant to avoid carry-over of moisture.

WasherRinsingStorage TankScrew Conveyor
Hulling & Hull Separation

The softened hull is rubbed off the seed in the hulling section. Hull and kernel are then parted on density and in an air stream, since the two are close in size.

DehuskingSkin RemovalGravity SeparatorAir Aspirator
Drying & Cooling

Hulled sesame is dried back down from its wet state and then cooled before storage. Moisture is checked so that the seed is stable for packing.

Fluidized Bed Dryer – FBDContinuous Conveyor, Tunnel DryerCooling ConveyorMoisture Analyzer
Colour Sorting & Inspection

Optical sorting removes unhulled, dark and stained seed from the white hulled sesame. Metal detection is applied before the seed is packed or sent for pressing.

Color SorterVision InspectionMetal Detector
Roasting & Oil Expelling

Seed routed to oil is roasted and then pressed in an expeller to draw out til oil. The cake left after pressing is taken off as a by-product and the oil is held in tanks.

RoasterExpeller MachineStorage TankJacketed Tank
Weighing & Packing

Hulled sesame is weighed and packed for food use and for export despatch. Packs may be gas flushed, as the seed is oil-rich.

Multi Head WeigherVertical Form Fill Seal (Vffs)Nitrogen PackingCheckweigher With Printer
