Overview

About the Plan Sifter

Plan sifters are one of the most important machines used in:

  • Flour mills
  • Grain processing plants
  • Food processing industries
  • Powder classification systems

where accurate particle separation, product consistency, and high-capacity continuous operation are essential.

The machine operates using:

  • Multi-deck sieve boxes
  • Reciprocating motion
  • Vibratory sifting action
  • Mesh-based classification

to efficiently separate materials into different grades and particle sizes.

Plan sifters are highly suitable for:

  • Flour grading
  • Atta classification
  • Maida separation
  • Bran separation
  • Semolina grading
  • Powder particle classification
  • Food powder screening

How it works

Working Principle of Plan Sifter Machine

Material Feeding
Flour, powders, semolina, bran, or granular materials are fed into sieve compartments
Reciprocating Sifting Motion
Plansifter generates controlled reciprocating movementMaterial spreads uniformly across multiple sieve decks

➜ Enables accurate particle separation and grading

Multi-Deck Classification
Different mesh sizes separate material into multiple grades simultaneously

Machine can separate:

  • Fine flour
  • Medium particles
  • Bran
  • Coarse particles
Continuous Product Flow
Product moves continuously through different sieve chambers for high-capacity industrial processing
Fine & Coarse Material Separation
Smaller particles pass through fine meshLarger particles remain on upper sieve layers
Product Discharge
Different grades discharged separately through dedicated outlets
