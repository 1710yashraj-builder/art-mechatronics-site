Overview

About the Cooking Kettle, Boiling Tank

These machines are widely used in industries such as food processing, dairy, pharmaceuticals, chemicals, nutraceuticals, and cosmetics, where controlled heating and uniform cooking are essential.

The system is designed with a jacketed structure, allowing steam, thermal oil, or electric heating to provide uniform heat distribution, ensuring efficient cooking, reduced processing time, and high product quality.

How it works

Working Principle of Tilting Cooking Tank

Material Loading
Raw material (liquid / semi-solid) is loaded into the tank
Heating System
Heat is applied through jacket using:SteamThermal oilElectric heaters
Heat Transfer
Heat is transferred uniformly through vessel wallsPrevents burning or uneven cooking
Mixing (OPTIONAL)
Agitator can be providedEnsures uniform consistency
Cooking / Boiling Process
Material is heated to required temperatureCooking or boiling takes place
Tilting & Discharge
Tank tilts for easy dischargeImproves efficiency and hygiene
