About Wafer processing

The manufacturing process requires precision batter preparation, uniform sheet baking, controlled cream filling, and accurate cutting and packaging to achieve consistent quality and high production output.

How it works

Complete Wafer Process Flow

Raw Material Handling & Dosing

Ingredients such as flour, starch, sugar, oil, and additives are received and fed into the system.

Material Handling SystemsIngredient Dosing System

➜ Ensures accurate formulation.

Batter Preparation (CORE PROCESS)

Ingredients are mixed to form a smooth batter.

Batter Mixer

➜ Ensures:

  • Uniform consistency
  • Lump-free batter
Wafer Sheet Baking (VERY CRITICAL STAGE)

Batter is deposited onto heated plates to form thin wafer sheets.

Automatic Wafer Baking Oven

➜ Ensures:

  • Uniform thin sheets
  • Crisp texture
Cooling

Baked wafer sheets are cooled before further processing.

Cooling Conveyor
Cream Preparation & Spreading

Cream is prepared and spread between wafer layers.

Cream MixerCream Spreading Machine

➜ Ensures:

  • Uniform cream layers
  • Consistent taste
Layering & Block Formation

Multiple wafer sheets are stacked with cream layers.

➜ Forms large wafer blocks for cutting.

Cutting (CRITICAL STEP)

Wafer blocks are cut into final shapes.

Wafer Cutting Machine

➜ Produces:

  • Rectangular wafers
  • Stick wafers
  • Customized shapes
Final Cooling

Cut wafers are cooled before packaging.

Cooling Conveyor
Packaging (HIGH-SPEED FMCG)

Finished wafers are packed into pouches or trays.

Flow Wrap MachineHorizontal Packaging Machine

➜ Ensures:

  • Hygienic packaging
  • Long shelf life
