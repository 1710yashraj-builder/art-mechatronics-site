Eggs Processing Plant & Machinery Solutions

Turnkey plant · Machinery · Automation · India · UAE · Thailand

Egg processing turns shell eggs into graded table eggs, pasteurised liquid egg and dried egg powder. Eggs are washed, candled and graded on the shell, then broken and separated into whole egg, albumen or yolk, pasteurised, and either chilled or spray dried.

How it works

Complete Eggs Process Flow

Intake & Shell Washing

Shell eggs arrive on trays and are washed with warm water and a sanitising rinse, then dried before further handling. Washing removes soil and litter from the shell so that surface contamination is not carried into the breaking stage.

Loading & Unloading ConveyorWasherRinsingConveyor Belt
Candling & Grading

Eggs pass over a light source for candling and are graded by weight, with cracked, leaking and stained eggs diverted off the line. Only sound shell eggs go forward to breaking, and the rest are separated for other uses.

GraderVision InspectionAuto Reject SystemRoller Conveyor
Breaking & Separation

Shells are cracked and the contents are taken as whole egg or split into yolk and albumen. The liquid is strained to remove shell fragments and membrane before it is held in buffer tanks.

Shell RemovalSeparatorSieverStorage Tank
Pasteurisation & Chilling

Liquid egg is pasteurised, then cooled and held chilled until it is filled or dried. Homogenising gives a uniform liquid for filling and for feeding the dryer.

Heat ExchangerPasteurizerHomogenizerChillerJacketed Tank
Spray Drying to Egg Powder

Pasteurised liquid egg is atomised into a hot air stream and dried to a free-flowing powder. Fines are recovered from the exhaust air and the powder is sieved before storage.

Spray DryerCyclone SeparatorSifterScrew Conveyor
Filling, Packing & Cold Storage

Liquid egg is filled into pouches or cans and powder is filled by auger into pouches or sacks. Packs are checked for metal, coded and labelled, then held in chilled or ambient storage as the format requires.
