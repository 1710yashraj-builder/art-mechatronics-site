Pre CleanerDestonerMagnetic SeparatorAir Classifier

  • Product purity
  • Protection of machinery
Dehulling / Decortication (VERY IMPORTANT)

Outer husk or shell is removed from seeds.

Dehuller / Decorticator

➜ Applicable for:

  • Sunflower
  • Soybean
  • Canola

➜ Improves:

  • Oil yield
  • Processing efficiency
Cracking / Size Reduction

Seeds are cracked or broken to prepare for further processing.

Cracker / Crusher

➜ Ensures better downstream processing.

Conditioning (CORE PROCESS)

Seeds are heated or moisture-conditioned.

Conditioning SystemCooker / Heating Unit

➜ Ensures:

  • Proper softening
  • Improved oil extraction efficiency
Flaking (OPTIONAL FOR OIL INDUSTRY)

Seeds are flattened into flakes for efficient processing.

Flaking Machine
Drying (IF REQUIRED)

Moisture content is controlled for storage and processing.

DryerHot Air Drying System

➜ Ensures longer shelf life.

Seeds are graded based on size and quality.

Grading MachineVibratory Sifter

Inspection System
