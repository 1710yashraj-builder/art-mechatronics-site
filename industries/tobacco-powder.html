Tobacco Powder (Gul / Manjan) Processing Plant & Machinery Solutions

Turnkey plant · Machinery · Automation · India · UAE · Thailand

Tobacco powder, commonly known as gul or manjan, is a finely processed product used in traditional oral applications. The manufacturing process requires ultra-fine grinding, controlled moisture levels, hygienic processing, and uniform blending to achieve consistent texture and quality.

How it works

Complete Tobacco Powder Process Flow

Raw Tobacco Receiving & Handling

Tobacco leaves or processed tobacco are received and transferred into the processing system.

Conveyor SystemsMaterial Handling Equipment

➜ Ensures smooth and controlled handling.

Cleaning & Dust Removal

Tobacco is cleaned to remove sand, dust, and foreign particles.

Pre CleanerAir ClassifierCyclone Separator

➜ Improves product purity and protects downstream equipment.

Conditioning & Drying

Tobacco is conditioned and dried to achieve the ideal moisture level for grinding.

Conditioning ChamberTray Dryer / Continuous Dryer

➜ Ensures efficient grinding and prevents clogging.

Fine Grinding / Pulverizing

Tobacco is processed into ultra-fine powder.

PulverizerFine Grinding Mill

➜ Ensures:

  • Uniform fine particle size
  • Smooth texture
  • Consistent product quality
Blending & Mixing

The powder may be blended with additives or processed further for uniformity.

Ribbon BlenderPaddle Mixer

➜ Ensures homogeneous consistency.

Sieving & Classification

The powder is screened to remove coarse particles and ensure uniform size.

Vibro SifterAir Classifier

➜ Critical for fine-grade product quality.
