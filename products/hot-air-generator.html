Overview

About the Hot Air Generator

Hot air generators are widely used across industries such as food processing, agro processing, chemicals, pharmaceuticals, textiles, packaging, plastics, and manufacturing, where controlled hot air is required for drying, roasting, curing, or heating operations.

Unlike steam-based systems, hot air generators offer direct and efficient heat transfer, making them ideal for energy-saving and precise temperature control applications.

How it works

Working Principle of Hot Air Generator

Fuel Combustion
Fuel is burned using a burner system:Diesel / Gas / Biomass / Oil
Heat Generation
Heat is generated inside a combustion chamberHeat is transferred to air through heat exchanger (indirect type)
Air Heating
Blower pushes ambient airAir passes over heated surfacesGets converted into hot air
Hot Air Delivery
Hot air is supplied to process equipment:DryersOvensFurnaces
Temperature Control
Controlled using sensors and control systemsEnsures stable process conditions
