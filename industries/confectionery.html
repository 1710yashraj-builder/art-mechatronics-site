Confectionery Processing Plant & Machinery Solutions

Confectionery covers wafers, chocolate, cakes and energy bars. Batters and masses are prepared and mixed, baked or refined and tempered, filled or creamed, cooled, cut into shape and wrapped.

How it works

Complete Confectionery Process Flow

Ingredient dosing and batter preparation

Ingredients are weighed to the recipe and worked into a batter, dough or mass. Wafer batter is mixed thin; cake batter and bar masses are mixed heavy.

Weighing & DosingSigma MixerPlanetary MixerAgitator
Refining and conching — the chocolate branch

Chocolate mass is ground down and worked until the particles are reduced, then held warm and moving in a jacketed vessel. This branch does not apply to the wafer, cake or bar lines.

Ball Mill MachineColloid MillWet GrinderJacketed Tank
Baking and depositing

Wafer batter is baked into thin sheets, cake batter is deposited and baked, and bar masses are formed and set. Heat is carried to the oven by hot air or a thermic fluid circuit.

Rotary Rack OvenTray Dryer, Tray OvenHot Air GeneratorConveyor Belt
Creaming, filling and enrobing

Cream or filling is prepared and applied between wafer sheets, into cake, or over the bar, and the assembled book or bar goes forward. Chocolate is deposited or enrobed at this stage.

High Shear MixerStirrerPiston FillingFilling
Cooling and setting

The filled product passes through a cooling tunnel so the fat sets before it is cut, and may be held in a cold room.

Cooling TunnelCooling ConveyorChillerCold Storage/ Cold Room
Cutting to shape

Set books, slabs and cakes are cut into fingers, squares or bars. Cut size follows the pack format rather than the process.

Batch CutterKnife CutterCube CutterRoller Cutter
Wrapping and secondary packing

Pieces are fed into a wrapper, cartoned, over-wrapped and palletised.

Flow WrapDosing & Flow WrappingCartoningOver WrappingPalletizer & De Palletizer Robot
