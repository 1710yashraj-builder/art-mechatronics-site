Instant Food Processing Plant & Machinery Solutions

Turnkey plant · Machinery · Automation · India · UAE · Thailand

Instant food processing produces noodles, soups, porridge and savoury mixes that need only hot water before eating. Grains and pulses are cleaned and milled, cooked or extruded, dried down to a storage-stable moisture, then blended with seasoning and filled into sachets or cups.

How it works

Complete Instant Food Process Flow

Grain Cleaning & Milling

Grains and pulses are cleaned of dust, chaff, stones and tramp metal before they reach the mill. They are then milled and sifted to the flour or semolina grade the recipe needs.

Air Screen CleanerDe-StonerMagnetic SeparatorGrinding Mill MachinePlan Sifter
Cooking, Extrusion & Shaping

Flour is hydrated and cooked or extruded so the starch gelatinises, which is what lets the finished product rehydrate in hot water. The cooked mass is shaped into noodle strands, flakes or granules.

Cooking Kettle, Boiling TankExtruder MachineSteam GeneratorFormingConveyor Belt
Drying

The shaped noodle cake or granule is dried, either by frying or by hot air, until it is stable for storage without refrigeration. Liquid bases for soups and gravies are dried separately into powder.

FryerFluidized Bed Dryer – FBDContinuous Conveyor, Tunnel DryerSpray DryerMoisture Analyzer
Seasoning & Blend Preparation

Spices, dehydrated vegetables, salt and flavour powders are ground to a common particle size and blended into the seasoning mix. The blend is sieved and checked before it goes to filling.

Micro Pulverizer MachineWeighing & DosingRibbon MixerDouble Cone MixerSifter
Sachet & Cup Filling

Seasoning is filled into small sachets on multi-lane machines, while the noodle cake or mix is filled into pouches or cups. Sachets and the main product are brought together in the same outer pack.

SachetMulti LaneAuger FillerCup FillerPillow Pack
Coding, Inspection & Cartoning

Every pack is coded and passed through weight and foreign-body checks. Packs are then collated into cartons and cases for dispatch.

Batch CodingMetal DetectorCheckweigher With PrinterCollating & CartoningMaster Carton Packing
