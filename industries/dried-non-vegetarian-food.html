Dried Non Vegetarian Food Processing Plant & Machinery Solutions

Turnkey plant · Machinery · Automation · India · UAE · Thailand

Dried non-vegetarian food includes dried fish and prawn, meat floss, egg powder and dried pet-food ingredients. The raw material is cleaned and salted or cooked as the product requires, dried in trays, tunnels or by freeze drying, milled or flaked where needed, checked for moisture and packed in sealed pouches.

How it works

Complete Non-Vegetarian Food Process Flow

Cleaning, Salting & Pre-Cooking

Raw fish, prawn or meat is washed and then salted, brined or cooked according to the product. Salting and cooking draw out water and set the protein before the drying stage begins.

WasherJacketed TankCooking Kettle, Boiling TankBlancher
Drying

Material is spread on trays or a moving belt and dried in heated air until it reaches the target dryness. Air temperature and flow are controlled so the surface does not case-harden and trap moisture inside.

Tray Dryer, Tray OvenContinuous Conveyor, Tunnel DryerRotary Dryer, Drum DryerHot Air Generator
Freeze Drying

Heat-sensitive items such as prawn, egg and pet treats can be frozen and dried under vacuum instead of hot air. Freeze drying removes water from the frozen material under vacuum, so the piece keeps its shape and rehydrates when water is added.

Freeze DryerVacuumChillerTrolley
Milling, Flaking & Sieving

Dried material is broken down into floss, flakes or powder and screened to the size the buyer wants. Oversize is returned to the mill so the batch leaves at one particle size.

Shredder MachineGrinderHammer Mill MachineSifter
Moisture Control & Holding

Moisture is measured on the dried batch, and the holding area is kept dehumidified before packing. Dried protein picks up moisture from the air, so the room condition matters as much as the dryer.

Moisture AnalyzerDehumidifierDay BinScrew Conveyor
Pouch Packing

The dried product is weighed and filled into pouches, sealed with a nitrogen flush and cased. A gas flush and a tight seal are what keep dried protein from going rancid on the shelf.

Multi Head WeigherVertical Form Fill Seal (Vffs)Nitrogen PackingMetal DetectionMaster Carton Packing
