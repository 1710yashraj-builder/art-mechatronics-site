Dried Coconut Processing Plant & Machinery Solutions

Turnkey plant · Machinery · Automation · India · UAE · Thailand

Dried coconut is edible coconut kernel cut into halves, cubes, chips or pieces and dried to a shelf-stable moisture level for confectionery, snacking and retail packs. The kernel is dehusked, deshelled, pared, cut to size, washed, blanched and dried before sorting and packing.

How it works

Complete Dried Coconut Process Flow

Dehusking & Deshelling

Nuts are dehusked and the shell is broken away so that whole kernel halves are released without cracking. Kernel is kept in one piece at this stage because the cut size is set later to suit the pack.

DehuskingShell RemovalApron ConveyorLoading & Unloading Conveyor
Paring & Cutting to Size

The brown testa is pared off and the white kernel is cut into cubes, chips or pieces according to the order. Cutting to a consistent piece size keeps the pieces drying at the same rate later in the line.

Skin RemovalCube CutterDicerKnife Cutter
Washing & Blanching

Cut pieces are washed to remove loose fines and then blanched to reduce surface microbial load. Pieces are cooled and surface water is drained before they enter the dryer.

WasherBlancherCooling ConveyorSteam Generator
Drying

Pieces are dried on trays or in a batch hot-air dryer until they are firm and stable for storage. Moisture is checked on the dried pieces before the batch is released for sorting.

Tray Dryer, Tray OvenBatch Type - Hot Air DryerHot Air GeneratorMoisture Analyzer
Sorting & Grading

Dried pieces are graded by size and sorted by colour so that scorched, discoloured or shell-marked pieces are removed. Fines and broken pieces are screened off and sold as a separate grade.

GraderColor SorterScreenerVibro
Packing

Graded pieces are weighed into pouches or cartons, with nitrogen flushing used where a longer shelf life is required. Packs pass metal inspection and are cartoned for retail or bulk despatch.

Multi Head WeigherPremade PouchNitrogen PackingMetal DetectorCartoning
