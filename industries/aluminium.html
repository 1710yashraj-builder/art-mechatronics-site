Aluminium Processing Plant & Machinery Solutions

Turnkey plant · Machinery · Automation · India · UAE · Thailand

Aluminium processing covers sorting and melting scrap and ingot, casting billets, extruding or rolling sections and sheet, and finishing the surface by anodising or coating. Aluminium melting releases furnace fume, and grinding and polishing produce fine reactive dust that is collected wet rather than dry.

How it works

Complete Aluminium Process Flow

Scrap Sorting & Charge Preparation

Aluminium scrap, extrusion offcuts and ingot are sorted, with steel and other contaminants taken out. Loose and bulky scrap is compacted so it can be charged into the furnace.

Magnetic SeparatorAir ClassifierHydraulic Press / Baling PressVibratory FeederConveyor Belt
Melting & Fume Treatment

The charge is melted in a reverberatory or crucible furnace and treated with flux before casting. Furnace fume and flux gases are hooded, treated and collected before discharge.

Furnace BurnerFume Extraction SystemDry ScrubberBag House Dust Collector MachineHeat Exchanger
Billet & Ingot Casting

Molten aluminium is cast into billets, ingots or slabs and cooled with a water circuit. Cast stock is moved out of the casting bay once it has cooled enough to handle.

Cooling TowerCooling ConveyorChain ConveyorBlower Fan
Extrusion & Rolling

Billets are heated and pressed through a die to form sections, or slabs are rolled down into sheet, coil and foil. Extruded and rolled stock is cooled along the run-out table before it is cut and stacked.

Hot Air GeneratorThermic Fluid HeaterRoller ConveyorCooling TunnelPLC Automation Control Panel
Anodising, Coating & Drying

Profiles and sheet are degreased, etched, anodised or powder coated, then rinsed and dried. Air from the treatment line and the coating oven is cleaned before it leaves the plant.

WasherAir Pollution Control EquipmentAir Handling UnitContinuous Conveyor, Tunnel Dryer
Grinding, Polishing & Fines Collection

Cut ends are dressed and surfaces are buffed or polished where a bright finish is wanted. Aluminium fines are reactive, so this dust is kept separate from other collection circuits and taken out wet.

Wet ScrubberDust Extraction SystemCentralised Dust Collection SystemCyclone SeparatorPolisher
