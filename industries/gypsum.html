Gypsum Processing Plant & Machinery Solutions

Turnkey plant · Machinery · Automation · India · UAE · Thailand

Gypsum processing converts mined gypsum rock, or by-product gypsum, into plaster and plasterboard binder. The rock is crushed, dried and ground, then calcined so that chemically combined water is driven off and the gypsum becomes hemihydrate plaster.

How it works

Complete Gypsum Process Flow

Rock Crushing & Screening

Mined gypsum rock is crushed and screened to a size the mill can take. By-product gypsum from fertiliser or flue gas plants joins the line at this stage instead.

CrusherCrushing MachineScreenerApron ConveyorVibratory Feeder
Drying & Grinding

Free moisture is driven off and the rock is ground to a powder before it goes to the calciner. Ground gypsum is lifted to the calciner feed bin.

Rotary Dryer, Drum DryerRoller Mill MachineHammer Mill MachineBucket Elevator
Calcination

Heating the ground gypsum drives off part of the chemically combined water and converts it into hemihydrate, the plaster used in building products. Heat is supplied indirectly or by hot gas, depending on the plaster grade being made.

Rotary Tube DryerFurnace BurnerHot Air GeneratorThermic Fluid Heater
Cooling & Conditioning

Freshly calcined plaster leaves the calciner hot and reactive, and is cooled and held before use so that its setting behaviour settles down. Cooled plaster goes to storage silos.

Cooling ConveyorScrew ConveyorHeat ExchangerSilo
Fine Milling & Classification

Plaster is milled to the fineness the end product needs and classified, with coarse material returned to the mill. Grades of different fineness are stored separately.

Impact Pulverizer MachineAir ClassifierPlan SifterDay Bin
Blending with Additives

Retarders, accelerators and fillers are weighed in and blended so that the plaster sets in the time the application needs. Batches are mixed dry, with no water added at the plant.

Ribbon MixerPlough Shear MixerWeighing & DosingHopper
Packing & Palletising

Blended plaster is packed into bags, closed and coded, then stacked for despatch. Packing points are vented so that fine plaster is not lost into the packing hall.

Bag PackingStitchingBatch CodingPalletizerPulse Jet Dust Collector Machine
