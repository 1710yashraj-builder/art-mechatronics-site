How it works

Complete Raw Tobacco Process Flow

Raw Leaf Receiving & Handling

Fresh or dried tobacco leaves are received from farms and transferred to the processing line.

Conveyor SystemsMaterial Handling Equipment

➜ Ensures smooth and damage-free material movement.

Pre-Cleaning & Dust Removal

Leaves are cleaned to remove dust, sand, stems, and foreign impurities.

Pre CleanerAir ClassifierCyclone Separator

➜ Improves product quality and protects downstream equipment.

Grading & Sorting

Leaves are sorted based on size, quality, and type for uniform processing.

Grading ConveyorSorting Table

➜ Ensures consistency in further processing.

Moisture Conditioning / Humidification

Leaves are conditioned to achieve optimal moisture levels for cutting and processing.

Conditioning ChamberHumidification System

➜ Prevents breakage and ensures flexibility during cutting.

Cutting / Size Reduction

Conditioned leaves are cut into required sizes depending on application requirements.

Tobacco Leaf Cutting MachineStrip Cutter / Shredder

➜ Ensures uniform size suitable for downstream industries.

Drying / Moisture Control

Cut tobacco is dried or stabilized to maintain consistent moisture levels and prevent spoilage.

Tray Dryer / Continuous Dryer

➜ Ensures long shelf life and stable product quality.

Blending (Optional)

Different grades of tobacco may be blended to achieve specific characteristics.

Ribbon Blender / Paddle Mixer
Dust Collection & Air Handling

Tobacco processing generates fine dust that must be controlled for safety and hygiene. Systems Used:

Dust CollectorCyclone SeparatorCentralized Dust Extraction System

➜ Ensures:

  • Safe working environment
  • Product recovery
  • Compliance with industrial standards
Final Inspection & Quality Control

Processed tobacco is inspected for size consistency, moisture level, and quality.

Inspection ConveyorScreening System
Packaging & Dispatch

Processed tobacco is packed into bulk bags or containers for supply to manufacturers.

Weighing SystemBag Filling Machine

➜ Suitable for bulk industrial supply.
