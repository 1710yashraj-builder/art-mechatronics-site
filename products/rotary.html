How it works

Working Principle of Rotary Packing Machine

Premade Pouch Feeding

Empty premade pouches are loaded into pouch magazine section

Rotary Indexing Operation

Rotary indexing system moves pouches station-to-station automatically

➜ Each rotary station performs a specific packaging operation in synchronized sequence.

Pouch Opening & Product Filling

Machine handles:

  • Powders
  • Granules
  • Pan masala
  • Tobacco
  • Spices
  • Snacks
  • Liquids & paste products accurately and continuously.
Sealing & Packaging Process

Rotary sealing stations perform: Heat sealing Zipper sealing Vacuum sealing (optional) Nitrogen flushing (optional) with premium sealing quality.

Coding & Inspection Integration

Optional systems include: Batch coding Date printing QR code printing Metal detection Check weighing

Finished Pouch Discharge

Finished pouches discharged automatically for: Collection Cartoning Secondary packaging Dispatch operations
