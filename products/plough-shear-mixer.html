Overview

About the Plough Shear Mixer

The machine operates using specially designed plough-shaped mixing elements mounted on a horizontal shaft, which create a mechanical fluidized mixing zone inside the chamber. This fluidization ensures:

  • Extremely fast mixing
  • Uniform product distribution
  • Excellent dispersion
  • Efficient liquid addition
  • Lump-free blending

Plough shear mixers are widely used in industries such as:

• Pharmaceuticals
