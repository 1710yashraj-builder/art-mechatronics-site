Working Principle of Dust Collector Machine

Dust Generation

Dust is generated from processes like:

GrindingMixingConveyingPackagingCrushing
Dust Capture (AT SOURCE)

Dust is captured using:

Suction HoodDucting System

Air Conveying

Dust-laden air is transported through:

Industrial BlowersDucting Network
Filtration

Dust particles are separated using:

Fabric Filter Bags (Bag House)Traps fine particlesCartridge FiltersFor ultra-fine dust
Dust Collection

Collected dust is stored in:

HopperCollection Bin
