Beverage manufacturing splits into two routes: dry powders and leaf — coffee, tea, cocoa, milk powder and premixes — and liquids such as mineral water, soft drinks, fruit juice, coconut water and energy drinks.

How it works

Complete Beverage Process Flow

Raw material intake and cleaning

Green coffee, cocoa bean, tea leaf and dry ingredients are taken in and cleaned of stones, dust and tramp metal. Liquid lines take in water and concentrate instead, and treat it before use.

HopperPre-CleanerDe-StonerMagnetic SeparatorAir Aspirator
Roasting and grinding — the coffee and cocoa branch

Bean is roasted, cooled and then ground or pulverised to the particle the brew method calls for. Tea and green tea skip roasting and go to sorting and blending.

RoasterGrinding Mill MachineMicro Pulverizer MachineCooling Conveyor
Drying to powder — the milk powder and premix branch

Liquid feed is atomised and dried into a powder, agglomerated so it disperses when made up, and the fines are recovered from the air stream.

Spray DryerFluidized Bed Dryer – FBDCyclone SeparatorBag House Dust Collector Machine
Blending and dosing of powders and leaf

Coffee, tea, cocoa, milk powder, sugar and creamer are weighed out and blended to the premix recipe, then sifted before packing.

Double Cone MixerRibbon MixerSifterWeighing & Dosing
Liquid preparation and treatment

Syrup, juice, coconut water or energy drink base is mixed in tank, homogenised where it carries pulp or fat, and heat treated before filling.

Storage TankAgitatorHomogenizerPasteurizer
Liquid filling and closing

Bottles and cans are rinsed, filled and closed, and mineral water runs the same route without a syrup stage.

RinsingBottle Packing (Spray, Pet, Glass)Can PackingCappingSeaming
Dry-side packing and coding

Powder is auger filled into sachets, sticks or jars, leaf is filled into tea bags, and every pack is coded and labelled.

Auger FillerSachetStickTea BagLabelling
