Pre CleanerDestonerAir Classifier

Shelling

Pistachio Shelling Machine

  • Minimal kernel breakage
  • Maximum yield
Kernel & Shell Separation

Air SeparatorGravity Separator

Drying / Moisture Control (IF REQUIRED)
