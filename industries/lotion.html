How it works

Complete Lotion Process Flow

Oil & Water Phase Preparation

The water phase with humectants and stabilisers and the oil phase with emollients, esters and waxes are prepared separately in jacketed vessels. Both phases are heated by steam through the jacket until the waxes are molten and the gums are fully hydrated.

Jacketed TankSteam GeneratorAgitatorWeighing & Dosing
Emulsification

The oil phase is drawn into the water phase in the jacketed vessel under high shear, so that the oil is broken into a fine, stable dispersion. Working under vacuum keeps air out of the emulsion while it is being formed.

Jacketed TankHigh Shear MixerHomogenizerVacuum
Controlled Cooling & Late Additions

Chilled water is circulated through the jacket while the batch is stirred slowly, so the emulsion sets without breaking. Perfume, preservative and heat-sensitive actives are added once the batch has cooled.

ChillerHeat ExchangerStirrerCooling Tower
Bulk Holding & Release

The finished emulsion is transferred to a bulk tank and held while appearance, pH and viscosity are checked against the specification. Batch records are captured by the plant automation system before the bulk is released to filling.

Storage TankMixer Automation SystemCentralised Automation System
Bottle & Pump Pack Filling

Containers are rinsed and filled by piston filler, then fitted with pumps, discs or screw caps. Filled packs are labelled on line.

RinsingPiston FillingCappingBottle Packing (Spray, Pet, Glass)Labelling
Coding, Leak Check & Cartoning

Packs are coded, leak checked and placed into unit cartons. Cartons are collated into shipper cases for despatch.

Batch CodingLeak TesterUnit Carton PackingMaster Carton Packing
