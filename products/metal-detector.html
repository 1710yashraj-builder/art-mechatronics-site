Metal Detector Machine (Industrial Metal Contamination Detection System)

Custom-engineered · Turnkey supply · India · UAE · Thailand

A Metal Detector Machine, also known as an Industrial Metal Detection System or Food Metal Detector, is an advanced industrial inspection equipment designed for detecting and identifying metallic contaminants such as ferrous metals, non-ferrous metals, stainless steel particles, broken machine parts, and tramp metal from food products, powders, granules, liquids, pharmaceuticals, packaging lines, and bulk materials.

How it works

Working Principle of Metal Detector Machine

Product Feeding

Products move through metal detector inspection zone via:

  • Conveyor system
  • Gravity feed system
  • Pipeline system
Electromagnetic Detection Field

Machine generates electromagnetic field inside detection head

➜ Any metallic particle entering field disturbs signal balance

Metal Contamination Detection

Machine detects:

  • Ferrous metals
  • Non-ferrous metals
  • Stainless steel particles
  • Broken metallic contaminants with high sensitivity.
Signal Processing & Analysis

Advanced digital electronics analyze signal disturbance Machine differentiates between product effect and metal contamination

Automatic Rejection System

Upon detection:

  • Reject flap
  • Air blast system
  • Pusher system
  • Diverter mechanism automatically removes contaminated product.
Safe Product Discharge

Clean product continues through processing or packaging line
