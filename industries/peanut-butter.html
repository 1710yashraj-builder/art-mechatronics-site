How it works

Complete Peanut Butter Process Flow

Raw Peanut Handling & Cleaning

Raw groundnuts are received and cleaned to remove dust and impurities.

Material Handling SystemsPre CleanerDestoner

➜ Ensures clean raw material input.

Roasting

Peanuts are roasted to enhance flavor and aroma.

Peanut RoasterContinuous Roasting System

➜ Ensures:

  • Uniform roasting
  • Flavor development
Cooling

Roasted peanuts are cooled before further processing.

Cooling Conveyor

➜ Prevents overcooking and preserves quality.

Peeling / Blanching

Outer skin is removed from roasted peanuts.

Peanut Peeling Machine

➜ Ensures clean kernels.

Sorting & Inspection

Peanuts are sorted to remove defective kernels.

Inspection ConveyorColor Sorter (Optional)

➜ Ensures premium product quality.

Grinding

Peanuts are ground into paste.

Peanut Butter GrinderColloid Mill

➜ Produces:

  • Smooth paste
  • Controlled particle size
Mixing & Formulation

Ingredients such as salt, sugar, stabilizers, and flavors are added.

Mixing Tank

➜ Ensures:

  • Uniform taste
  • Consistent formulation
Homogenization

Peanut butter is homogenized for smooth texture and stability.

Homogenizer

➜ Ensures:

  • Creamy consistency
  • No oil separation
Deaeration (OPTIONAL)

Air is removed to improve shelf life.

Storage & Holding

Finished product is stored before packaging.

Storage Tanks
Packaging (RETAIL & BULK)

Peanut butter is packed into jars or containers.

Jar Filling MachineBottle Filling MachineCapping Machine

➜ Suitable for:

  • Retail packaging
  • Bulk supply
