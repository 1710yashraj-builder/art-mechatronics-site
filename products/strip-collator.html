A Strip Collator Machine, also known as a Strip Collecting Machine, Automatic Strip Collating System, or Strip Counting & Stacking Machine, is an advanced industrial automation solution designed for automatic collection, alignment, counting, stacking, batching, and collating of strips, sachets, pouches, packets, wrappers, and packaging outputs from high-speed packaging machines.

How it works

Working Principle of Strip Collator Machine

Strip Receiving from Packaging Machine

Sachet strips or packet strips discharge continuously from:

  • VFFS machines
  • Multi-lane packaging machines
  • Sachet packing machines
  • High-speed pouch packaging lines
Strip Alignment & Synchronization

Conveyor and sensor systems align strips automatically

➜ Machine synchronizes incoming strip flow for smooth collating and stacking operation.

Automatic Counting & Batching

Machine handles:

  • Sachet strips
  • Pan masala strips
  • Tobacco strips
  • FMCG packets
  • Food pouches
  • Pharma sachets accurately and continuously.
Strip Collating & Stacking

Machine performs: Strip collection Packet stacking Batch formation Strip grouping for efficient manual or automatic cartoning operations.

PLC & Automation Control

System controls: Counting accuracy Conveyor synchronization Strip flow management Batch quantity Production speed

Finished Batch Discharge

Collated strips discharged automatically for: Carton packing Mono carton filling Secondary packaging Dispatch operations
