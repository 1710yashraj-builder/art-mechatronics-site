Coating Pan Mixer Machine for Thailand

A Coating Pan Mixer tumbles product in a rotating pan while seasoning, syrup, oil or a coating solution is sprayed on, with hot air or burner heating to dry or roast in the same pass. Thai snack, cashew and peanut, dried fruit, tablet and confectionery lines coat and glaze this way. ART Mechatronics supports Thai plants from its office in Pattaya.

Supplied to plants in Thailand from ART's Thailand Marketing Office in Pattaya City, with a service team in Chonburi; designed and built in Kanpur, India.

Why Thai processors choose the Coating Pan Mixer Machine

  • Coated nuts and snacks go soft in Thai humidity; heated pan drying sets the coating before packing.
  • Cashew, peanut, tamarind, dried mango and seaweed snacks take sugar, chilli and salt coatings in a rotating pan.
  • Follows ART's Fryer or Roaster and feeds the Multi Head Weigher and Vertical Form Fill Seal (Vffs) on a snack line.
  • Burner, hot air and pan drive parts are supported by ART's service team in Chonburi, close to the Eastern Seaboard food plants.
