How it works

Working Principle of Labelling Machine

  • 1. Product Feeding & Conveyor Handling Products are automatically transferred through synchronized conveyor systems for labeling operation.
  • 2. Label Detection & Positioning Process Machine automatically detects product position and dispenses labels with precise synchronization before application.

➜ Servo synchronization ensures accurate label alignment and smooth product handling.

  • 3. Label Application Operation Machine handles labeling on: ✔ Bottles ✔ Jars ✔ Pouches ✔ Sachets ✔ Cartons ✔ Drums ✔ Jerry cans ✔ Industrial packaged products

accurately and continuously.

  • 4. Labeling & Finishing Process Machine performs: Sticker application Wrap-around labeling Front & back labeling Top labeling Side labeling Barcode labeling QR code application

for attractive and retail-ready packaging operations.

  • 5. Intelligent Automation & Inspection Integration Optional systems include: Vision inspection systems Barcode verification Missing label detection Reject systems Industrial printer integration
  • 6. Finished Product Discharge Labeled products discharged automatically for: Cartoning Case packing Warehouse storage Retail distribution Export shipment handling
