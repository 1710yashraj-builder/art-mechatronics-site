How it works

Working Principle of L Sealer Machine

Product Feeding

Products are manually or automatically fed onto conveyor system

Film Wrapping Process

Shrink film wraps around product automatically

➜ Machine synchronizes product movement with L-sealing system for continuous wrapping operation.

L-Shaped Sealing Operation

Machine performs: ✔ Side sealing ✔ End sealing ✔ L-shape sealing ✔ Sleeve sealing for secure and professional packaging quality.

Shrink Tunnel Integration

Wrapped products may pass through shrink tunnel for: Tight film shrinkage Attractive retail finish Product protection

PLC & Automation Control

System controls: Product synchronization Film movement Sealing timing Conveyor speed Packaging cycle

Finished Pack Discharge

Wrapped products discharged automatically for: Cartoning Secondary packaging Retail display Dispatch operations
