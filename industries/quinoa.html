Quinoa Processing Plant & Machinery Solutions

Turnkey plant · Machinery · Automation · India · UAE · Thailand

Quinoa processing removes the bitter saponin coating from the seed and prepares it for cooking or milling. Harvested quinoa is cleaned and graded, then scarified or washed to strip the saponin layer, dried back down, and sorted by colour before packing.

How it works

Complete Quinoa Process Flow

Seed Intake & Cleaning

Field-run quinoa is screened to remove stalk, chaff, soil and stones before any saponin work begins. Ferrous contamination is taken out at the same point.

Bag Dump StationAir Screen CleanerDe-StonerMagnetic Separator
Grading & Density Separation

Seed is graded to size and separated by density so light, immature seed and weed seed leave the stream. Even-sized seed scours more consistently in the next stage.

GraderGravity SeparatorAir AspiratorScreener
Saponin Removal by Scouring & Washing

The saponin-bearing outer layer is abraded off dry, and a water wash follows where a fully de-bittered grade is wanted. This is the stage that distinguishes a quinoa line from an ordinary grain cleaning plant.

ScourerPolisherWasherRinsing
Drying & Cooling

Washed seed is dried back to a storable condition and cooled before it goes to sorting. Moisture is checked on the line so the seed is neither over-dried nor left damp.

Fluidized Bed Dryer – FBDContinuous Conveyor, Tunnel DryerCooling ConveyorMoisture Analyzer
Optical Sorting & Inspection

Dried seed is colour sorted to take out dark, stained and foreign seed, with rejects diverted automatically. Metal detection runs ahead of packing.

Color SorterVision InspectionAuto Reject SystemMetal Detector
Retail & Bulk Packing

Sorted quinoa is weighed and filled into resealable retail packs or bulk bags, coded and cartoned.

Multi Head WeigherZip Lock/ Zipper PouchCheckweigher With PrinterMetal DetectorMaster Carton Packing
