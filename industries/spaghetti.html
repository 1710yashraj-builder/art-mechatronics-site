Spaghetti Processing Plant & Machinery Solutions

Turnkey plant · Machinery · Automation · India · UAE · Thailand

Spaghetti processing turns durum semolina into long, round solid strands. Dough is extruded through a fine die, and the strands are draped over drying sticks and carried through a long-goods dryer, then trimmed to length, weighed and packed.

How it works

Complete Spaghetti Process Flow

Semolina Handling

Semolina is drawn from silos, elevated, sifted and passed over magnets before dosing.

SiloBucket ElevatorPlan SifterMagnetic Separator
Mixing & Hydration

Semolina and water are dosed and mixed to a uniform dough, since any dry pocket shows up as a weak point in a long strand.

Weighing & DosingRibbon MixerMixer Automation System
Extrusion & Spreading

Dough is extruded through a fine round die and the continuous strands are draped over sticks that carry them through the plant. From this point the product is handled hanging rather than lying on a belt.

Extruder MachineChain ConveyorTrolley
Long-Goods Drying

The hanging strands pass through pre-drying, drying and stabilising zones with controlled air. Drying too fast leaves the strands brittle and they snap during handling.

Continuous Conveyor, Tunnel DryerDehumidifierHot Air GeneratorMoisture Analyzer
Stripping & Cutting to Length

Dried spaghetti is stripped from the sticks, trimmed to a uniform length and the trimmings are separated out for reuse.

Blade CutterCooling ConveyorScreener
Weighing & Packing

Measured bundles are wrapped, coded, checked and packed into cartons.

Flow WrapCheckweigher With PrinterMetal DetectorMaster Carton Packing
