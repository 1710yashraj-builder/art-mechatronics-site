Rusk Manufacturing Plant & Machinery Solutions (Toast Biscuit & Double-Baked Production Lines)

Turnkey plant · Machinery · Automation · India · UAE · Thailand

Rusk, also known as toast biscuit, is a popular bakery product known for its long shelf life, crisp texture, and wide consumer demand. It is produced through a double baking process, where bread is first baked, sliced, and then baked again to achieve the desired crunch and durability.

How it works

Complete Rusk Process Flow

Raw Material Handling

Ingredients such as flour, sugar, yeast, milk powder, and additives are received and fed into the system.

Material Handling SystemsIngredient Dosing System

➜ Ensures accurate and consistent formulation.

Mixing & Dough Preparation

Ingredients are mixed to form dough.

Dough Mixer / Spiral Mixer

➜ Ensures:

  • Uniform mixing
  • Proper gluten development
Fermentation / Proofing

Dough is fermented to develop structure and texture.

Fermentation ChamberIntermediate Proofer

➜ Ensures soft and airy structure.

Moulding & Panning

Dough is shaped and placed into trays for baking.

MoulderPanning System
First Baking (BREAD FORMATION)

Dough is baked into bread loaves.

Industrial Oven (Tunnel / Deck Oven)

➜ Produces base bread for rusk.

Cooling

Baked bread is cooled before slicing.

Cooling Conveyor
Slicing

Bread is sliced into uniform pieces.

Bread Slicer

➜ Ensures uniform thickness for even baking.

Second Baking / Toasting

Sliced bread is baked again to form rusk.

Rusk Oven / Continuous Baking Oven

➜ Ensures:

  • Crisp texture
  • Low moisture
  • Long shelf life
Cooling

Rusk is cooled before packaging.

Cooling Conveyor
Packaging (HIGH-SPEED FMCG)

Finished rusk is packed into pouches.

Flow Wrap MachineForm Fill Seal (FFS) Machine

➜ Ensures:

  • Hygienic packaging
  • Extended shelf life
