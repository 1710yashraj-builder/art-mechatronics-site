Lasagna Processing Plant & Machinery Solutions

Turnkey plant · Machinery · Automation · India · UAE · Thailand

Lasagna processing turns wheat dough into flat rectangular sheets rather than a shaped short pasta. The dough is mixed, extruded as a continuous sheet, rolled to an even thickness, cut into rectangles, dried flat on trays so the sheets stay unwarped, then interleaved and packed.

How it works

Complete Lasagna Process Flow

Dough Mixing

Sifted semolina, water and any egg or additional ingredients are dosed and worked into a smooth, extensible dough. A sheet dough is kept softer than a short-cut pasta dough so it rolls without tearing.

SifterWeighing & DosingSigma Mixer
Sheet Extrusion & Rolling

The dough leaves the die as a continuous ribbon and is reduced to an even thickness across the full width of the sheet.

Extruder MachineFormingSlat Conveyor
Cutting to Rectangles

The sheet is trimmed at the edges and cut into rectangular sheets of the required size.

Roller CutterKnife CutterConveyor Belt
Flat Drying

Sheets are laid flat on trays and dried with controlled air so they do not curl, warp or crack. Flat drying is the step that separates a lasagna line from an extruded short-cut line.

Tray Dryer, Tray OvenHot Air GeneratorDehumidifierMoisture Analyzer
Stacking & Carton Packing

Dried sheets are counted, stacked and placed into cartons, which protect the brittle sheets better than a flexible pouch.

Count & StackCartoningMono Carton PackingMetal Detector
