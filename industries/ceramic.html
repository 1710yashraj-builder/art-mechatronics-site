Ceramic Processing Plant & Machinery Solutions

Turnkey plant · Machinery · Automation · India · UAE · Thailand

Ceramic processing prepares a body of clay, feldspar and quartz for sanitaryware, tableware and ceramic products. The batch is weighed, wet-ground into slip, screened and de-ironed, spray-dried into free-flowing granulate, pressed or cast to shape, glazed, fired in a kiln, then inspected and packed.

How it works

Complete Ceramic Process Flow

Body Batch Preparation

Clay, feldspar and quartz are received, lump material is broken down, and each raw material is weighed to the body recipe. Materials are held in separate bins so that recipes can be changed between batches.

CrusherHopperWeighing & DosingBucket Elevator
Wet Grinding into Slip

The weighed batch is ground with water in a ball mill until the body becomes a pourable slip. The slip is held in stirred tanks so that the solids do not settle out.

Ball Mill MachineWet GrinderAgitatorStorage Tank
Screening & De-Ironing

Slip is screened to take out coarse residue and passed over magnets, because iron specks show as dark spots after firing. Rejected residue is taken out of the circuit.

SieverSifterMagnetic SeparatorStirrer
Spray Drying to Granulate

The slip is sprayed into a hot air stream, which drives off the water and leaves free-flowing granules that press well. Fines carried out with the air are recovered in a cyclone.

Spray DryerHot Air GeneratorCyclone SeparatorSilo
Forming & Pressing

Granulate is pressed in dies, or slip is cast in moulds for sanitaryware, to give the green shape. Green pieces are dried before any glaze is applied.

Hydraulic Press / Baling PressFormingRoller ConveyorPLC Automation Control Panel
Glazing & Firing

Glaze is applied to the dried body and the piece is fired, which vitrifies the body and fuses the glaze to it. Heat recovered from the kiln is taken back to the dryers.

Furnace BurnerContinuous Conveyor, Tunnel DryerHeat Recovery Unit - HRUConveyor Belt
Inspection & Packing

Fired ware is inspected for cracks, warping and glaze faults, then sorted into grades. Accepted pieces are packed into cartons and palletised.

Vision InspectionSorting RobotMaster Carton PackingPalletizerStretch Wrapping
