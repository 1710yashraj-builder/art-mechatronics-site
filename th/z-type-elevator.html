Z Type Elevator Machine for Thailand

The Z Type Elevator lifts product vertically in buckets that follow a Z-shaped path, so fragile pieces are carried rather than dragged. Thai snack, rice cracker, dried fruit, cashew and pet food lines use it to feed weighers and packing machines mounted above floor level. ART Mechatronics' Pattaya office is the contact point for Thai plants, backed by service staff in Chonburi.

Supplied to plants in Thailand from ART's Thailand Marketing Office in Pattaya City, with a service team in Chonburi; designed and built in Kanpur, India.

Why Thai processors choose the Z Type Elevator Machine

  • Fried snacks and rice crackers soften in humid air; a short lift from Cooling Conveyor to Multi Head Weigher limits exposure.
  • Buckets carry whole cashews, dried mango pieces and coated peanuts without pushing them along a surface.
  • Sits between ART's Cooling Conveyor or Vibratory Feeder and the Vertical Form Fill Seal (Vffs) packing head.
  • ART has a conveying and elevation assembly installed at a Thai customer plant, and its service team sits in Chonburi.
