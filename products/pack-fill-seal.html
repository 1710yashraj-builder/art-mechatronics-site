How it works

Working Principle of Pack Fill Seal Packing Machine

Premade Pouch Feeding

Empty premade pouches are loaded into pouch magazine or feeding section

Pouch Pickup & Opening

Machine automatically picks and opens pouches using vacuum or pneumatic systems

➜ Pouches move through synchronized stations for filling and sealing operations.

Product Filling Operation

Machine handles:

  • Powders
  • Granules
  • Pan masala
  • Tobacco
  • Spices
  • Snacks
  • Liquids & paste products accurately and continuously.
Sealing Process

Machine performs: Heat sealing Zipper sealing Vacuum sealing (optional) Nitrogen flushing (optional) to ensure leak-proof and hygienic packaging.

Coding & Inspection Integration

Optional systems include: Batch coding Date printing QR code printing Metal detection Check weighing

Finished Pouch Discharge

Finished pouches discharged automatically for: Collection Cartoning Secondary packaging Dispatch operations
