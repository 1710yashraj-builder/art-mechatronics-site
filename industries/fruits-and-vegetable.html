Fruits & Vegetable Processing Plant & Machinery Solutions

Turnkey plant · Machinery · Automation · India · UAE · Thailand

Fruit and vegetable processing takes fresh produce and turns it into dried pieces, flakes, pulp and powders that keep without refrigeration. Produce is washed, graded, peeled and cut, dried, then milled and sieved to the required particle size before packing.

How it works

Complete Fruits & Vegetable Process Flow

Intake, washing and grading

Produce is tipped into a hopper, washed and rinsed clear of soil and field trash, then graded and colour sorted so damaged and unripe pieces are taken out.

HopperWasherRinsingGraderColor Sorter
Peeling, coring and cutting

Skin is removed where the product needs it, seeds and stems are taken out, and the produce is sliced or diced to a size that will dry evenly.

PeelerSeed RemovalStem RemovalSlicerDicer
Blanching and pre-treatment

Cut pieces are blanched to stop enzyme activity and hold colour, and may be dipped or sulphited before drying, depending on the fruit or vegetable.

BlancherCooking Kettle, Boiling TankSteam GeneratorConveyor Belt
Drying

Moisture is driven off in a tunnel, tray or freeze dryer until the pieces are stable at ambient storage. Freeze drying is used where the piece shape and colour have to be kept.

Continuous Conveyor, Tunnel DryerTray Dryer, Tray OvenFreeze DryerHot Air Generator
Milling and sieving to flake or powder

Dried pieces are milled and then sieved and classified so the flake or powder is held to one particle range.

Impact Pulverizer MachineHammer Mill MachineVibroAir Classifier
Pulp and paste branch

Where the output is pulp or puree rather than powder, the washed produce is crushed, refined and held in tank instead of dried.

CrusherHomogenizerJacketed TankStorage Tank
Weighing and packing

Flake, powder or pulp is filled into pouches or bulk packs, gas flushed where oxygen would spoil it, checked and cased.

Auger FillerPremade PouchNitrogen PackingMetal DetectionMaster Carton Packing
