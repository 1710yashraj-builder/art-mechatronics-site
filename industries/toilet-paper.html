Toilet Paper Converting Plant & Machinery Solutions

How it works

Complete Toilet Paper Process Flow

Parent Reel Intake & Core Feeding

Parent reels and wound paper cores are brought to the rewinder and loaded onto the unwind and core feed. Reel movement between the store and the line runs on floor and vertical conveying.

Vertical ConveyorGoods LiftMaterial Handling RobotConveyor Belt
Rewinding, Embossing & Perforating

Plies are embossed, perforated and wound onto the core to build a log. Fibre and dust released at the winding head are taken away at source so they do not build up on the machine.

RewinderVacuum ConveyorPulse Jet Dust Collector MachineBlower Fan
Log Sawing & Roll Transfer

The log is sawn into individual rolls and the rolls are transferred to the wrapping line. Saw dust is captured at the blade and separated from the conveying air.

Blade CutterDe-Dusting SystemSpiral ConveyorCyclone Separator
Pack Counting & Wrapping

Rolls are counted into pack sizes and wrapped in printed film, then sealed. Batch and date details are printed onto the pack as it leaves the sealer.

Count & StackFlow WrapWeb SealerPrinter (Cij, Inkjet, Co2 Laser, Hand)
Bagging, Palletising & Dispatch

Consumer packs are grouped into larger bags or cases for the trade. Cases are palletised, wrapped and moved to dispatch.

Bag PackingMaster Case PackingPalletizerStretch Wrapping
Off-Spec Roll Recovery & Extraction Control

Rejected rolls, tail ends and collected fibre are shredded and baled for return to the mill. The whole extraction and conveying network is sequenced from a central panel.

Shredder MachineHydraulic Press / Baling PressCentralised Dust Collection SystemPLC Automation Control Panel
