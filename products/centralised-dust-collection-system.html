Overview

About the Centralised Dust Collection System

Unlike standalone dust collectors, a centralized system connects various dust-generating machines and processes through a network of ducting, suction points, and high-capacity filtration units, making it ideal for large-scale manufacturing facilities.

These systems are widely used in industries such as food processing, pharmaceuticals, chemicals, cement, steel, packaging, plastics, and powder handling plants, where maintaining clean air, hygiene, and regulatory compliance is critical.

How it works

Working Principle of Centralized Dust Collection System

Dust Generation Across Multiple Points

Dust is generated from:

GrindingMixingConveyingPackagingCrushingMaterial transfer
Multi-Point Dust Capture (CORE FEATURE)

Dust is captured at multiple locations using:

Suction HoodsPickup PointsFlexible Connections

➜ Ensures dust is captured at source

Ducting Network (SYSTEM BACKBONE)

All dust-laden air flows through:

Main Duct LineBranch Duct Connections

➜ Connects entire plant to central unit

Air Movement

Industrial fans / blowers create suction and transport air.

Central Filtration Unit (CORE PROCESS)

Dust is filtered using:

Bag House Dust CollectorCartridge Filter System

➜ High-efficiency dust separation

Dust Collection & Disposal

Dust is discharged through:

HopperRotary Airlock ValveScrew Conveyor (optional)
Clean Air Discharge

Clean air is released safely or recirculated.
