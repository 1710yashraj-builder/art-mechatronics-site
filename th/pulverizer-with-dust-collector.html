Pulverizer with Dust Collector for Thailand

The Pulverizer With Dust Collector grinds dry material to powder and draws the airborne fines into a cyclone and dust collector unit supplied with it. In Thai lines, mills of this type grind chilli, white pepper, turmeric, galangal and lemongrass, along with icing sugar, rice flour, dried fish and dried shrimp. For Thai plants

Why Thai processors choose the Pulverizer with Dust Collector

  • In humid Thai plants, fine chilli and pepper dust clings to walls and floors; capturing it at the mill limits that spread.
  • Chilli and pepper dust irritate eyes and airways; drawing it off at the grinding point reduces operator exposure in the mill room.
  • Feeds ground powder to the Vibro or Sifter for grading and on to a Ribbon Mixer for seasoning blends.
  • An ART in-plant dust collector with ducting is installed at a Thai customer site; filter and fan servicing comes from Chonburi.
