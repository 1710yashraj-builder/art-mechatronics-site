Sugar Processing Plant & Machinery Solutions

Turnkey plant · Machinery · Automation · India · UAE · Thailand

Sugar processing takes crystal sugar and works it into the grades food manufacturers buy: granulated, caster, icing and cube. The crystal is cleaned of tramp metal, conditioned, milled to the fineness required, sieved into grades, blended or pressed into cubes and packed, with dust extraction on every transfer point.

How it works

Complete Sugar Process Flow

Intake & Cleaning

Crystal sugar is tipped, screened for lumps and passed over magnets to take out tramp metal picked up in handling and transport. Transfer is kept enclosed so the sugar stays dry and the dust stays inside the plant.

Bag Dump StationMagnetic SeparatorVibroBucket ElevatorScrew Conveyor
Conditioning & Drying

Sugar that has picked up moisture is dried and cooled so that it grinds cleanly instead of caking inside the mill. Dry air is supplied to the handling area to keep humidity away from the open product.

Fluidized Bed Dryer – FBDAir Duct HeaterDehumidifierCooling Conveyor
Grinding to Caster & Icing Grades

Granulated sugar is milled down to caster and icing fineness, with the mill chosen to suit the grade being made. Sugar dust is combustible, so grinding is run with dust collection connected directly to the mill.

Impact Pulverizer MachinePulverizer With Dust CollectorHammer Mill MachineAir Classifier Mill Machine
Sieving & Grading

Milled sugar is separated into grades and the oversize is returned for regrinding. The particle spread set here is what decides how the sugar dissolves and how it behaves in a dry blend.

Plan SifterScreenerAir ClassifierSiever
Blending & Cube Forming

Icing sugar is blended with an anti caking agent, while cube lines press dampened sugar and cut it to shape. Formed cubes are dried and cooled before they are collated for packing.

Double Cone MixerPlow MixerCube CutterTray Dryer, Tray Oven
Dust Control

Mills, transfer points and packing machines are put under suction so that airborne sugar dust is captured where it is created. Collected fines are discharged through a controlled point instead of settling across the plant.

Pulse Jet Dust Collector MachineCentralised Dust Collection SystemCyclone Dust Collector MachineDe-Dusting System
Packing

Finished sugar is filled into sachets, sticks, pouches or sacks, then sealed and coded. Retail packs are cartoned for despatch, while bulk grades go straight into sacks.
