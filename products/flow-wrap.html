How it works

Working Principle of Flow Wrap Packing Machine

Product Feeding

Products are manually or automatically fed onto infeed conveyor system

Film Feeding & Product Wrapping (CORE PROCESS)

Packaging film moves horizontally around product

➜ Machine wraps product continuously into tubular pillow pack structure.

Longitudinal Sealing Process

Rotary sealing system creates: Center seal Back seal Continuous longitudinal sealing for strong and hygienic packaging.

End Sealing & Cutting

Rotary jaws perform: End sealing Pouch separation Product cutting synchronization at high production speeds.

PLC & Servo Automation Control

System controls: Product synchronization Film tracking Wrapping speed Seal timing Product spacing

Finished Pack Discharge

Wrapped products discharged automatically for: Collection Cartoning Secondary packaging Dispatch operations
