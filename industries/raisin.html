Raisin Processing Plant & Machinery Solutions

Turnkey plant · Machinery · Automation · India · UAE · Thailand

Raisin processing turns grapes into dried fruit ready for retail and bakery use. Grapes are dipped so the waxy skin dries faster, dried down, then de-stemmed and de-capped, washed, de-stoned and separated from stalk and cap fragments by air, graded by size, lightly oiled so the fruit does not cake, and packed.

How it works

Complete Raisin Process Flow

Dipping & Drying

Bunches are dipped in a hot alkaline emulsion that breaks the waxy bloom on the grape skin, and the fruit is then dried. Dipped grapes dry faster and more evenly than untreated fruit of the same size.

Cooking Kettle, Boiling TankJacketed TankContinuous Conveyor, Tunnel DryerBatch Type - Hot Air DryerHot Air Generator
De-Stemming & Cap Removal

Dried fruit is separated from the stalks and the cap stems are pulled off each berry. Stalk and cap material is taken out here rather than being left to show up as a defect at the packing end.

Stem RemovalVibratory ConveyorScreenerShaker
Washing & Cleaning

Raisins are washed to remove field dust, sand and sugar bloom, and stones and tramp metal are separated out. The washed fruit is surface dried again before it moves on to grading.

WasherRinsingDe-StonerMagnetic SeparatorFluidized Bed Dryer – FBD
Air Separation & Grading

Light stalk, cap and skin fragments are drawn off by air and the fruit is graded into size bands. Berries that have stuck together during drying are broken apart as they pass through.

Air AspiratorWinnowerAir ClassifierGraderSifter
Colour Sorting

Colour sorting takes out unripe green berries, sugared and darkened fruit, stones and foreign matter. Camera inspection follows to catch stalk and cap that came through the air stage.

Color SorterVision InspectionSorting RobotVibratory Feeder
Oiling & Packing

A light food-grade oil is applied so the fruit stays loose in the pack instead of caking into a block. The raisins are then weighed and filled into pouches, cartons or bulk cases and coded.

Coating Pan MixerMulti Head WeigherVertical Form Fill Seal (Vffs)Checkweigher With PrinterMaster Carton Packing
