Frozen Food Processing Plant & Machinery Solutions

Turnkey plant · Machinery · Automation · India · UAE · Thailand

Frozen food processing takes fresh vegetables, potatoes, fruit and prepared snacks and freezes them so they can be stored and moved on the cold chain until they are cooked. Produce is washed, peeled, cut, blanched, cooled and frozen, then packed and held in cold storage.

How it works

Complete Frozen Food Process Flow

Washing, Sorting & Grading

Incoming produce is washed to remove field soil, then sorted to take out stones, damaged pieces and off-colour material. Grading separates the crop by size so that later cutting and freezing behave predictably.

WasherRinsingDe-StonerColor SorterGrader
Peeling & Cutting

Skins are removed where the product calls for it, and the produce is cut to the finished shape — dice, slices, strips or florets. Cut size is what decides how the pieces freeze and how they cook later.

PeelerSkin RemovalChipperDicerSlicer
Blanching & Cooling

Cut produce is blanched in hot water or steam to stop enzyme activity that would otherwise dull colour and flavour in storage. It is then cooled quickly so the pieces do not carry on cooking.

BlancherSteam GeneratorCooking Kettle, Boiling TankCooling TunnelCooling Conveyor
Freezing

Pieces are frozen individually on a fluidised or belt freezer so they remain free-flowing, while blocks and packed formats go to a blast freezer. Frozen product is moved into cold rooms held at frozen storage conditions.

IQF FreezerBlast FreezerChillerCold Storage/ Cold RoomSpiral Conveyor
Packing & Cold Dispatch

Frozen product is weighed, filled into pouches, bags or trays and sealed. Packs are coded, checked and palletised for cold-chain dispatch.

Multi Head WeigherVertical Form Fill Seal (Vffs)Metal DetectionCheckweigher With PrinterPalletizer
